## Stranger's question (G16)
"Which 4 numbers on my hand stop sub-4 pace on my watch turning into a 4:02?" Stages 1 and 2 answer numbers 1 to 3 and 30K TO THE FINISH gives number 4 and the reason, but a runner can guess both halves from line one: the watch reads long, and the numbers are the 10K split times.

## FAILs, in the order to fix them
1. G17: take the cause out of line one. Make a claim in clock time that runners would argue with and can't explain from the line (for example, sub-4 pace for all 42K still finishes in 4:02, with no mention of the watch), and keep "your watch reads a bit long" for 30K TO THE FINISH.
2. G16: rebuild the gap around that one question, so line one leaves "how?" and only the last stage answers it. Rewrite the rehook so it reopens something numbers 1 to 3 don't already give away (for example, why the pace on your watch can't be trusted, rather than which sign number 4 sits at). Then re-check that the hold line ("we call it the hand plan") doesn't hint at the answer.
