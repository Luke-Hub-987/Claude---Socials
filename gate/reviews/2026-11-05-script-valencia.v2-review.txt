## Stranger's question (G16)
"Which 3K, and how does a stretch running away from the finish decide my whole race?" The 31K TO THE FINISH stage answers "which" (31K to the park at 34K). Most runners will already have guessed that, because it's the wall zone. No stage answers "how does it decide my race": the stage says it "feels like the wrong way" and moves on, and the claim is still marked unconfirmed in the facts table.

## FAILs, in the order to fix them
1. **G16 / G17 (the gap, line one):** rebuild line one around a Valencia fact that's outside the wall zone, one runners would argue with, and that a stage pays off with a reason. Or confirm why 31K to 34K decides the race and say it in the stage. A repeat of ledger row 17.
2. **G13 (layers):** title and line one must not share "3K", "Valencia ... decide your race". Change one of them.
3. **G8 / G20 (known advice, stage 1 not a scene):** make the stages Valencia-only (the gel hand-outs against their clock, the 33cl bottles, the 31K to 34K leg). The band, gel clock and corners shrink to one line. A repeat of ledger row 26.
4. **G23 (doable on this course):** the gel-with-water instruction doesn't line up with Valencia's stations for the sub-4 clock. Give the bottle method.
5. **G22 (grams first):** "a 30 gram gel at 20 minutes, then one every 30".
6. **G18 (pain and dream):** the dream is 200 plans' "watch at the end" shape again, and the pain has no cost. Build both new. A repeat of ledger row 16.
7. **G9 / G24 (premise, literal words):** cut "stop looking up the road for the end", say the direction literally, and give the 31K to 34K leg a measurable cue.
8. **G29 (templates):** a new rehook and send line, not "Which leaves the..." and "Send this to whoever you're... with". A repeat of ledger row 16.

None of these FAILs needs a decision from the owner to fix. The writing can fix all of them. The one claim that does wait on his OK ("they decide your whole race") can be avoided by fix 1.
