## Stranger's question (G16)
"Which 4 numbers on my hand?" Stages 1 and 2 answer it a piece at a time (57:30, 1:54, 2:50), and 30K TO THE FINISH closes it ("Number 4 is 3:46 or less at the 40K sign").

## FAILs, in the order to fix them
1. G17: rebuild line one around the claim they'd argue with (sub-4 pace on the watch gets you a 4:02), not the promise of 4 numbers. Then grade G16 again: the stranger's question becomes "so what do I run?", and the hand plan has to stay unguessable until the stages.
2. G11: put "marathon" in the title, e.g. "SAID JUST TO FINISH. / WANT A SUB-4 MARATHON?"
3. G13: once the goal is in the title, line one must not say it again ("want a marathon under 4 hours"). Open on the viewer ("If that's secretly you, ...") and go straight to what's new.
4. G18: rebuild the pain so the cost is the secret, not another near miss on the 4-hour line. Fix the stack table's "last 3 used" column to the real last 3 (1 Nov where it goes wrong, 3 Nov long run v4, 4 Nov 200 plans).
