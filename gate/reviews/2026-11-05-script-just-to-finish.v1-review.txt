## Stranger's question (G16)
"How does sub-4 pace on my watch end up as a 4:02?" It's answered in 30K TO THE FINISH ("Your watch reads a bit long, a little more on every bend"). Anyone who has finished a race with GPS can guess that answer ("the watch said 42.6"). The save line ("the 4 numbers that tell you the truth") gives it away early, and so does stage 1's "look at the time on your watch" at the sign.

Re-work the bands and sign times (suggested: 5:30 to 5:35 after 10K; signs about 58:30, 1:54:30, 2:50:30, 3:46:30; finish about 3:58:40), mark it Luke to OK, and fix the FACTS block.
6. **G4 and G11 (title).** Plain goal words a non-runner reads in one go, plus a second element of the five (named race or deadline, or our named method).
