## Stranger's question (G16)
"So how am I meant to get my gels down?" Stage 2 answers it (5.5K TO 34K: take a station bottle and carry it until the gel's due), at about 0:50. That's not the declared closer, 34K: THE FREE GEL. A stranger guesses it ("carry water"), and the pain's opening, "Take one without water", points straight at it.

## FAILs, in the order to fix them
1. G8 / G16 / G20: the aha and its structure. Find one course-specific answer a club coach wouldn't give and runners can't guess, and hold it until the last stage. Cut the free-gel stage to one line inside stage 2 (it's "nothing new on race day", and we've already said it). Write a rehook that names a real exception, not one the method already covers.
2. G22 / G23 (needs Luke): settle one bottle method. Either the FACTS drink-your-gel / "100m before a station" method we already taught, or Luke OKs carrying a bottle from the pen and between stations and the gel reel is corrected to match. Fix the watch-versus-course pace: correct the bands for a watch reading 1% long, or give the 10K sign time (about 58:00) as the check, so "2:00:20" and "3:47" hold.
3. G17: rebuild line one on one marker in K or minutes, with a "why" they can't supply themselves.
4. G18: rebuild the pain in a shape the last 3 didn't use: no "[X] and you're [suffering] with A and B", no cause up front, no sloshing from too little water.
5. G11: put the event in everyday words, e.g. UNDER 4 HOURS AT THE / VALENCIA MARATHON.
6. G24: make the rehook literally true and consistent with the stage that follows.
7. G29: drop the two back-to-back "not cups" / "not at them" contrasts.
