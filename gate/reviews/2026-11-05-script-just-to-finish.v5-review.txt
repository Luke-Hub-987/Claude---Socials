## Stranger's question (G16)

## FAILs, in the order to fix them
1. G16 and G17 (one fix): end line one on the question they can't answer. Keep the 4:02 claim and add the fix in the same sentence, e.g. "If that's secretly you, sub-4 pace on your watch can still get you a 4:02, and 4 numbers on your hand stop it." Then cut "4 numbers on your hand fix it" from the hold line so it only names the hand plan and asks for the save. Keep the hook at 80 to 95 words and recheck G1/G3 (the question inside 10 words).
