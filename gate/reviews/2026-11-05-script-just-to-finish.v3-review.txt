## Stranger's question (G16)
"How can I run every K at sub-4 pace and still finish in 4:02?" It's answered in 30K TO THE FINISH ("Your watch reads a bit long, so sub-4 pace on it brings you in at 4:02"). It's guessable: "the watch measures long" is the obvious answer, and we posted it on 14 Oct.

## FAILs, in the order to fix them
1. **G16 and G17 (line one):** move the gap off the GPS fact, which viewers can guess and we've already posted. Open it on something only the stages can pay, e.g. the 4 numbers ("4 numbers on the back of your hand tell you you're under 4 hours before 40K"). Make the watch one line inside the last stage. Rewrite the rehook so it says literally what number 4 does.
2. **G11 (title):** break the "THE HAND PLAN: A" line (it reads as "Plan A"), and add a real second element a stranger counts: the viewer's own constraint, e.g. SAID JUST TO FINISH, / WANT UNDER 4 HOURS?, or our proof. A renamed pace band isn't "our own method".
3. **G24 (the gel cue):** put "then one every 30 on the clock" in stage 1, where the 50-minute gel falls, and make stage 2 a continuation ("Keep a gel every 30 minutes").
