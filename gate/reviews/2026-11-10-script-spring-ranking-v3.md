## Stranger's question (G16)
"How can a flat course cost me before I've even started running?" They can't guess the pen rule cold, but London's "crowds you till the starts merge" and the turn's "flattest course in Europe" hand over the mechanism and the race before S TIER.

## FAILs, in the order to fix them
1. **G23 [BLOCK], needs Luke.** The question for Luke: "Can a runner already entered for Seville 2027 still add a marathon or half time to move pens? If so, where do they do it (the entrant area or an email to the organisers) and by what date? If not, what's the one thing a runner in the over-4 pen should do on the morning?" His answer sets S TIER's last line and its number (G20).
2. **G21 + G8 [BLOCK].** Rewrite the caption's Seville row: the pen rule, the half times by pen (1:35 / 1:40 / 1:45), and Luke's answer from item 1. Cut "nothing slows you early" and the 1 Nov "5 seconds a K slower" method. One edit clears both.
3. **G16 [BLOCK].** Cut "London crowds you till the starts merge. Then" from B TIER. Keep "flattest course in Europe" out of everything before S TIER.
4. **G31 [BLOCK].** Write a new turn: a moment, something they didn't believe, not about pace on the watch (that's 5 and 6 Nov's line one), and not giving away the pen.
5. **G18 [RISK].** Risk: the hook can sound like the 200 plans one and gives them no picture to hold, so the first 10 seconds may lose people. Fix: a new pain shape with the feeling or the cruel timing, and a dream they'll see (a clock at a sign on their own course).
6. **G17 [RISK].** Risk: an abstract line one with nothing to picture is the shape that skipped 43% to 52% before (row 6). Fix: name an object in line one (e.g. the day you entered) without pointing at the pen. Check the result against G16.
7. **G24 [RISK].** Risk: viewers won't know what "let the rise go" or "your one change" means, so they get no step to take on the climb. Fix: literal, measured cues, and the concept built on one clear noun.
8. **G20 [RISK].** Risk: two tiers end with no number, so there's less to save. Fix: end every tier on a sign, a K or a time.
9. **G11 [RISK].** Risk: a general title with one of the five has skipped 52% to 55% (row 27). Fix: add named races to the title.
10. **G7 [RISK].** Risk: each viewer runs only one of the 8, so many may leave after their own tier or never reach S TIER. This is the format Luke chose; if he keeps it, it goes in the Known risks box.
11. **G5 [RISK].** Risk: with every race 3 to 6 months away, strangers may file it as "later" and scroll on (the last spring video skipped 52.1%). If nothing in the hook can be inside 4 weeks, it goes in the Known risks box.
