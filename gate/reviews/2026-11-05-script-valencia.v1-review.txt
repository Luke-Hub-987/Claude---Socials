## Stranger's question (G16)
"Which 6K of my Valencia marathon decide it, if the whole course is flat?" 32K TO 38K (stage 4) answers it. But it can be guessed as "the last 10K, from 32K". "The long way home" (late, a detour) and the rehook ("start right after" the 27K old town) narrow it before the marker. The caption's opening line ("It's 32K to 38K") gives it away outright. The only part a stranger couldn't guess is the why (the loop away from the finish to the park at 34K), and the gap isn't built around that.

## FAILs, in the order to fix them
1. G8: two stages of advice they already have (the 16 results trial's start band, 10 words and gel clock); the only new thing is a map fact with an old action. Cut stages 1 and 2 to one short stage, and give the 32K to 38K stretch a new instruction with a number at the 34K and 36K turns.
2. G16: the answer can be guessed (the wall zone), the concept name and the rehook narrow it early, and the caption names it. Build the gap on what they can't guess, give the concept a name with no hint of late or detour, write a rehook that reopens without placing it, and keep the stretch out of the caption's opening lines.
3. G17: line one has nothing to argue with on an all-flat course. Write a K-based claim Valencia runners would dispute.
4. G18: the pain repeats 200 plans' shape (the same Briar model) and long run v4's walking; the dream repeats match v2's reversal. Use new shapes, and the dream as something they see on the day.
5. G20: stage 2 has no pace and ends on "is fine". End it on the 10K to 27K band and the halfway clock.
6. G21: the caption plan skips 10K to 27K and the 6K the title is about, for every goal. Fill every stretch, with clock times at 10K, halfway, 32K and 40K, in km and miles.
7. G22: "about 5 words" at 32K+ contradicts the sub-4 plan's 2 or 3. 58:00 at 10K is already 67 s down, which clashes with "a minute down at halfway". The direction claims don't match the map. The blue carpet, the crowds and "won't spot them on the map" are untraced but marked Pass.
8. G9: cut "That's the bit you'll have heard about", and put the surge as an instruction, not a claim about runners.
9. G24: rewrite "won't spot them on the map", "watching it all go", "Most of it", "turns west" and "feels free" in plain words.
10. G29: rewrite the caption with no "isn't X. It's Y" and no fragments, and drop the "flew past you" echo.
11. G14: redraw the route as our own map frames, not the organiser's map artwork.
