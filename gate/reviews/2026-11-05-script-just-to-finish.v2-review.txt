## Stranger's question (G16)
"How can I run every K at sub-4 pace and still finish in 4:02?" It's one specific question, about the viewer, and most can't guess the answer from line one alone. It's meant to be answered in 30K TO THE FINISH ("Because your watch reads a bit long"). In practice the stages and the rehook ("So why can't you just run the pace on your watch?") name the watch first, so the answer lands one line early.

## FAILs, in the order to fix them
1. **G11 (title, a repeat of ledger row 27):** only one of five ("under 4 hours"). Add a second element without naming the watch: a method name used in the title, the hold line and the stages, or proof from our own work.
2. **G16 (the gap):** the rehook names the watch before the closing stage. Reopen line one without the watch ("And number 4 is where those 2 minutes come back."), and name the watch for the first time in the first line of 30K TO THE FINISH.
3. **G18 (dream, a repeat of ledger row 16):** "hit the 40K sign knowing sub-4 is done" is the 200 plans dream (a number on your watch at 40K). Build a dream at a different moment and with a different object, and fill in the "last 3 used" column honestly.
4. **G24 (words):** replace "your hand beats your wrist" with a literal line ("trust the numbers on your hand over the pace on your watch").
5. **G29 (second meanings):** cut "Hit that, and it's done" and the "beats" line. For example: "If your watch says 3:46 or less at the 40K sign, sub-4 is yours."
