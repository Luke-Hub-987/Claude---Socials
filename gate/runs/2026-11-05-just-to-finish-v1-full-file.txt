**[10K TO 30K]**

"Now settle between 5:35 and 5:40. Take a gel every 30 minutes, and grab a bottle of water if you're passing a station. At the 20K sign, your watch should say about 1:54. At the 30K sign, about 2:50."

"So why can't you just trust the pace on your watch?"

**[30K TO THE FINISH]**

"Your watch reads a bit long, a little more on every bend. Run sub-4 pace on it all day and you finish in about 4:02. That's why the time at each sign is the number to trust. If it's later than it should be, run the quicker end of your band. At the 40K sign, your watch should say about 3:47, and sub-4 is yours."

"Send this to the friend you're running with who also says it's just to finish."

## Caption
Told everyone your marathon is just to finish, but you want sub-4? Here's the plan, with the 4 numbers to check on the day.

Run the first 10K between 5:45 and 5:50 a K on your watch (9:15 to 9:23 a mile). Then settle between 5:35 and 5:40 (8:59 to 9:07 a mile) all the way home.

The 4 numbers are the time on your watch at each 10K sign: about 58:00 at 10K, 1:54 at 20K, 2:50 at 30K and 3:47 at 40K. Your watch reads a bit long over a marathon, so the pace on it can look like sub-4 and still bring you in around 4:02. The signs are measured on the course, so they're the check. If you're behind at a sign, run the quicker end of the band until you're back on.

Gels: a 30g gel at 20 minutes, then one every 30 minutes on the clock. If you're passing a water station, grab a bottle and take a few sips with it. That's about 60g of carbs an hour.

Want the same plan for your own goal? Email us your race and goal time through the link in the bio and we'll send it to you for free.
