## Script

"If you've got a marathon coming up, 10 words at the 5K sign tell you how you'll feel at 32K."

"Without them, 35K is a walk, and the people you passed at 10K go back past you one by one."

"After watching this, the race photo you'll share the next morning is you passing people at 35K."

"It's one plain sentence you can say without even thinking. You'll hear it when we get to the 5K sign, so save this."

**[THE START]**

"Your first 10K should feel too easy, with people streaming past you. For a sub-4, that's between 5:45 and 5:50 a K on your watch."

"If you want those numbers for your own goal, email us your race and goal time through the link in the bio."

**[THE FIRST GEL]**

"Your first gel goes in at 20 minutes, before you feel you need it. Take a 30 gram gel with a few sips of water. Then take one every 30 minutes on the clock, whether you feel like it or not."

**[THE 5K SIGN]**

"At the 5K sign, say this in one breath. I am running a marathon today and I feel good. If you can't get all 10 words out, you're going too fast, so ease off until you can."

"That's what it tells you about 5K. What it tells you about 32K is the part to save."

**[32K TO THE FINISH]**

"By 30K you'll get about 5 words out in a breath. Near the end, it's 2 or 3. If you had all 10 at the 5K sign, that's right on plan. The easy start is what's carrying you now. If you were down to 5 before halfway, this is where that start catches up with you. Every good result we've been sent with splits ran the second half as fast as the first or faster. So from 32K, take shorter, quicker steps and run one lamppost at a time."

"Send this to whoever's on the start line with you."

## Caption
Say this at the 5K sign, in one breath: "I am running a marathon today and I feel good." If all 10 words come out, your pace is right. If they don't, ease off until they do. By 30K you'll get about 5 words out in a breath, and 2 or 3 near the end. That's on plan. If you were down to 5 before halfway, you went off too fast, and 32K is where it shows.

The first 10K should feel too easy. For a sub-4, run it between 5:45 and 5:50 a K (9:15 to 9:23 a mile). For 3:45, between 5:25 and 5:30 (8:43 to 8:51 a mile). For 4:30, between 6:30 and 6:35 (10:28 to 10:36 a mile).

Your first gel goes in at 20 minutes: a 30g gel with a few sips of water. Then one every 30 minutes, whether you feel like it or not. That's about 60g of carbs an hour.

Every good result we've been sent with splits ran the second half as fast as the first or faster. From 32K, shorter, quicker steps, one lamppost at a time.

Want your own plan? Email us your race and goal time through the link in the bio and we'll send it to you for free.

Cover photo: [photographer], [licence], via [site].
