## Caption

Running a spring marathon? Here's each course's one change on the day, how to get in now if you haven't got a place, and the Monday your 16 weeks of training start.

S tier, Seville, Sunday 21 February. The flattest course in Europe, so nothing slows you early: run the first 10K about 5 seconds a K slower than goal pace, then check the clock at the 10K sign. Get in: the official waiting list (sold out at 20,000). 16 weeks started Monday 2 November.

A tier, Manchester, Sunday 18 April. About 54 metres of climbing all race; let the rise into Altrincham before halfway go, and check the clock at the 20K sign. Get in: charity places (general entry full). 16 weeks start Monday 28 December.

B tier, Barcelona, Sunday 14 March. The highest point is near 14K, with less climbing in the second half; hold the effort up to 14K, not the pace. Get in: open on the official site (until 37,000 or 7 March). 16 weeks start Monday 23 November.

B tier, Paris, Sunday 4 April. The tunnels by the Seine cut your watch's GPS, so ignore its pace in there and go by the clock at the next 10K sign. Get in: charity places. 16 weeks start Monday 14 December.

B tier, London, Saturday 24 or Sunday 25 April. Crowded till the starts merge, then Canary Wharf scrambles your watch past 30K; trust the clock at the 30K sign. Get in: charity places only (the ballot's closed, and places can't be transferred). 16 weeks start Monday 4 January.

C tier, Rome, Sunday 14 March. About 6K of cobbles; shorten your steps there and let the watch pace go. Get in: open on the official site. 16 weeks start Monday 23 November.

C tier, Brighton, Sunday 4 April. Short, sharp climbs, mostly done by 18K; hold the effort up them, not the pace. Get in: charity places (general entry sold out, no transfers). 16 weeks start Monday 14 December.

D tier, Belfast, Sunday 2 May. A steep drop out of Stormont and the hardest climb near 30K; don't let the drop pull you quicker than your plan for the first 10K. Get in: open on the official site. 16 weeks start Monday 11 January.

The time on your watch at the 10K, 20K, 30K and 40K signs, by goal: under 3:30, about 51:00, 1:39:30, 2:28 and 3:16. Under 3:45, about 54:15, 1:47, 2:39:30 and 3:32. Under 4, about 57:30, 1:54, 2:50 and 3:46. Under 4:30, about 1:05, 2:08, 3:10:30 and 4:13. Within 30 seconds is on plan.

Dates and entry routes checked on 9 November; check your race's own site before you book anything.
