If you've got a spring marathon booked, your 16 weeks might start before Christmas, or might have started already.

Start a few weeks late and you squeeze 16 weeks into 12, and race day takes back minutes your training earned.

Writing spring plans, we found the date matters as much as the course. It decides if your 16 weeks start in Christmas week, and if your longest runs are in January dark or March light.

Each race gets one reason and its start Monday, when your 16 weeks begin. Save this.

Belfast on 2 May is still open. It's got short, sharp hills and wind off the Lough. Start Monday 11 January.

Email us your spring race and goal time through the link in the bio for your free plan.

Rome on 14 March is still open, and about 6K of the course is cobbles. Start Monday 23 November. Brighton on 4 April has short, sharp climbs, most of them done by 18K. Start Monday 14 December.

Barcelona on 14 March is still open, on long straight roads with more of the climbing in the first half. Start Monday 23 November. Paris on 4 April rolls, with short dips through the tunnels by the river. Start Monday 14 December. London on 25 April gets crowded once the starts merge, so you'll weave. Start Monday 4 January.

Manchester on 18 April has about 54 metres of climbing in the whole race, one of the flattest in the UK. Start Monday 28 December, Christmas week.

And S tier is the only one where your 16 weeks have already started.

Seville on 21 February is the flattest course in Europe, on a mild February morning. Your 16 weeks started on 2 November, so if you've got a place, start from the week you're in.
