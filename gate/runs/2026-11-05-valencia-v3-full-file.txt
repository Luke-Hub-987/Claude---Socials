## Script

"If you're running Valencia, most of your gels will be due on a stretch of road with no water."

"Take one without water and you're hacking down a thick, sickly gel, with your stomach sloshing and 10K still to go."

"After watching this, you'll reach the blue carpet over the water with every gel taken on time. And your stomach won't have turned once."

"I'll go through where every one of them lands on this course, K by K, so save this."

**[THE START PEN]**

"Take a bottle of water into the start pen and keep hold of it. Your first 30 gram gel is due at 20 minutes, about 2K before the first water. Take it with that bottle, then bin it. Keep your watch between 5:45 and 5:50 a K to the 10K sign."

"If sub-4 isn't your goal, email us your race and goal time through the link in the bio for your own plan."

**[5.5K TO 34K]**

"From 5.5K, the water comes in small bottles, not cups. Most of your gels come due between the stations, not at them. So take a bottle at every station, sip it, and carry it with you. When a gel's due, take it with 3 or 4 sips from that bottle. Settle between 5:35 and 5:40, and you'll pass halfway at about 2:00:20."

"That works for every gel but one, and the course hands that one to you."

**[34K: THE FREE GEL]**

"At 34K the course hands out gels, with and without caffeine, that you've never trained with. For a sub-4, your own is due at about 35K, so leave theirs and wait 1K. That's your last gel, and 2 or 3 words between breaths is right on plan by now. Hold between 5:35 and 5:40 and you'll reach 40K at about 3:47."

"If someone you train with is running Valencia, send them this before they pack."

## Caption
Running Valencia on 6 December? Here's where every gel lands for sub-4, 3:45 and 4:30, and the paces in km and miles.

The water comes in 33cl bottles at 5.5, 10, 14.5, 18.7, 23, 28.3, 33.1, 37.1 and 40.4K, plus the finish. Your gels go on the clock: a 30g gel at 20 minutes, then one every 30 minutes, with 3 or 4 sips of water. That's about 60g of carbs an hour, and none in the last 20 minutes. Take a bottle into the start pen for your first gel, then take a bottle at every station and carry it until your next gel is due.

Sub-4: the first 10K between 5:45 and 5:50 a K (9:15 to 9:23 a mile), about 58:00 at 10K. Then between 5:35 and 5:40 (8:59 to 9:07 a mile) to the finish: halfway about 2:00:20, 40K about 3:46:40, finish about 3:59. Your gels land at about 3.5, 8.6, 13.9, 19.3, 24.6, 29.9 and 35.3K.

3:45: the first 10K between 5:25 and 5:30 a K (8:43 to 8:51 a mile), about 54:35 at 10K. Then between 5:15 and 5:20 (8:27 to 8:35 a mile): halfway about 1:53:20, 40K about 3:33:20, finish about 3:45. Your gels land at about 3.7, 9.2, 14.8, 20.5, 26.1, 31.8 and 37.5K.

4:30: the first 10K between 6:30 and 6:35 a K (10:28 to 10:36 a mile), about 1:05:25 at 10K. Then between 6:15 and 6:20 (10:03 to 10:12 a mile): halfway about 2:15:15, 40K about 4:14:10, finish about 4:28. Your gels land at about 3.1, 7.6, 12.3, 17.1, 21.9, 26.6, 31.4 and 36.2K.

The course hands out its own gels at about 16K, 26K and 34K, with and without caffeine. Leave them and take the ones you've trained with.

Want a plan for your own goal? Email us your race and goal time through the link in the bio and we'll send it to you for free.
