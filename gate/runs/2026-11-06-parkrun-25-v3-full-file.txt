## Script

"If you can still kick for the line in the last 100m, that time was in your legs all along."

"Then the email comes in at 25:04, 4 seconds off, and the next Saturday you go and run it the same way."

"After watching this, the friend who always out-sprints you at the end is behind you at the 4K beep."

"It all comes down to 3 pace ranges and the beep on your watch, so save this one before your next parkrun."

**[THE FIRST K]**

"Let the crowd go in the first 500m, even when it feels like you're losing ground. Your first K is the slowest of the 5, on purpose. Your watch beeps every K, and that first beep should read between 4:55 and 5:00."

"If you're training for a marathon, email us your race and goal time through the link in the bio."

**[K2 TO K4]**

"Now hold between 4:50 and 4:55, every K, all the way to the 4K beep. That's 3 beeps, and each one is a check. If a beep reads quicker than 4:50, ease off. If it's slower than 4:55, push until it reads 4:55 again."

**[THE 4K BEEP]**

"At the 4K beep, go. That last K takes about 5 minutes, and you'll want to stop in every one of them. Push between 4:45 and 4:50 all the way to the line."

"Which changes what happens in the last 100m."

**[THE LAST 100M]**

"There's nothing left to kick with, and that's where your sub-25 was hiding. The seconds you used to keep for the last 100m went into the middle 3K. Run those 3 ranges and the email should say about 24:37. That's your 4 seconds back, and 23 more."

"Send this to whoever you run parkrun with."

## Caption
If you can still sprint the last 100m at parkrun, those seconds belonged earlier. Here's the K-by-K plan for under 25 minutes, and every other common goal.

Your watch beeps every K. Run the first K a little slower than goal pace while the crowd goes, hold a touch quicker than goal pace from the first beep to the 4K beep, then push from 4K to the line so there's nothing left to sprint with. GPS watches read a bit long, so these ranges sit a few seconds under goal pace and the times below are your official parkrun time.

Under 25: the first K between 4:55 and 5:00 a K (7:55 to 8:03 a mile), K2 to K4 between 4:50 and 4:55 (7:47 to 7:55), the last K between 4:45 and 4:50 (7:39 to 7:47). About 24:37.

Under 20: 3:55 to 4:00 a K (6:18 to 6:26 a mile), then 3:50 to 3:55 (6:10 to 6:18), then 3:45 to 3:50 (6:02 to 6:10). About 19:34.

Under 22: 4:20 to 4:25 (6:58 to 7:06), then 4:15 to 4:20 (6:50 to 6:58), then 4:10 to 4:15 (6:42 to 6:50). About 21:40.

Under 28: 5:30 to 5:35 (8:51 to 8:59), then 5:25 to 5:30 (8:43 to 8:51), then 5:20 to 5:25 (8:35 to 8:43). About 27:34.

Under 30: 5:55 to 6:00 (9:31 to 9:39), then 5:50 to 5:55 (9:23 to 9:31), then 5:45 to 5:50 (9:15 to 9:23). About 29:40.

Building up to a marathon? Email us your race and goal time through the link in the bio and we'll send you your own plan for free.
