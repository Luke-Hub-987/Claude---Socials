## Formats and mechanics that pull (from the C accounts and podcasts: shapes, not topics)
- **The familiar thing beats the famous thing** (conor.talks: Cheesecake Factory beat Nvidia, 616K, 112x; Qatar owns padel, 15x).
- **The experience line** (conor.talks: "after 10 years with the biggest banks, the most impressive people had one thing in common", 35x; runnerdadlife: "30 marathons, 20 years coaching, one thing I've learned", 18x). Ours: the plans we've sent (state the true base, P187).
- **Sorry, gurus** (conor.talks: "Sorry, motivational speakers", 30x): a myth's own believers named in line one.
- **10 tiny habits / 10 tricks** (maya.mental.fitness 2.4M, 23x; projectaltuz 912K, 294x): the again series shape works on performance topics.
- **Borrowed from the elite** (maya: Gilbert Enoka's All Blacks technique, 6x): the pros' method for an amateur.
- **Team X or Team Y?** (precisionfandh: team gel or team chew; cnozrun: team pace vs team heart rate): sides to pick, affiliation in the comments.
- **Rating 1 to 10** (ryantafazolli 1.4M): rate the meals they eat.
- **Comment the word, get the thing** (therunningeffect, untapped, motionalintelligencepod, kelsey: "Comment FUEL for your free fuel station guide", 143K): our comment word to email (P220) is the norm.
- **Podcast clips that pull without a famous guest:** a hot take or debate (Room 101), a surprising number (112 bpm), a lesser-known rule (4 things that get you disqualified, 586K), "how hard is it really". Luke and Jamie can do all of these.
