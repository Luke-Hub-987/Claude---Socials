What's in your plan

A pace calculator gives you one number. Your plan gives you four.

Divide 42.2 by your goal and you get one even pace, run from the gun. That's how most people meet the wall at 30K. Here's what your plan does instead.

01
Even pace, 10K53:20
Your plan, 10K54:00

It starts you behind, on purpose

The first 10K runs 5 to 10 seconds a K slower than goal. For a 3:45 that's about 40 seconds down at the sign, and it's the cheapest 40 seconds of your race.

02
1%

It builds in your watch's error

A GPS watch reads about 1% long in a race. Your paces are a few seconds quick to cover it, so the chip still says your goal when the watch says 42.6.

03
30g

It puts your fuel on the clock

First gel at 20 minutes, then 30g every 30 minutes with water. That's about 60g of carbs an hour, and your plan tells you how many gels to carry.

04
2:00

It leaves room for a portaloo

Lose up to 2 minutes and you go straight back to your pace. No sprinting to win it back. The slack is already in your 4 times.
