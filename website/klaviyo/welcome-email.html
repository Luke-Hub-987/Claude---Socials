{% if person|lookup:'plan_10k' %}The four rules behind your plan{% else %}Four rules for your marathon{% endif %}
1
Start slower than your goal pace
Run the first 10K 5 to 10 seconds per K slower than the pace your goal time needs. You'll be up to a minute behind at halfway, and that's exactly how it should feel.
 
2
60g of carbs an hour, from minute 20
Take your first gel at minute 20, before you feel you need it. With 30g gels, take one every 30 minutes after that (every 40 with 40g gels, every 20 with 20 to 25g gels), each with 3 or 4 sips of water. Use gels you've already used on your long runs. If you have a fuelling routine that works in training, keep it on race day.
 
3
Check your effort with ten words
Say "I am running a marathon today and I feel good" out loud. At 10K you should get all ten words out in one breath. At 20K you still get ten words, but you sound breathy. At 30K you manage about five, and at 40K two or three.
 
4
When it hurts, shorten your steps
Take shorter, quicker steps. Then run one K at a time, or one lamppost at a time.
