Why we made it

Race morning is the hardest meal of the year

You're up at 6, you're nervous and you're not hungry. Breakfast was hours before the gun, and the start pen is no place for a bowl of porridge.

We wanted something you'd actually want to eat standing in the pen, that sits easy, with the carbs already counted. Nothing like it was on the shelf, so we started making it ourselves.

When you eat it

Before the run, never during it

Race morning

After breakfast, on the way to the start. 30g of carbs topped up before the gun.

Before a long run

Practise race morning on your Sunday long run, so nothing on the day is new.

Gels for the race

Once you're running, stick to your gels: 30g every 30 minutes with water. The bar does its job before the start.

What's in it

Short enough to read in the pen

Crisped riceHoneyMaple syrupSugarCoconut oilBeef gelatineSea saltVanilla

Rice gives you glucose and honey gives you fructose. Your gut takes the two in by different routes, so more carbs get in without sitting heavy. We're still testing the final recipe, and the waitlist gets the full label first.

The gelatine is beef, so the bar isn't vegetarian. We'd rather tell you now.
