What's in it

Short enough to read in the start pen.

  • Crisped riceThe crunch, and most of the carbs
  • HoneyGlucose and fructose, which your gut takes in by different routes
  • Maple syrupFlavour, and more fast carbs
  • SugarBinds it, and it's quick fuel
  • Coconut oilCoats the rice so it stays crisp
  • Beef gelatineHolds it together. Not vegetarian, sorry

How it fits

The bar before the gun. Gels after it.

In the start pen

Something you can actually eat

Nerves kill your appetite, and porridge doesn't sit well an hour out. A rice crisp square does. 30g of carbs, counted, so your first hour runs on a full tank.

Once you're running

Stick to your gels

Chewing at race pace is hard work. From 20 minutes in, it's 30g of gel every 30 minutes with water, like in our videos. The bar has done its job by then.
