# Website v5 brief: from emails to customers (22 Oct 2026)

Mail-tester: **10/10** (22 Oct, after SPF and DKIM went live). The inbox
problem is fixed at the root; the page now has to turn visitors into
customers for the bar.

## 1. What a top professional would do that wasn't asked for

- **Design the sale, not the page.** Map visitor → plan → email sequence →
  waitlist → founding order, and give each step one job and one number.
  A waitlist is cheap intent. The real test is money: a founding pre-order
  or a small refundable deposit once FSAI and price are settled.
- **Put the product inside the value.** The plan already covers race
  morning, which is where the bar lives. Selling inside the plan isn't a
  bolt-on banner.
- **Design for a thumb on a phone first.** Instagram traffic is mobile:
  the interactive bit has to sit in the first screen, above the email
  field, and be usable one-handed. Keep the page light.
- **Let the founders be seen and heard.** People buy from people they
  feel they know. The reels prove that; the page has two still photos.
- **Build the proof machine, not just the proof.** After Dublin on Sunday,
  results arrive by the dozen. Collect them on the page, with permission,
  so the results wall refills itself.
- **Measure every step.** Form start, plan unlocked, waitlist yes,
  scroll depth, by device and by video.

## 2. The rawest material to start from

- Raw reel footage and outtakes of Luke and Jamie (the personality), not
  the edited captions.
- DMs, voice notes and email replies after races, in full, with dates.
- Session recordings of real mobile visitors (Microsoft Clarity).
- Klaviyo form events by day and source; welcome email replies.
- The bar itself: photos and video of it breaking, tester reactions on
  camera, the lab panel.
- Instagram comments asking "what about my time?" (the demand, in their
  words).

## 3. Methods and tools

- **Copy:** Ogilvy (specific facts, news and "free" in the headline,
  captions under every picture, testimonials with names); Caples
  (test headlines on real traffic); Cashvertising (self-interest, social
  proof, specificity); Briar Cochran (unexpected value, counter-position).
- **Mobile UX:** 390px-first layout, thumb zone, native inputs, a sticky
  call to action, nothing that needs a hover.
- **Speed:** WebP images, explicit image sizes, lazy loading; PageSpeed
  Insights on mobile (target LCP under 2.5 s, CLS under 0.1).
- **Measurement:** Microsoft Clarity (heatmaps, recordings, rage clicks);
  Klaviyo "Viewed pace plan" events and properties; UTM per reel; Shopify
  sessions by device.
- **Testing:** Klaviyo/Shopify A/B on the headline; a 5-second test with
  5 runners who've never seen the account.
- **Validation:** a founding pre-order or deposit product in Shopify once
  price and FSAI are done.

## 4. What shows it's right (other than Luke or Claude)

- Mobile visitor → email conversion, against today's baseline.
- Plan unlocked → waitlist yes rate.
- Waitlist → founding orders (money is the only honest test of the bar).
- Results submitted after Dublin and Frankfurt.
- PageSpeed mobile score and LCP; Clarity scroll depth and rage clicks.
- Mail-tester 10/10 holding; "I didn't get it" messages near zero.
