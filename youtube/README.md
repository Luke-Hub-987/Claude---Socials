memory, a chat per job (`podcast/README.md`). **This chat does only the
monthly long-form YouTube videos and works in `youtube/`.** The podcast has its

## What a long-form video is here (P212, plan 0.4)
- **Monthly, produced, separate from the podcast.** Filmed on Luke's real runs
  and in the car (the set behind our top 5 reels), with voiceover and graphics.
  About 10 to 15 hours of work between Luke and Jamie, so one a month is the
  ceiling.
- **Never cut into clips**, with one exception: a 30-second trailer reel and a
  story on release day, because a small channel gets almost no browse traffic.
- **Every video has its own email reason** tied to that video, on a separate
  YouTube link to the same form (a different URL, no extra step: P154), so we
  can see whether YouTube brings sign-ups (plan 0.5).
- **Rules that apply:** FACTS.md for every number, grams an hour first (P118),
  the bar's slot is before the start and never in the race (P134), UK and EU
  health-claims rules on anything said about the bar, the viewer as the hero
  (P158), only what this audience does (P126), a method they use today (P127),
  a brief before a script (P138), the LLM Council (P150). **GATE.md is for
  reels:** the hook word counts don't apply here.
- **Race facts come from FACTS only** (sourced there, P214); anything not in
  FACTS isn't stated. **Jobs (P215):** "we've both got jobs" is fine, titles
  included; out is the career angle (running or ultras make you better at work).
- Packaging: 1920 x 1080 thumbnail (the 16:9 rule), one face plus 3 or 4 words,
  black bold on white in the top band (P205); chapters with searchable names;
  description line one is the email reason.

## The Art O'Neill documentary: shot list
**The rule (plan 0.7):** Luke's race is the story; the marathoner's lesson is
what each chapter hands over. Race facts on screen only from FACTS.
**The goal (Luke, 17 Nov): top 10 in his class (Ultra - Male).** 10th in that
class in 2026 was **10:35:37**. Always "top 10 in my class", never "top 10"
alone: 10th overall was 8:29:15, so "top 10" on its own claims a goal Luke
hasn't set (FACTS).
**Title (recommended):** *8 WEEKS TO BEAT 10:35 (A 62KM NIGHT RACE, WITH A
FULL-TIME JOB)*. "Top-10 finish" in a title reads as overall, so the target
time carries it instead: a number, a deadline and a constraint (P178), and
nothing to misread. The bet is filmed Fri 20 Nov and the race is Fri 15 Jan:
exactly 8 weeks. "Art O'Neill" and "top 10 in my class" go in the first line of
the description and the spoken open. Fallback: *8 WEEKS TO A TOP 10 IN MY CLASS
(WITH A FULL-TIME JOB)*.
**The target on screen:** 10:35:37, the time the whole film chases. Thumbnail:
Luke in a head torch at night, top band black on white **BEAT 10:35**, with a
watch showing the time so far. Say "last year's 10th place" every time it's
used: this year's 10th could be faster or slower.

**No crew (P216).** Everything is phone video Luke or Jamie shoots, propped up
or in hand, and all of it is optional. If a clip doesn't get filmed, the film
leans on the podcast footage instead. Four things, that's the list:
