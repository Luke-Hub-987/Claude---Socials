# The 2027 framework: brief

## The promise and the belief broken
**The belief:** your finish time is set by your fitness, so the only way to run
faster is to train more.
**What we break:** fitness sets the ceiling; race day decides how much of it
reaches the finish. Most runners lose minutes they already earned, in four
places, and all four come down to one thing: **about 500 g of stored carbs that
runs short at about 3 hours at 4-hour pace, around 32K.** Every part of the
framework fills that tank, spends it slower, tops it up, or checks you aren't
burning it too fast.
**The promise:** the same legs, a faster finish. The viewer leaves with their
own numbers for all four parts, for their goal and weight, on one page.

**Why this beats a list of our old reels:** each part is already a reel (carb
load, first 10 minutes, the gel clock, every 10K). Played back as four tips,
it's advice they've seen (ledger row 26). The tank is the new thing: one reason
that ties the four together, so they remember the system, not four rules.

## LLM Council (P150)
- **Contrarian:** It's a compilation. Anyone who follows us has seen all four
  parts, and anyone who doesn't won't find an 18-minute video from a channel
  with no subscribers. "Faster than you deserve" over-promises: the framework
  stops you losing time, it doesn't add fitness.
- **Assumption-ripper:** That YouTube search will find it in week one (it
  won't; the trailer reel and the emails will). That the viewer's goal is
  sub-4 (most are 3:30 to 4:30, so sub-4 as the example and the rest on the
  page). That Jamie wants to be on camera (plan question 3, still open). That
  "deserve" means fitness to the viewer: it does if line one says so.
- **Expansionist:** The tank makes it a system nobody else in our space
  teaches as one idea, and the page turns every viewer into an email with a
  race, goal and weight, which is the launch list for the bar. Every reel from
  January can end "the full framework is on the channel".
- **Outsider:** "What's a framework?" Say "four things you do on race day and
  the two days before" and never say "framework" in the audio after the title.
- **Executor:** Luke's yes this week; two long runs filmed in December (the
  first 10K and the gel clock) and one car session; script by 14 Dec through
  the facts check; edit over Christmas; out Sun 3 Jan with the trailer reel and
  an email the same morning. Judged on sign-ups from the YouTube link and
  average view duration, not views.
- **Blind cross-review:** The compilation point stands, and the tank answers

  the first minute as "what your training already gave you". Search won't
  carry it at first, so the trailer and the email are the launch, as the
  Assumption-ripper said. "Framework" leaves the audio.
- **Chairman:** **Make it, built on the tank: four parts, each a number they
