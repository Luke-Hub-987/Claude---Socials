# Why slow internet club's 10/10 carousels work (25 Nov; Luke: no time to film tomorrow, a carousel like this instead; assess first, plan after)

Post: 10/10 BRAIN SKILLS YOU CAN LEARN IN A DAY (part 4), TikTok photo carousel, 6 Nov: 2.3M views, 230.5K likes, **170.8K saves (about 7.4% of views; our best reel is 2.7%)**, 11.5K shares, 338 comments. Screens `research/title-examples/2026-11-25-slowinternet-*.png`.

## Why it works
1. **The cover is a score plus a promise.** A giant blue 10/10 does the stopping, and "YOU CAN LEARN IN A DAY" removes the cost (P244, low friction). The status is in the word (brain, cooler, genius, smart): Seth's status, achieved cheaply.
2. **A real person cut out, sitting on the number.** It feels editorial, not stock and not an ad. A white background with nothing else on it.
3. **One slide = one skill, always the same three layers:**
   - a 2-line verb-first headline, with line 2 in a colour that changes per slide (READ ANY / RUSSIAN WORD);
   - one picture: someone else's YouTube thumbnail, with the channel and its views (6.9M views), so the proof is borrowed;
   - a 4-to-5-word payoff: the time it costs and the outcome ("9 minutes. Cyrillic, decoded.").
4. **It's a filter, not a lesson.** It doesn't teach; it picks the best long video for each skill. The save is a to-do list ("save this for a boring day"), so saves are close to likes.
5. **Seven slides, not nineteen.** Each one can be read in about 2 seconds, and swiping is at the viewer's pace: the "stop and wait" Luke felt.
6. **A series** (part 3, part 4, tier list of ...): the format is the brand, and the audience comes back for the next part.
7. **One sponsor slide (Memo, an app) in the middle**, in the same layout, so it doesn't read as an ad. That's the future bar slot, only when the bar exists (P238).

## What it means for us (from our own carousels)
- The gut carousel read as an ad in ERN colours (455 IG views); the heavier carousel had 19 slides with the aha at slide 6. This format fixes both: white, about 7 slides, payoff on every slide (P211).
- The save pattern matches ours: our 1.6%+ saves all handed over numbers to keep (P224). Their "9 minutes. Cyrillic, decoded." is the same act.
- Risk: other people's thumbnails are their copyright. Credit them (as they do), or use our own pictures or reels on the slide.
- Must still pass: a problem they know they have (P246), measured numbers only (P245), a comment word -> email with the links (P220).
