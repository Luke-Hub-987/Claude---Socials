# After the autumn marathons: what we cover

## What the brand advice says, and what our data says
- **The advice:** people don't care how the brand started. They care
  whether they see themselves in what happens next: who they were before,
  who they become after. Every message sits on that line.
- **Our data already agrees.** The surprise (a founder story) ran 44.1%
  skip and 0 saves. Our winners are all the viewer's before and after: the
  runner walking at 32K becomes the one passing people (the wall, without
  the wall, quit, the walk).
- **For ERN the line is:** before, the runner who goes out too fast, takes
  gels when they feel like it, walks the last 10K and dreads the next
  gel. After, the runner who runs the last 10K strong, on a plan, with
  fuel they don't dread (the bar's job: the 30 minutes before).

## The calendar doesn't stop
Marathon season moves rather than ends. December races (Valencia, Malaga
and others) are weeks away, and spring races (Seville, Barcelona, Rome,
Paris, Manchester, London; check each date) open training blocks from
December. Race-day topics are searched all year. The gate "If you've got a
marathon coming up" (P153) covers all of it.
