## What is wrong with the current bio

**1. "Not a protein bar. Not a gel." is the banned construction.** We

writing tell and a false-negative framing, and then the profile that
those scripts point at opens with two of them. It also spends the
second line of the account telling people what it **is not**, which is

**2. "Hybrid Professional" names a person who is not arriving.** Every

are greeted with a category they have never heard and would not use

actually brings.**

**3. It is about the product, not the viewer.** P57 exists because
every ERN OST used to be about ERN. The bio reverted to it. Someone who
just watched a good marathon video lands on a bar pitch.

**4. It gives no reason to follow.** A profile visit is a follow
decision, and nothing on the page says what they get if they do.

**5. It implies the bar exists.** "The first carb bar built for" reads
as available. The videos say "still pre-launch", which is both honest
and more interesting.

## The caption template

The caption is the second search surface and it is currently doing
nothing. It has three jobs, in this order:

1. **Carry the search terms the OST could not.** The OST has to read
   in one second, so it drops words. The caption picks them up.
2. **Carry the dated urgency**, which P82 keeps out of the audio so
   the video keeps earning after 25 October. The caption can be edited
   or left to age, and it costs nothing.
3. **Ask the question that earns the save**, once, at the end.

**Template:**

```
[The dated line. "Dublin is 5 weeks on Sunday."]

[One sentence of what the video gives, written with the words
people type, not the words on the card.]

[The question, one line.]

#marathon #marathontraining #dublinmarathon #running #runningtips
```

**Worked example, the goal pace video:**

```
Dublin is 5 weeks on Sunday, so this is the last weekend the
answer is still useful.

How to work out your real marathon goal pace off one long run:
20k easy, 10k at goal marathon pace, 2k home, then check your
heart rate drift, your breathing and your legs. Full numbers in
the video.

What time are you going for?

#marathon #marathonpace #marathontraining #dublinmarathon
#goalpace #running #runningtips #marathonfuel
```

**Rules:**

- **Never repeat the OST as the first line of the caption.** It is
  already on screen, so it wastes the one line people read.
- **Write the search line in query words**, not the prefix. "How to
  work out your marathon goal pace" rather than "In this video I
  explain".
- **The question goes last and stays open.** "What time are you going
  for" gets an answer. "Did this help" does not.
- **Hashtags: five to eight**, mixing one broad, two topic, one race.
  They are a small lever, so do not spend copy on them.
