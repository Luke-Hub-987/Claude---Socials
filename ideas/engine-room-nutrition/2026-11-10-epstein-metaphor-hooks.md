## Rules (so it stays true and ours)
- One metaphor a video, an everyday object, and it has to match the mechanism
  exactly. A decoration fails.
- Never one we've used: "a full fuel tank" (carb load) and "a furnace" are taken.
- Loss words only when true (Luke's rule for intensifiers): destroying,
  wrecking, stealing, killing, dying. "Rubbish" and "lie" stay out of titles.
- The proof is a real study or our own count, said with its true base.
