## Line one (a scene they've lived, then the gap)

someone who looked half as fit as you, you've met the third."

## The stages (one clock: one race, three runners)
1. **THE FINISHER.** "I just want to get round." No goal time, a gel at halfway
   because a volunteer held one out, walking the stations from 30K. What moves
   them up: a goal time and a plan.
2. **THE RACER.** Goal pace from the gun, the watch checked every 400 metres,
   5K ahead of schedule and feeling brilliant, gels "when I feel I need one".
   The words at the finish: "I was on for 3:50 till 30K." What moves them up:
   the clock, not the feeling.
3. **THE HUNTER.** Bored at 10K and fine with being passed. A gel at 20
   minutes when they feel great, then every 30, on the clock. At 35K, one back
   20 metres ahead, then the next. **The honest cost:** people pass you in the
   first 10K, and it stings.
**Rehook before stage 3:** "And the hunter isn't fitter than the racer. Most
of the time it's the same runner, one marathon later."
**Repeat line:** "The racer wins the first 30K. The hunter wins the last 12."
**Comment ask (pinned):** "Finisher, racer or hunter? One word."
**Follow, identity last:** "Follow if you want to be the one hunting at 35K."
