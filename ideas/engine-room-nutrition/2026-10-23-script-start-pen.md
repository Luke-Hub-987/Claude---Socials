## Script (327 words, about 1:36)
If you're running a marathon in the next few weeks, join the portaloo queue, and the second you come out, join it again. That's the calmest you'll be all morning.

Skip it, and it's 10 minutes to the gun, you're 30 people back in the queue, bursting, and you can hear them closing the pens. You start at the very back and spend the first 3K weaving past people.

Do it, and you walk into your pen 20 minutes early, empty, hoodie still on, and the gun's just the start of another long run.

And there's one thing to do the moment the gun goes that sets up your whole first 10K. Save this, and send it to whoever you're starting with.

Be at the start 75 minutes before the gun, in an old hoodie you'll throw away. At 60 minutes to go, join a portaloo queue. At a big race they can take 15 to 25 minutes.

Want your whole race morning timed out like this? Email us your race and goal time through the link in the bio, free. Right, you're out of the portaloo.

Walk straight to the back of the queue again. You don't need to go yet, but you will in about 20 minutes, because nerves do that, and by then you're near the front.

Last sips of water by 30 minutes to go. Anything after that is in your bladder by 5K.

At 20 minutes to go, you're out of the second queue and into your pen. Stand at the back of your pace group, keep the hoodie on, and drop it at the side a minute before the gun.

So what's the one thing when the gun goes?

When the gun goes, walk to the mat. Don't jog, don't weave. Start your watch the moment you step on it, and let the first K be the slowest of your day. That's how your first 10K starts calm instead of chasing.
