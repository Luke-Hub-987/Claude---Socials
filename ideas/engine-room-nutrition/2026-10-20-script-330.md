## Script v2 (332 words, 1,716 characters spoken, about 1:37)
If you're running a marathon in the next few weeks, you can stop for a portaloo at twenty K and still break three thirty, and that's your race going to plan.

Panic about that lost minute and you'll run the next K flat out to win it back, then pay for it at thirty-five. Get this right, and your chip still says three twenty-nine.

There's a right way to get that minute back, and it's slower than you'd think. Save this.

Start with four times. Fifty minutes at ten K, one thirty-nine at twenty, two twenty-nine at thirty and three eighteen at forty. Write them on your wrist, or put them on your lock screen.

If three thirty isn't your goal, email us your race and goal time through the link in the bio and we'll send you your own four times, free. Back to three thirty.

For the first ten K, run four fifty-nine a K on your watch. You should be able to say "I am running a marathon today and I feel good", all ten words in one breath, in your normal voice. Fifty at the ten K sign means you've nailed it.

From ten to thirty, drop to four fifty-two on the watch. At twenty, look for one thirty-nine, ten words but a bit breathy. At thirty, two twenty-nine, and about five words before you need a breath.

So what do you do about that portaloo minute?

Leave it. From ten K you've been running a few seconds a K quicker than three thirty needs, so those four times carry nearly two minutes spare. Lose one and you'll just be a minute behind each time on your wrist, which is fine. Go straight back to four fifty-two, hold it to forty, then run the last two K at four forty-two, and you'll finish around three twenty-nine. Lose more than two minutes, and take it back five seconds a K over the next ten K, never in one go.

## Caption
How to pace a 3:30 marathon at every 10K. Your four times: about 50 minutes at the 10K sign, 1:39 at 20K, 2:29 at 30K and 3:18 at 40K. Put them on your wrist or your lock screen.

Run 4:59 a K (8:01 a mile) on your watch for the first 10K, then 4:52 a K (7:50 a mile) to 40K, then 4:42 a K (7:34 a mile) for the last 2K. That finishes around 3:28. The watch paces are a few seconds quicker than real 3:30 pace (4:59 a K) because a GPS watch reads about 1% long in a race, and from 10K they leave you nearly two minutes spare.

That spare time is for the race you'll actually run. If you stop for a portaloo or lose time at a drink station, don't sprint to win it back. Up to two minutes, go straight back to your pace and you'll be a minute or two behind your four times, which is fine. More than that, take it back five seconds a K (about eight seconds a mile) over the next 10K, never in one go.

Effort check with the sentence "I am running a marathon today and I feel good": all ten words in one breath to 10K, ten slightly breathy to 20K, about five by 30K, two or three at the end.

Different goal? Email us your race and goal time through the link in the bio and we'll send your own four times in km and miles, free.
