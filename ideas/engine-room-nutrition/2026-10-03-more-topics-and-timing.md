# Timing, and more topics that pass

## 1. Do we hold a video until the day before? No

- **There is no single race day for this audience.** Chicago 11 Oct,
  Amsterdam 18 Oct, Dublin 25 Oct, Frankfurt 26 Oct, New York 1 Nov.
  Any day we post is race week for some of them and three weeks out for
  others.
- **The 24 hours video proves it.** It went out on 1 October, days
  before any major race, and grew to 26.9K with a record 4.4% save rate:
  people save it for the day it applies.
- **Videos compound for days** (P92: 2x to 3x after the first read).
  Posting earlier gives them that time before the big race weekends.
- **Nothing in the audio is dated** (P82) and the gate is "in the next
  few weeks", so a video is never early or late.

**Rule: post when it is ready, in queue order.** The last week video
going out now lands in Chicago's race week and two to three weeks ahead
of Amsterdam and Dublin, which is exactly when people save it.
