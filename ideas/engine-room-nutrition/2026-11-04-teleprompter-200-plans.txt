The first is the start. Every set of splits we saw from a PB was as fast or faster in the second half. For a sub-4, that's between 5:40 and 5:45 on your watch for the first 10K, and it should feel almost too easy.

The third is one sentence. One runner said it on repeat from the start to 40K: I am running a marathon today and I feel good. By 40K they couldn't remember the words. That's the test working: all 10 in one breath early, a bit breathy by halfway, about 5 by 30K.
