# The last 30 minutes before the gun

## Why this topic

Every marathon video ever made covers the pacing, the carb load, and
the fuelling from 10k onwards. **Nobody covers the last thirty
minutes**, which is the exact window the brand exists for (P44), the
last point anything can still be changed for free, and a genuine
race-week search query while Dublin is coming ⚑.

Counter-position, stated plainly in the video: everyone plans the race
and nobody plans the half hour before it.
