### The psychology
1. **Curiosity needs a gap between what they think and what's true.** If
   the answer is "eat 3 hours before", there's no gap; they already have
   it. The gap here: players blame fitness for the late fade and answer it
   with more laps. The wall explains it in a way they haven't heard.
2. **The stake is public.** A marathoner hits the wall alone. A player
   who's gone at 80 minutes is watched by the team, the opposition and the
   sideline. That's a bigger fear to name.
3. **Half-time is the empty slot.** Most club players have water, an
   orange slice or nothing at half-time. It's the one moment v1 left out,
   and it's the marathon gel taken before you need it, in football form.
   It's also the bar's second slot in every match.
4. **The share goes to the group chat.** Every amateur team lives in one
   WhatsApp group. "Send this to the team WhatsApp" is a picture and a
   real habit; "whoever drives you to the match" wasn't.
