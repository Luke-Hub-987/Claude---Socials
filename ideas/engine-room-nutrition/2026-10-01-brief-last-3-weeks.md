## The secret (Thiel)

**Doing less in the last weeks can make you faster, and most runners
waste it by cutting the wrong thing.** The research says cut the
miles and keep the pace. Most people cut both, and many feel flat and
heavy in race week and panic, when that is normal.

## Four stages, the days before the race

## ⚑ To verify against the full papers before scripting (P99)

- **Bosquet et al., 2007, Med Sci Sports Exerc** (meta-analysis, 27
  studies): about a 2-week taper, volume reduced 41 to 60%, intensity
  and frequency kept. Confirmed from the abstract; read the paper.
- **"About three percent"**: commonly cited from Mujika and Padilla,
  2003. Check the figure and its range in the paper before it goes in
  the hook; if it does not hold, the hook uses the volume finding.
- **Feeling flat in race week**: widely reported, not a study finding.
  Said as "a lot of runners".
- **Carb loading**: match the night-before script's wording exactly.
