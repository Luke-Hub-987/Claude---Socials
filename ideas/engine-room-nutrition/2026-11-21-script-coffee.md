## Script

"One instant coffee before your run is about half the dose the studies used, if you weigh 70kg. At the full dose the same pace feels about 5% easier. So here's the dose for your weight, and when to drink it."

**[YOUR DOSE]**

"The studies dose caffeine by weight, at about 3 milligrams for every kilo you weigh. So at 70kg you need about 210 milligrams. That's 2 mugs of instant, or a filter and a half. A mug of instant has about 100, but a filter has about 140. Save this and screenshot the card, so you've got the dose for your weight."

**[WHAT IT DOES]**

"Across 21 trials, caffeine made the same effort feel about 5.6% easier than a dummy pill. It won't give you new legs, but halfway through your parkrun the pace burns a bit less. It doesn't change how the very end feels. So it's the middle of the run where you'll notice it most. The pace stays the same, but it costs you less."

**[WHEN]**

"Have it about 60 minutes before you start, because that's how the studies timed it. So for a 9am parkrun, your coffee goes in at 8 with breakfast. That's an hour before the start, not in the car on the way there."

"So is more always better?"

**[HOW MUCH IS TOO MUCH]**

"No, because doses around 9 milligrams a kilo bring more side effects without the extra boost. Café cups don't print the caffeine, so on run days count the mugs you make at home. Try your dose on a training run first, never on race day, because the trials found big differences between people."

"If your Saturday group can't start without a coffee, send them this before their next long run."

## Caption
One instant coffee before your run is about half the dose the studies use for a 70kg runner. The sports nutrition guidance (ISSN position stand, 2021) puts the dose at 3 to 6 mg of caffeine per kg of body weight, usually taken about 60 minutes before, with the minimum maybe as low as 2 mg/kg. Across 21 studies, caffeine made the same effort feel about 5.6% easier than a placebo (Doherty and Smith, 2005), though the rating at the very end of an all-out effort didn't change.

Your dose at 3 mg per kg, about 60 minutes before (mugs are rough: instant about 100 mg, filter about 140 mg; café coffees vary and don't print it). 60kg: 180 mg, about 2 instants or 1.5 filters. 70kg: 210 mg, about 2 instants or 1.5 filters. 80kg: 240 mg, about 2.5 instants or 1.5 filters. 90kg: 270 mg, about 2.5 instants or 2 filters. The floor is about 2 mg per kg (120, 140, 160 and 180 mg). Around 9 mg/kg brings more side effects and no extra benefit. Try it on a training run first, never on race day; people respond very differently.
