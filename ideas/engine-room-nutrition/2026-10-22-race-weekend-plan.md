## The things we wouldn't think of
1. **Old reels count.** The window counts views on any reel, not just new
   ones. Pin the wall, 7 days and the day before to the top of the grid
   today, and reshare the day before (Fri), 30 minutes before (Sun 6:30am)
   and the wall (Mon) to stories. People racing this weekend binge them.
2. **Send beats save this weekend.** Shares drive non-follower reach, and
   race-weekend content gets sent to running partners (Dublin km by km:
   1.8% shares, our best). Swap "save this" for "send this to who you're
   running with" on the Fri and Sat videos.
3. **Trial Reels before every post.** Two first lines, posted as trial
   reels at 8am to non-followers; share the lower-skip one at 12:30. No
   risk to the main account's numbers.
4. **Sunday is dead for new reels.** Racers are running or sleeping; don't
   waste a reel. Stories only.
5. **The audience's calendar ends on Sunday.** After Dublin, Frankfurt and
   New York, "the next few weeks" applies to far fewer people. From Mon 27
   the gate, the CTA ("email us your next race") and the topics need to
   move to spring marathons. Decide that before Monday's script.
6. **Facebook doesn't count toward the 1M.** It's Instagram only, so don't
   chase Facebook numbers in the reviews.
7. **Reply to every comment in the first hour.** Comments in the first hour
   are an early signal; 0.2% comment rate is our weak spot.
8. **No goal-time video this weekend.** They bring emails, but this
   weekend's racers already have their plan; reach is the job until Monday.
