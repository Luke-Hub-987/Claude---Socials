## The fix: one verb, one "you", same brutal tag
**THE MARATHON WALL / HITS YOU IN 5 STAGES / (EACH ONE MORE BRUTAL)** (17 / 20 / 22)

- **HITS YOU** is the violence and the viewer in two words. It's also the
  opinion: the wall isn't one moment at 32K, it's five blows (fixes 1, 2, 3).
- **The top line is the famous fear** (THE MARATHON WALL), not "THE 5 STAGES OF
  THE" (fixes 4).
- **(EACH ONE MORE BRUTAL)** stays as it was: dread, and the reason to stay for
  stage 5.
- **Rule check:** 22 characters or fewer a line (G10); in what? the marathon,
  with a number (G11, two of five); doesn't answer itself (which stages, which
  one am I at, how do I stop it, G12); no contradiction with the payoff (it
  hits in stages, so there's a stage where it can be stopped, row 32); true.

## Line one, so it adds something new (G13)
The old line one repeated "it came in 5 stages", which the title now says.

"If you've ever hit the wall at 32K, the first warning came at 23K, and that's
the only stage where you can still stop it." (25 words)

- New in it: 32K (where they felt it), 23K (the warning they missed), and the
  way out. The gap: what's the warning, and how do I stop it there?
- It doesn't contradict the 147K reel (the wall *starts* in the first 10K):
  the cause starts early, the first warning shows at 23K.

## Honest odds

spring blocks start). This is the strongest version our evidence supports: a
feeling word Luke would stop for, a verb, the viewer, the famous fear, a number.

13.5h. A wall title in the new spec should sit at the top of that range or above.
