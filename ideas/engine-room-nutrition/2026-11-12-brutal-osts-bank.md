# The brutal bank: 15 titles beyond the wall

## The 24x edit (what changed between 1,227 and 29.7K likes)
"How To Develop Nerves Of Steel" became "3 Habits To Create Nerves Of Steel
(progressively more brutal)". Three edits: **a count** (3), **a stronger verb**
(create, not develop), **a tag that promises it gets harder**. Every title below
is our dull version put through an edit like that, and each uses one of these:

| Device | From | The move |
|---|---|---|
| Count + ability + tag | Nerves of steel (29.7K) | Number, the thing they want to become, a bracket that escalates |
| The myth said as if true, then killed | Morning Coffee ("Carbs will make you fat... That is the stupidest thing I can say") | Say what everyone believes in your own voice, then demolish it with proof |
| A bad word made good | 3 "Cold" Behaviours You SHOULD DO | A word they'd avoid, in quote marks, recast as the secret |
| The danger | Photographic memory ("Be warned... massive strain on your nervous system") | Point at a real cost or risk |
| Nobody tells you / they aren't doing it | Dante Brunelli ("5 things your competitors aren't doing") | Hidden knowledge plus an enemy |
| The price contrast | (ours) | Money against a free fix |
| The flip line | (Ogilvy) | A sentence that turns on itself, so it sticks |
| The bracket twist | (SECRETLY), our 10.4K | One word in brackets that changes the whole title |

## Morning Coffee's opener, our way
His shape: **the myth in his own voice, as if he believes it → the kill → the
proof → straight into number one.** He never says hello and he's at item one by
0:05. Our kill lines (rotate, one per video): "That's backwards." "That's the
worst advice in running." "It's about 36 hours too late." Our proof lines:
Luke's 3:44:13 in Dublin; the runners he passed in carbon shoes; the 200 free
plans; the studies we've vetted (5,205 runners' watch data).
**Not ours:** swearing, "stupidest" (Luke's call: it isn't swearing, but it's
on the playbook's not-ours list), insulting the viewer.
**A rule conflict for the gate chat:** his kill lines are 1 to 3 words
("Wrong."), and P202 bans 3- and 4-word sentences. My recommendation: one terse
kill line allowed in the opener, nowhere else.

## Pre-mortem (the bank)
| Why these would flop | Fix |
|---|---|
| **Brutal turns into rude**: Morning Coffee works because he's himself and has the proof; copied harshness without proof sounds like a stranger shouting | Every kill line is followed inside 5 seconds by Luke's proof or a named study |
| **The kill is wrong**: overturning a myth that's actually true loses trust for good | Nothing goes up until the fact is vetted (the status column) |
| **The same device twice in a row becomes a template** (row 16) | One device per post, rotated down the table |
| **Repackaged old topics read as repeats** (7, 8, 10) | Trial reels to strangers only (P174, G32), never the profile within weeks |
| **The titles are brutal but the body is our old 1:30 with 40 to 55 words before stage 1** | The patterns note: the value by 0:07, a named item every 15 seconds |
