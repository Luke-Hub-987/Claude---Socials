"We sent 200 runners a free marathon plan, and one who finished 10 minutes ahead of hers was behind it at 5K."

**[THE START]**

"The first is the start. Every set of splits we saw from a PB was as fast or faster in the second half. For a sub-4, that's between 5:40 and 5:45 on your watch for the first 10K, and it should feel almost too easy."

"If you want your own, email us your race and goal time through the link in the bio, and you'll be runner 201."

**[THE GEL CLOCK]**

"The second is the gels. The ones who mentioned fuel said they stuck to the clock: a 30 gram gel at 20 minutes, then one every 30, with a few sips of water, whether they felt like it or not."

**[THE SENTENCE]**

"The third is one sentence. One runner said it on repeat from the start to 40K: I am running a marathon today and I feel good. By 40K they couldn't remember the words. That's the test working: all 10 in one breath early, a bit breathy by halfway, about 5 by 30K."

"And then there's the 2 that missed."

**[THE 2 THAT MISSED]**

"One was bang on pace until both calves cramped. The other first slowed at 33K. Neither went wrong at the start. That's the part a plan on race day can't fix, because it comes from the long runs you do before it."

"Send this to whoever you're running your next marathon with."
