"The third is the 10 words. I am running a marathon today and I feel good. All 10 in one breath early, a bit breathy by halfway, about 5 by 30K. Down to 5 before halfway, you've gone too fast. By 40K that runner couldn't remember the words, which is exactly where you want to be."

"One was bang on pace until both calves cramped. The other first slowed at 33K. Both started right. That's the part race day can't fix, so practise the gel clock and the 10 words on every long run before it."

"Send this to whoever you're running your next marathon with."
