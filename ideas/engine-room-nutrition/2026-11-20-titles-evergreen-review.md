## Top 3 (rewritten)
1. **T4 rewritten.** Long-run runners with no race booked confidently believe "a gel is instant energy", the packet's own claim, so the enemy is outside them. It beats T1 because its payoff hasn't been posted.
2. **T1 rewritten.** It stops more strangers and passes the pub test, but the payoff has to be new (the fuel mix by pace on a long run, not "carbs run out at 32K"). It beats T5 because it ends in something to use.
3. **T5 rewritten.** It has the widest pull of the nine (parkrunner, footballer, non-runner), but the use is the thinnest and the claim the shakiest.
