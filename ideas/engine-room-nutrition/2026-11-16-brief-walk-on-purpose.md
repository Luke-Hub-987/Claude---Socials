## Line one (myth, kill, proof, Luke's words)
"Walking in a marathon feels like failing, but in ultras I walk on purpose, and
when researchers had 4-hour marathoners walk a minute every 2.5K, they finished
in a similar time with less pain in their legs."
**Gap test:** a stranger asks "so should I walk, and when?" Answered by the
method, not the title.

## The method (P127)
1. **The study, through one runner:** run 2.5K, walk a minute, from the start,
   before you're tired.
2. **How to walk:** never stop dead; to the side, fast, arms bent "like you're
   late for a train" (ours, 27 Oct); back to running before the minute feels
   finished.
3. **Where:** at the same marker every time, so it's a plan, not a decision.
   Take the gel on the walk nearest its time on the clock.
4. **The honest cost, and the version for a tight goal:** the walkers were
   about 7 minutes slower on average. Chasing sub-4 by a few minutes? Walk only
   the drink stations, 15 to 20 seconds each (the 4:30 reel).
**Repeat line (ours, 27 Oct):** "A walk you pick lasts a minute. A walk your
legs pick lasts a K."
**Pinned comment:** "Have you ever walked in a marathon? Yes or no."
**Follow, identity last:** a new line from the gate chat.
