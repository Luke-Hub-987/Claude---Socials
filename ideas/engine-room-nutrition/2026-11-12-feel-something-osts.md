**What all five share, and our 10% title didn't:**
1. **The viewer is in it, becoming or losing something:** nerves, ambition,
   the arc you're on, what you'll go through. Ours was about a study.
2. **One feeling word that's physical or about identity:** STEEL, BRUTAL,
   OBSESSIVE, RARE. Ours (FAILED, TRIAL) are the words of a report.
3. **Huge type in the top band, high contrast, full width.** You read it before
   you've decided to watch. Ours was half the size, mid-frame, blue on blue.
4. **A row of three photos above the title** (three of the five). They're famous
   faces (Bond, Ronaldo, Jobs, Kobe, Picasso) that you recognise in a quarter of a
   second. **Not ours to copy:** unlicensed famous faces are off the list, and our
   own collages lost every time (ledger row 5). Without a face everyone knows,
   it's a busy first second (row 22). We take the type size and the words, not
   the photo row.

## The title spec (frame one, from today)
- The title goes in the top band, full width less the 16px edges, in 2 lines of
  22 characters or fewer. A third, smaller line is allowed only for a bracketed
  tag (a gate question: G10 counts 2 lines).
- Black bold on a white box, or white bold on dark. Never a coloured box over
  clothes or a background of the same colour.
- About twice the size of the 10% title: each line about 4% of the screen's
  height, the size in "3 Habits To Create Nerves Of Steel".
- Luke and the title only until stage 1. Luke sends a screenshot of 0:00 at
  phone size before posting.
