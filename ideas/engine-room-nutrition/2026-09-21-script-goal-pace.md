## ⚑ The science, checked

Luke supplied this. The structure is sound. Five things changed on the
way in.

**1. "Doubling your half and adding ten is a lie" was softened** to
"quietly assumes something about you that is usually not true". It is
not a lie, it is a formula that works reasonably for high-mileage
runners and badly for everyone else, because it assumes the durability
is already there. **That is also the better hook**, because an
unnamed assumption is an open loop and "it is a lie" is a closed one.
It also avoids the banned construction.

**2. No jargon spoken** (P81). Cardiac drift, decoupling, lactate
threshold, ventilatory threshold, fat oxidation, glycogen, zone 4 and
2:2 breathing are all in the source and **none of them are said out
loud**. The mechanisms stayed, the names went.

**3. "Burning carbohydrates twice as fast as your gut can replace
them" was cut.** False precision on a number nobody can check, which
is the arithmetic failure.

**4. "Your legs will structurally fail" became "your legs give out
before your lungs do."** Same meaning, no overclaim.

**5. The 22km and 120-minute protocols became one shape**, because two
protocols split by finish time is arithmetic on screen (P62). The card
carries the proportions and the audio says "about two hours".

**Flags to resolve before filming:**

- **The 5% figure** ⚑. This is a field heuristic off a watch, not a
  lab measure, and it depends on heat, terrain and how hard the start
  was. **Say "about five percent" and never a decimal.** This is the
  exact shape of the cardiac-drift failure, so keep it loose.
- **"Ten to fifteen seconds a kilometre"** ⚑ is a sensible
  adjustment, not a measured constant. Keep "ten to fifteen".
- **"About two hours with an hour at goal pace"** ⚑ depends on the
  plan and the runner. The card carries proportions rather than a
  prescription for everyone.
- **60 to 80 grams an hour** ⚑. Now consistent across this, the gels
  script and the long run script. **Pick this range and use it
  everywhere.**
- **He should have done a version of this run himself**, or say
  plainly that he is doing it on the 4th. Do not present it as
  something he has done if he has not (standing rule).
- **The failed-batch count** ⚑[N].
