## What the course actually does (from the sources)
| K | What's there | What it does to you |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 10 | Wide, flat avenues out towards the sea; crowded start | Too fast, too early, because it feels free |
| 29 to 31 | The old town: twisty streets, the biggest crowds | You surge with the noise |
| 30 to 34 | A gentle rise, about 1% at most, most noticeable 30 to 32 | Barely visible, but you feel it on tired legs |
| 34 to 38.5 | A 2K-plus straight with far fewer people | The mental low: no noise, no landmarks, legs going |
| 38.5 to finish | Crowds back along the last stretch; a ramp down onto the blue finish | The payoff, if you've anything left |
Flat overall (about 30 to 75m of climb across the whole race, sources
differ). Water and sports drink at stations about every 5K.
