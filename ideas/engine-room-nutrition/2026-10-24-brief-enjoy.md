## The method in numbers
1. **0-10K, let them go:** if the start feels exciting, you're going too
   fast. The check: all 10 words of "I am running a marathon today and I
   feel good" in one breath, normal voice. Let them go; you'll see a lot
   of them again later.
2. **10-20K, head up:** the best you'll feel all day. Look at your watch
   once a K, at the K sign, and nowhere else. Between signs, read 1 sign
   and high-five 1 kid. Gels on the clock as always: 20 and 50 past.
3. **20-30K, your name:** the night before, write your first name on the
   front of your top in black marker, letters as tall as your hand. When it
   goes quiet in your head, strangers shout it. Wave back, and that's
   another K done.
4. **32K, your people (the payoff):** the text, sent the night before:
   stand at 32K, not the finish, which side of the road, and the colour of
   your top. The finish is already packed with people cheering; 32K is
   where it goes quiet and your legs go. From 30K you run to them, one K
   at a time. **If nobody's coming,** your name on your top does the same
   job.
