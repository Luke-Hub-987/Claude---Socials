## ⚑ Before filming

1. **"Peaks between 24 and 48 hours"** ⚑. Well established for the
   delayed soreness after unaccustomed or long efforts. Say it as a
   range, never a single figure.
2. **"The repair arrives a day late"** ⚑ is the plain-speech version
   of the mechanism. **Do not say the name of it out loud** (P81).
   Jargon is not the mechanism.
3. **"Blood moving carries the repair in"** ⚑, and the honesty line
   after it is deliberate. **The evidence that easy movement speeds
   actual recovery is genuinely mixed**, and saying so out loud is
   worth more than the claim would be. It is the same rule that kept
   us off a hard anabolic window yesterday.
4. **"You do not have many quality sessions left"** is deliberately
   vague, because the real number depends on his plan. **Do not put a
   number on it.**
5. **The sharp, one spot, one leg line** is the boundary. It stays in,
   it stays early in beat three, and it never becomes medical advice.
6. **The failed-batch count** ⚑[N].
