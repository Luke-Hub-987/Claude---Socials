Race day, in order
Six moments. Which is yours?
1
8:45, the start pen
Your mouth's dry and you're forcing down a gel you've never had before. Your stomach turns over. The fix: pick your race breakfast now and have it three hours before every long run, so race morning feels like a Sunday.
 
2
3K, it all feels easy
The crowd's loud, your watch says 20 seconds a K faster than plan, and you feel brilliant. That's where the wall starts. The fix: run the first 10K 5 to 10 seconds a K slower than goal pace, even when it feels too easy.
 
3
12K, a minute down
You check your watch, see you're behind, and speed up to win it back. A runner who replied after Dublin did exactly this and finished five minutes over goal. The fix: hold your band. Up to a minute behind at halfway is the plan.
 
4
25K, no gel since 10K
You didn't feel like one, so you skipped it. You'll pay for it at 32K. The fix: first gel at 20 minutes, then a 30g gel every 30 minutes, about 60g an hour, before you feel you need it.
 
5
32K, the wall
Your legs go heavy and the next K looks like a mile. The fix starts back at 3K. On the day: shorter, quicker steps, and one K at a time, or one lamppost at a time.
 
6
35K, the calf twitch
A flicker in your calf, then another. The fix: ease off and shorten your stride straight away. If it locks, stop, keep the leg straight, push your heel down, walk for a minute, then run again easier.
 
Which one's yours?
