## Content and checks

Countdown, one job a day (v2, 22 Oct): Thursday lay out kit; Friday
start the carb load; Saturday keep loading and eat dinner at 5-6 pm;
Sunday rehearsed breakfast 3 hours out, then the race rules.

From the videos: kit as on the last long run and number pinned; carb load
starts two days out; dinner 5-6 pm, white rice, pasta or potatoes, plain
sauce, little fat and fibre; same breakfast, same amount, 3 hours before;
5-10 s/K slower first 10K; gel every 20 min from minute 20 with water;
shorter, quicker steps. New, for Luke to approve:

- **New claim for Luke to approve:** "8 to 10 g of carbohydrate per kilo
  a day" for the two days (70 kg: 560 to 700 g). The sports-nutrition
  guideline (ACSM, IOC) for a 36-48 hour carb load is 10 to 12 g/kg; 8 to
  10 is a lower, easier target many runners actually hit. Change or cut
  it before sending if Luke disagrees.
- A carb snack every two to three hours on load days (new).
- Low fibre and fat on Saturday as well as at dinner (the video said it
  for the dinner only).
- "Check the grams on the packets" (new, practical).
- Commitment in the copy: Sunday racers who reply by Friday evening get
  their plan before race day; later races "after the weekend". Luke to
  confirm he can keep both.
- P118 audit done: the Friday cutoff appears once in the hero and once in
  the reply card; no triads, fragments, cliches or curly quotes.

## What a professional would add (Luke's 22 Oct brief)

1. Things not asked for: a hard reply cutoff tied to race day; a
   countdown with one action per day; tagging every reply with race and
   goal time in Klaviyo for later segmentation; a seed test before the
   full send; a post-race "how did it go?" reply email planned for
   Monday.
2. Rawest material: the replies and DMs themselves (races, goal times,
   the words runners use), the Klaviyo profile export with sign-up dates,
   opens by email provider, and the video comments asking questions.
3. Methods and tools: Klaviyo segments and exclusions; subject lines
   taken from the proven on-screen-text shapes; Gmail "Show original" for
   SPF/DKIM/DMARC PASS; a seed list across Gmail, Outlook and iCloud;
   Google Postmaster Tools; the P35/P118 writing audit; replies as the
   A/B metric.
4. What shows it worked, independent of us: replies per wave against the
   16 Oct campaign; spam complaints under 0.1%; inbox placement on the
   seed accounts; DMARC aggregate reports showing PASS; runners reporting
   their race after Sunday.
