Before your first Monday
Three things to sort now
1
Know the pace you're training for
Your goal time sets every session. For a 4:00 marathon it's 5:45 to 5:50 a K for the first 10K, then 5:35 to 5:40 (9:00 to 9:05 a mile). Find yours in the plan builder.
 
2
Take your race fuel on every long run
About 60g of carbs an hour: first gel at 20 minutes, then a 30g gel every 30 minutes, each with 3 or 4 sips of water. Your stomach gets used to it over about two weeks, so start in week one.
 
3
Pick your race breakfast now
Two white bagels with jam and a glass of juice, three hours before you run. Have it before every long run and by April it's routine. If you have diabetes or a medical condition, check with your doctor first.
