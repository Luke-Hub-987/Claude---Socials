Four rules for your marathon
1
Start slower than your goal pace
Run the first 10K 5 to 10 seconds per K slower than the pace your goal time needs. You will be up to a minute behind at halfway, and that is your race going to plan.
 
2
Take a gel every 20 minutes
Take your first at minute 20, before you feel you need it, and every 20 minutes after that. Take each one with water.
 
3
Check your effort with ten words
Say “I am running a marathon today and I feel good”. At 10K you should get all ten words out in one breath. By 20K, ten words but breathy. By 30K, about five. By 40K, two or three.
 
4
When it hurts, shorten your steps
Take shorter, quicker steps. Then run one K at a time, or one lamppost at a time.
