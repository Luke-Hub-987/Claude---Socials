```

The wall you hit at 32K gets built in the first 10K. You feel great, the crowd's loud, and you run 10 or 15 seconds a K too fast. You pay for it two hours later.

Reply with your race and goal time and I'll send your exact numbers.

YOUR FIRST 10K PACE (trust the clock at the 10K sign; your watch reads about 1% long)
3:30: 5:05-5:10 per km, 8:10-8:20 per mile, 10K sign 50:50-51:40
3:45: 5:25-5:30 per km, 8:45-8:50 per mile, 10K sign 54:10-55:00
4:00: 5:45-5:50 per km, 9:15-9:25 per mile, 10K sign 57:30-58:20

3. First gel at 20 minutes, before you feel you need it, with 3 or 4 sips of water just before a station.
```
