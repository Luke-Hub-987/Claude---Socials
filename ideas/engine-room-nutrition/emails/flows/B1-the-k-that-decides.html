The wall you hit at 32K gets built in the first 10K. You feel great, the crowd's loud, and you run 10 or 15 seconds a K too fast. You pay for it two hours later.

So run the first 10K 5 to 10 seconds a K slower than your goal pace. You'll be up to a minute behind at halfway, and that's your race going to plan.

How to hold it
Three checks in the first 10K
1
Ten words, one breath
Say "I am running a marathon today and I feel good". At 10K you should get all ten words out in one breath.
 
2
The sign, not the watch
Check the race clock as you pass the 10K sign. Within the range above means you're on plan.
 
3
First gel at 20 minutes
Before you feel you need it, with 3 or 4 sips of water just before a station.
