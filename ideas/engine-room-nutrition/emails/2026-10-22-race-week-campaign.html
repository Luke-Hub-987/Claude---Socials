Thursday to Sunday
What to do each day
1
Thursday: lay out your kit
Lay out everything you will race in, the same shoes, socks and kit you wore on your last long run, and pin your number on. If you run today, keep it short and easy, and eat your normal meals.
 
2
Friday: start your carb load
Your carb load starts on Friday, two days before the race. Make carbs the base of every meal and have a carb snack every two to three hours. Aim for about 8 to 10 grams of carbohydrate for every kilo you weigh: for a 70 kg runner that is 560 to 700 grams. Check the grams on the packets, because it is easy to fall short.
 
3
Saturday: keep loading, eat dinner early
Eat as much carbohydrate as on Friday, with less fibre and fat so it sits easy. Have dinner around five or six o'clock so it has gone down before you sleep. White rice, white pasta or peeled potatoes, with a plain tomato sauce or a light broth and a small bit of chicken if you want it. Leave the salad, the cream, the cheese and anything fried until after the race.
 
4
Sunday: the breakfast you rehearsed
Eat the breakfast you tested before your last long run, the same amount, about three hours before the start. In the race, run the first 10K 5 to 10 seconds per K slower than goal pace, take a gel every 20 minutes from minute 20 with water, and when it hurts, take shorter, quicker steps.
