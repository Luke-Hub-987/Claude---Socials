Tonight and tomorrow
From now to the finish
1
This afternoon: keep loading
Keep carbs at every meal and a carb snack every two to three hours, as you did yesterday, with little fibre or fat so it all sits easy. Check your kit is laid out and your number is pinned on.
 
2
Dinner at five or six
Eat early so it has gone down before you sleep. White rice, white pasta or peeled potatoes, with a plain tomato sauce or a light broth and a small bit of chicken if you want it. Leave the salad, the cream, the cheese and anything fried until after the race.
 
3
Tomorrow morning: the breakfast you rehearsed
Eat the breakfast you tested before your last long run, the same amount, about three hours before your start. Set the alarm tonight with that in mind.
 
4
In the race: the four rules
Run the first 10K at the first pace in the table. Take a gel every 20 minutes from minute 20, each with water. Check your effort with "I am running a marathon today and I feel good": all ten words in one breath at 10K, ten breathy words at 20K, about five at 30K, and two or three at 40K. When it hurts, take shorter, quicker steps and run one K, or one lamppost, at a time.
