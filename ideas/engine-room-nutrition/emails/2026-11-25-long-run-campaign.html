From this week to race day
How to build it
1
This week: 4 x 10
Forty minutes. The easy block should let you say a full sentence without gasping. The last block will feel hard on tired legs, and that's the bit a steady long run never gives you.
 
2
Then 4 x 15, then 4 x 20
When 4 x 10 feels easy, add five minutes to every block and keep the same paces. The 4 x 20 is 80 minutes: a proper long run that finishes faster than race pace.
 
3
Fuel it from the start
On any run over an hour, take your first gel 20 minutes in and one every 30, each with a few sips of water. Almgren drank carbs from the first minute, because in Maurten's testing it took about an hour before his drink was really counting.
