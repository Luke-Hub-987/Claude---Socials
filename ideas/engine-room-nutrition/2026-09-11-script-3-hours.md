# What to do in the 3 hours before a marathon

## Why this one, out of the twelve

**It inherits the query family of the only video either account has
that keeps earning.** Someone who searches "last 30 minutes before a
marathon" searches this too, and the two videos point at each other,
which turns one evergreen asset into two.

Same event window, same high intent, same proven walkthrough shape, and
it ends exactly where the last video starts, so the series architecture
is already built.

**On the fatigue rule (P72).** A straight repeat of the same device
back to back would normally be wrong. Two things make it the right call
here: the race window closes in a few weeks, and **85.5% of this
account's reach is non-followers from the Reels tab and Explore**, so
the fatigue risk lands on a following that is mostly not seeing these
back to back anyway. The device is still varied: this is an **hour by
hour countdown**, not three named steps.
