## How to post an Instagram trial reel
1. New reel from the ERN account, upload, edit as normal.
2. On the caption screen, switch **Trial** on.
3. Turn auto-share **off**.
4. No collaborator (trials can't have one, as far as we know).
5. Share. It shows in the Reels tab with a "Trial" label only you see.

## How to read them (24 hours, then 72)
- Screenshot: views, skip rate, average watch, shares, saves, comments.
- **Against T1, the control:** a test that gets T1's views or more, and a
  lower skip, goes to the profile (share it from the trial). One that
  doesn't stays a trial.
- T2 and T3: count MATCH comments. 10 or more on either = build the
  match-day series.
- Never a cause from one test (P174): we're looking for what repeats.
