# How to get out of the marathon wall

## OST

> **How to get out of the marathon wall**

On-screen markers only, as each stage starts: **THE FIRST SIGN /
MINUTE ONE / THE NEXT TEN / THE WAY BACK.**

## The secret, and the counter-position

**The standard advice at the wall is to dig deep. That is the worst
thing you can do.** The wall is the fuel running out, and effort does
not make fuel. The fastest way out is to slow down and let sugar
arrive. Every beat opens on the unexpected version of that (P94).

*Hook. Gate, the secret, the counter-position (HF13, P94):* "If you are

*Super hook, pain twisted, then dream outcome:* "Push through it and the

yourself moving again."

**[ONE. THE FIRST SIGN. The save prompt]**
"Save this, it is four steps.

The first sign is your pace slipping while the effort goes up, heavy
legs, and every thought turning dark ⚑. That is the moment to act,
before you are walking."

**[TWO. MINUTE ONE]**
"Minute one, get sugar in, and make it liquid. A gel with water or a
sports drink, and swish it round your mouth for a few seconds before
you swallow ⚑, because your mouth can sense it before your legs ever
get it. Solid food belongs in the first half. Now it is liquid only,
and never three gels at once, because your stomach has almost no blood
left to deal with them."

**[THREE. THE NEXT TEN]**
"The next ten minutes, slow down, around fifteen or twenty seconds a K
⚑. It feels backwards, and it is the fastest way out, because it gives
the sugar time to arrive. Shorten your stride and quicken your feet, so
your legs take less of a hammering."

**[REHOOK]**
"And the last step is the one that gets you home."

**[FOUR. THE WAY BACK]**
"If you have used a caffeine gel in training, take it now ⚑. Caffeine
makes the same effort feel smaller, and it takes a while to work, so
it is for the last few K. Then build back on effort, one K at a time."

## ⚑ Luke's science, checked

**Kept:**

| From the science | In the script as |
|---|---|
| Digging deep and slamming three gels are the conventional mistakes | The counter-position in the hook, and "never three gels at once" |
| A blood-starved gut cannot handle a big load | "Your stomach has almost no blood left to deal with them", consistent with four earlier videos |
| Liquid, not solid, at the wall | "Solid food belongs in the first half. Now it is liquid only." This is also the honest place for the bar across the whole account |
| Swish before swallowing | "Swish it round your mouth for a few seconds" |
| Slow down about 15 to 20 seconds a K for ten minutes | Kept as a rule of thumb ⚑ |
| Shorter, quicker steps | Same wording as the wall video, so the two agree |
| Caffeine, if tested | Kept, with the timing corrected (below) |

**Cut or changed, and why:**

1. **The Des Linden example is cut, and this one matters.** Linden did
   win Boston in 2018, in brutal conditions. The account of her
   "dropping pace, taking only liquid carbs, adjusting her cadence and
   letting her nervous system stabilise" is **not something she
   described.** What she has said publicly is that she felt so bad early
   in that race she considered dropping out, which is a different
   story and not a glycogen wall. Attributing a made-up protocol to a
   named athlete is the most serious kind of error this account can
   make. **Out entirely.**
2. **"Your central nervous system pulling the emergency handbrake" and
   "the brain cuts signals to your legs."** The Central Governor is a
   contested theory, cut from the wall video for the same reason, and
   "the wall is not a mental flaw, it is..." is the banned construction.
   Replaced with the plain fact: **"The wall is your fuel running
   out."**
3. **Mouth rinsing "sends immediate signals to your motor cortex,
   tricking your nervous system into lifting the brake."** The
   research is real: carbohydrate sensed in the mouth lights up reward
   and motor areas of the brain and can improve performance before any
   is absorbed. **The evidence is strongest in hard efforts of about an
   hour, often after fasting**, not at 32K of a marathon. So it is
   "your mouth **can** sense it", and swishing costs nothing because
   they swallow it anyway.
4. **"Guarantees you will be forced to walk by kilometre 36."**
   Overclaim. Out.
5. **"Increase cadence by 5 to 10 steps per minute."** Kept as
   "shorten your stride and quicken your feet", matching the wall video,
   without a number nobody can hold mid-race.
6. **Caffeine "the moment you feel the wall" and "50 to 100mg".**
   Caffeine takes a while to kick in, so taken at the wall it helps the
   last few K rather than the next one. The script says exactly that.
   **No milligrams** out loud, and only if tested in training.
   "Blocks adenosine receptors" is jargon (P81). "It does not replace
   glycogen, but..." is the banned construction, so it became "caffeine
   makes the same effort feel smaller."
7. **"Liver cannot maintain blood glucose."** Directionally fine and
   not needed. Out for length.

**Standing rules:** he has not hit the wall himself, so no personal
claim. "Dense bars sit like lead" is not used: ERN sells a bar, and the
honest line is about **when** solid food works, not whether it does.
