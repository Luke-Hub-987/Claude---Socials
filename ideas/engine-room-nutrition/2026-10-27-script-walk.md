## Script (311 words, 1,518 characters, about 1:34)

If you've got a marathon coming up, walking for a minute at 32K only costs you about 30 seconds. It's getting going again that costs you the race.

You know that long run where you stopped to walk for a second, and a second turned into 5 minutes? Do that at 32K and your goal time's gone.

Or you walk a minute, run the next K, and you're passing people at 40K.

And yes, walking's allowed. But there's one thing to do in the last 10 seconds of the walk that decides whether you run again.

**[2 WORDS: WALK BEFORE YOU HAVE TO]**
From 30K, keep checking how many words you can get out in one breath. When it's down to 2 or 3, walk on purpose at the next water station. A walk you pick lasts a minute. A walk your legs pick lasts a K.

Want your whole race planned like this? Email us your race and goal time through the link in the bio, it's free. Right, the walk.

**[THE FIRST 10 SECONDS]**
Don't stop dead. Move to the side of the road, have a quick look behind you, and walk fast straight away. Arms bent, like you're late for a train.

**[THE MINUTE]**
Walk for 1 minute on your watch, and use it. If a gel's due, take it now, with 3 or 4 sips of water. Save this, and send it to whoever you do your long runs with.

Now the last 10 seconds, the bit most people get wrong.

**[RUNNING AGAIN]**
Start running at 50 seconds, before the walk feels finished. Wait until you feel ready and you never will. Make your first 10 steps tiny, about half your normal step, then just run to the next lamppost. If you need to walk again, run a K, then walk a minute. Do that for the last 10K and all 10 walks cost you about the same as walking 1 K.

## Caption (final)
What to do when you have to walk in a marathon. Walking for a minute at 32K only costs you about 30 seconds. It's getting going again that costs you the race.

From 30K, keep checking how many words you can get out in one breath. When it's down to 2 or 3, walk on purpose at the next water station. A walk you pick lasts a minute; a walk your legs pick lasts a K.

When you walk, don't stop dead. Move to the side, look behind you, and walk fast straight away. Walk for 1 minute on your watch, and if a gel's due, take it then with 3 or 4 sips of water.

Start running again at 50 seconds, before the walk feels finished. Make your first 10 steps tiny, then run to the next lamppost. If you need to walk again, run a K, then walk a minute.

What a 1-minute walk costs you, at a fast walk: about 30 seconds if you're going for 3:30, 3:45 or 4:00 (about 8:00 to 9:10 a mile), about 25 seconds for 4:30 (10:18 a mile) and about 20 seconds for 5:00 (11:27 a mile). Walking a whole K costs about 5 minutes at 3:30 to 4:00, about 4 at 4:30 and about 3 and a half at 5:00. Ten 1-minute walks over the last 10K cost about the same as walking a single K.

Send this to whoever you do your long runs with. Want your whole race planned like this? Email us your race and goal time through the link in the bio and we'll send it, free.
