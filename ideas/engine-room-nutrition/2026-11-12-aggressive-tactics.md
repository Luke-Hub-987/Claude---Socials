# 5 AGGRESSIVE MARATHON / TACTICS FIRST-TIMERS / SHOULD NEVER TRY

**OST:** 5 AGGRESSIVE MARATHON / TACTICS FIRST-TIMERS / SHOULD NEVER TRY (21 / 20 / 16),
AGGRESSIVE in red.

## Why it stops people
- **It's a warning, and warnings make people lean in** (Luke's pack: "Be warned,
  doing this will put a massive strain on your nervous system"; "Promise me you
  won't use this maliciously"). Forbidden is more interesting than recommended.
- **Everyone who's run one marathon promotes themselves:** "I'm not a
  first-timer, so these are for me." First-timers watch because they've been
  told not to.
- **AGGRESSIVE is the pulse word,** and it's the opposite of everything we've
  said ("start slow", "hold back"). That's the surprise.
- **It's a numbered list of named tactics:** the save shape (saves at 30% to 64%
  of likes in Luke's pack).
