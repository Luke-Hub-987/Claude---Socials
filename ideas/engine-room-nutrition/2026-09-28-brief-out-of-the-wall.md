## Why this beats "When your marathon fuel actually kicks in"

Luke asked whether the fuel title was really the best. **It is good,
and it is not the best.** Audited honestly:

| Test | "When your marathon fuel actually kicks in" | "How to get out of the marathon wall" |
|---|---|---|
| **The secret (Thiel)** | **Weak.** "Fuel early" is advice most runners have already heard | **Strong.** Most runners believe that once you hit the wall, the race is over |
| **TAM (Briar)** | **Narrower.** Fuelling is a subtopic | **The widest term in the sport.** "The wall" is the phrase the outlier rode |
| **"My mother" test (Galloway CCN)** | Borderline | Passes, same as the outlier |

**The fuel video threw away the outlier's most valuable asset**, the
phrase "the wall". Its best fact is kept and moved in here (below).

## OST

- **Eight words, 35 characters.**
- **The secret is the premise.** "Get out of" quietly says it can be

## The staged structure, same package as the outlier

The timeline is **time since it hits**, with simple markers on screen,

| Marker | Opens on the unexpected (Thiel) | What to do |

and for most people it is only the start of the worst ten minutes of

**Where the fuel video's best fact went:** "the fuel you take now works
later than you think" becomes the reason for THE NEXT TEN: you slow down
**so the fuel has time to arrive**.

## ⚑ Before scripting, and it needs Luke's science

1. **"The sugar can help before it reaches your legs."** This rests on
   carbohydrate mouth-rinse research: the mouth senses carbohydrate and
   performance can improve before any is absorbed. **The strongest
   evidence is in shorter, hard efforts of around an hour**, not the
   thirtieth kilometre of a marathon. Say "can help", never "will".
   It is the most Thiel-shaped fact in the video and also the one most
   in need of a source.
2. **"You can get out of it."** Directionally sound: carbohydrate taken
   while depleted helps, and a few slower minutes to let it land is
   standard advice. **Never promise the old pace comes back.** "Get
   back moving" is the honest version.
3. **Walking a station** is common practice, not a proven time saver.
   Frame it as an option.
4. **"It starts in your head"**: the mood and effort changes are
   widely reported. Keep it descriptive and away from the Central
   Governor, which is a contested theory (cut from the last script).
5. **No grams per hour** until one range is chosen for the account.
6. **He has not hit the wall himself** (standing rule). No personal
   claim. The video is about what to do, not what he did.
