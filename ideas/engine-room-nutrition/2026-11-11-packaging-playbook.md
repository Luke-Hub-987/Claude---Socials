# The packaging playbook

## Why these stop people (one line)
Every winner picks a fight with something the viewer believes, does or admires,
in a word that has a pulse, pinned to one exact thing. **Enemy + pulse word +
exact thing.** The losers have none of the three ("How to analyze your content
data", "Feeling slow asf at Parkrun", "How to be young and rich").

## The exact thing (specificity)
Every winner pins the promise: "First 10K Video", "~20% more views", "in 90 Days",
"3 Hours a Week", "every 30 seconds", "1 min 59 secs". Ours: a minute (the 70th,
the last 20), a K (10K, 30K, 31K), a goal time (sub-4), a count of runs (4 a
week), a study size (5,200 runners).

## The rules
1. **Enemy + pulse word + exact thing**, in 2 lines of 22 characters or fewer.
2. **Point at the answer, don't deliver it** (Briar graded "Fixed watering
   schedules are killing your garden" a B+ for closing the loop).
3. **One pulse device a post, a different one each time** (row 16: a device
   repeated becomes a template).
4. **True or it doesn't go up.** The intensifier is on the promise, the loss word
   only where the evidence holds, numbers from FACTS or a vetted source.
5. **Not ours:** swearing (brand), "hack" and "cheat" (C17), "maxxing", unlicensed
   famous faces, "rubbish" and "lie". "Like a 5th grader" waits on Luke (C17).
