**Markers:** THE FIRST 20 MINUTES / THE MIDDLE / THE LAST 20 MINUTES

"If you're training for a marathon, your voice knows your long run is going wrong before your watch does."

"Get it wrong and you're walking the last 2K home with your watch still running and nothing left in your legs."

"Get it right and you run the last K past your own front door, feeling like you could go again."

"We call it the 10-word test, and the number you're listening for comes near the end, so save this."

**[THE FIRST 20 MINUTES]**

"Say the race-day sentence out loud, even if you feel daft: I am running a marathon today and I feel good. That's 10 words. For the first 20 minutes, all 10 should come out in one breath. If they don't, slow down until they do, even if your watch says you're crawling."

"If you want a plan built for your next race, email us your race and goal time through the link in the bio."

**[THE MIDDLE]**

"If the run's longer than 90 minutes, take a 30 gram gel with a few sips of water at 20 minutes and another every 30, the same as race day, and say the sentence again every time you take one. If the 10 words come out a bit breathy now, that's fine. That's the run doing its job, so hold the pace and leave the watch alone."

"And the last 20 minutes is where you hear the number that matters."

**[THE LAST 20 MINUTES]**

"5 words in one breath is where you should be between 20 and 30K on race day. So if you're down to 5 before the last 20 minutes of a long run, slow down until the 10 come back, and you'll run that last K past your front door instead of walking it."

"Send this to the mate who always goes off too fast on the long run."
