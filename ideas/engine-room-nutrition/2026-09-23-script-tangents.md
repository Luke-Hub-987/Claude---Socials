where the kilometre comes from."

## ⚑ Before filming

1. **"42.8, sometimes over 43."** This is the honest range for a big
   city marathon and the round 43 in the OST is shorthand. **Ground it
   in the first ten seconds** exactly as written so the title is not
   left overstating.
2. **The shortest possible route** ⚑. Courses are measured along the
   shortest route a runner could take, and measurers add a small
   margin so a course is never short. Both are real. **Do not name the
   governing body, do not quote the percentage**, and say "a small
   margin" rather than a figure.
3. **"Dozens of turns"** ⚑ rather than a number for Dublin, unless he
   counts them off the official course map.
4. **The 3 minutes** ⚑ is checkable: 400 to 600 metres at around 5:41
   a kilometre is 2.3 to 3.4 minutes, so three is the conservative
   middle. Say "about three minutes".
5. **The GPS line stays in.** It costs nothing and it is the
   difference between a tip and something true. Leaving it out invites
   the comment that undoes the whole video.
6. **No forced bar mention.** There is no honest fuel angle in a
   tactics video, and bolting one on is the P57 failure running
   backwards. The on-screen card and the CTA carry the brand, and that
   is enough for one video.
7. **The failed-batch count** ⚑[N].
