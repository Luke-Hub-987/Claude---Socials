# Four topics, scored against everything

## 1. How to actually enjoy your marathon (Luke's) — **CREATIVE PICK**

> **How to actually enjoy**
> **your marathon**

**Markers (distance plus a rule, P111):** 0-10K: MAKE IT BORING /
10-20K: LOOK UP AND EAT / 20-30K: ONE BLOCK AT A TIME / 30K-FINISH:
NOW IT GETS GOOD

**The secret (Thiel):** the way to enjoy a marathon is to make the
first ten K almost boring. Everyone thinks it is meant to hurt, and that
the fun is the start. The fun is the last ten K, if you earned it.

**Hook stack (P106):**
- Gate: "If you are running a marathon in the next few weeks,"
- Secret, open loop, "you": "the way to actually enjoy it is to make the
  first ten K almost boring."
- Pain, the race outcome: "Go out on the excitement and you spend the
  last ten K hanging on, hating every step of the race you trained
  months for."
- Dream, a picture: "Hold it back and the last ten K is where you start
  passing people, and it becomes the best part of the day."

| Stage | Opens on the unexpected | Action, nothing to carry |
|---|---|---|
| 0-10K MAKE IT BORING | If the start feels exciting, you are going too fast | Five to ten seconds a K slower than goal, let people go (matches first half) |
| 10-20K LOOK UP AND EAT | This is the best you will feel all day, so enjoy it on purpose | Look up, read the signs, high-five the kids, and take your gel at every twenty minutes (matches the wall) |
| 20-30K ONE BLOCK AT A TIME | Nobody enjoys 22 K to go | Race to the next 5K marker or water station only |
| 30K-FINISH NOW IT GETS GOOD | If you held back, this is where it turns fun | Same effort, shorter steps, count the people you pass |

**Merges the "How pros actually race a marathon" brief**: same clock,
same rules, and Luke's title is broader (every runner wants to enjoy it,
first-timers most of all) with no claims about pros to defend. The pros
brief is retired.

## 2. What to do when you want to quit a marathon — creative, next after 1

> **What to do when you want**
> **to quit a marathon**

**Markers:** THE FIRST THOUGHT / THE NEXT TWO MINUTES / THE NEXT LAMPPOST / THE LAST 2K

**The secret:** motivation does not fix it; making the goal smaller
does (Luke's lamppost rule, on ERN as a technique, not his story, P96).
Often the urge to stop arrives with the sugar running low, so the first
move is a gel, not a pep talk.

**Hook:** "If you are running a marathon in the next few weeks, there
will be a moment you want to stop, and digging deep is the worst way to
get through it." Pain: walking the last ten K of the race you trained
months for. Dream: running across the line.

## 3. How to run the second half of a marathon faster than the first — safe

> **How to run the second half of a marathon**

**Markers:** HALFWAY / 25K / 30K / 35K TO THE LINE

**The secret:** a faster second half is decided by doing less in the
