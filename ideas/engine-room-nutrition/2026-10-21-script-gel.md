## Script v2 (336 words, about 1:40)
If you're running a marathon in the next few weeks, you can take the same gel at 40K that you took at 10K and actually want it, and that's your race going to plan.

You're at 30K, you tear open the next gel, and the sweet, sticky smell alone stops you dead. It goes back in your pocket, and 5K later your legs are gone. Set it up right, and that 30K gel goes in like the first one of the day, and you're passing people over the last 5K.

What decides that 30K gel happens before halfway. Save this.

The night before, cut a bar into small chunks and put them in a sandwich bag with a handful of jelly sweets. Your first fuel, at about 30 minutes, is half that bar, chewed slowly with a few sips of water. Solid food is easiest early on.

Want your whole race planned like this? Email us your race and goal time through the link in the bio and we'll send you your plan, free. Now, the next 10K.

From 10K to halfway, swap every other gel for 4 or 5 jelly sweets. That's about the same sugar as a gel, in a different taste and a different texture.

From halfway it's gels only, because late on, anything you have to chew is hard work. Take each one about 100 metres before a water station. Swill the first sip of water round and spit it out, then take 3 good sips. Never wash a gel down with sports drink.

So why does a bar at 10K let you face a gel at 30K?

Your body gets sick of a taste it keeps getting, long before it's had enough sugar. Gel after gel from the start, and by 30K that sticky sweetness has been stacking up for 2 hours. Break it up with the bar and the jellies, clear it with that first sip, and the gel at 40K goes in like the one at 10K.

## Caption (final)

The night before, cut a bar into small chunks and put them in a sandwich bag with a handful of jelly sweets. At about 30 minutes, have half the bar (about 20g of carbs), chewed slowly with a few sips of water. Solid food is easiest early on.

Still can't face one? Skip that one, never two, sip plain water for 10 minutes, then take the next. Use only foods you've already had on a long run.
