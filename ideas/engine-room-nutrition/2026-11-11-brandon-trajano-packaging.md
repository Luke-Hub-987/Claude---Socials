# Brandon Trajano: selling the becoming, not the event

## Why it's year-round and ours isn't
Our winners were about a race day (the wall, 7 days before, carb load), so the
calendar caps us between seasons. Brandon sells **who you become** (athletic,
fast, harder to kill), and people want that every month. Briar's clients sell
money and views, which are also wanted every month. Our version: the runner
who's still strong at 30K, on a busy person's week.

### The named series (follows: 0 on the last 5 posts)
A small subtitle on every year-round post, for example "ENGINE ROOM 101", with
the part number. A series gives a reason to follow. Not in the first 3 seconds
if it clutters the frame (row 22): from 0:03, or on the cover.

## What we don't copy
- Swearing ("SO FU*KING"): "DISGUSTINGLY" carries it without the brand risk.
- "Maxxing": his audience's slang, not an Irish or UK runner's.
