## The Ogilvy test (what every one of the 30 had to pass)
1. **Specific:** a number, a distance or a named thing in the title (42.6K, 2:07, 35K, 4 pints, 3 things). The broad ones (how to run faster) are out.
2. **Self-interest:** every curious question ends in something they use on their next run. TED-Ed can stop at the itch; we can't.
3. **Believable:** every number is in FACTS or has a named study waiting to be read. Anything that can't be sourced dies before the script.
4. **News where there is some:** the boom (#16 to #18), the newer studies (#6, #10).
5. **No blind headline:** the title says what it's about. "The secret the pros won't tell you" fails; "a 2:07 marathoner eats 2x your gels" passes.
