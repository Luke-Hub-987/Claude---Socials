From 10K to halfway, swap every other gel for 4 or 5 jelly sweets. That's about the same sugar as a gel, in a different taste and a different texture.

From halfway it's gels only, because late on, anything you have to chew is hard work. Take each one about 100 metres before a water station. Swill the first sip of water round and spit it out, then take 3 good sips. Never wash a gel down with sports drink.

So why does a bar at 10K let you face a gel at 30K?

Your body gets sick of a taste it keeps getting, long before it's had enough sugar. Gel after gel from the start, and by 30K that sticky sweetness has been stacking up for 2 hours. Break it up with the bar and the jellies, clear it with that first sip, and the gel at 40K goes in like the one at 10K.
