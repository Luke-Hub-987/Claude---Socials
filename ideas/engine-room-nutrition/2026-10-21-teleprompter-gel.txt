If you're running a marathon in the next few weeks, your first gel should be the flavour you like least, and that's your race going to plan.

You're at 30K, you tear open the next gel, and the sweet, sticky smell alone stops you dead. It goes back in your pocket, and 5K later your legs are gone. Plan it the night before, and that 30K gel is one you actually want, and you're passing people over the last 5K.

There's a reason the same gel tastes fine at 10K and impossible at 30K, and it's decided before you start. Save this.

Lay your gels out the night before in the order you'll take them. Worst flavour first, never the same one twice in a row, and your favourite saved for after 30K. All the same flavour? Swap the after-30K ones for another you've trained with.

Take each gel about 100 metres before a water station, then 3 or 4 good sips of plain water. Never wash it down with sports drink, because that's sugar on top of sugar. Have your sports drink between gels instead.

Still can't face one at 30K? Don't force it. Put it away, swill some water round and spit it out, then sip plain water for 10 to 15 minutes. Skip that 1 gel, never 2, then take the next one in a different flavour.

So why does the same gel taste fine at 10K and impossible at 30K?

By 30K you've had that sweet taste over and over for 2 hours, and your body gets sick of a taste long before it's had enough sugar. A flavour you haven't had yet still tastes like the first one of the day. That's why your favourite's waiting in your pocket, and why the last 10K can be fuelled like the first.
