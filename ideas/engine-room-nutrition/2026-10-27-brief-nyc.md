## The course (as the guides describe it; check the official map before filming)
- Start on Staten Island, straight up the Verrazzano bridge (the first mile
  climbs, the second drops into Brooklyn).
- Brooklyn, miles 2 to 13: long and mostly flat, a short climb on Lafayette
  Avenue around mile 8. Into Queens over the Pulaski Bridge at halfway.
- Queensboro Bridge, about miles 15 to 16: the biggest climb of the middle
  miles, no spectators, near silent.
- Off the bridge onto First Avenue, about miles 16 to 19: flat, the
  loudest crowd of the day. The guides' common mistake: going too fast here.
- The Bronx, back over the Madison Avenue Bridge, then up Fifth Avenue
  (a long drag uphill, about miles 22 to 24) into Central Park, rolling to
  the finish.

## The aha (P140)
**The loudest mile is the one most likely to wreck your race, and you
reach it straight off the quietest.** The bridge feels slow, the crowd on
First Avenue feels like a gift, runners make up the "lost" time, and pay
for it on Fifth Avenue.

## The method: one worked example, 4:00 (9:09 a mile), audio in miles (the course signs), km in the caption
1. **The bridge, miles 0 to 2:** 9:30 a mile, the 10-word sentence in one
   breath. Don't win back the climb on the way down.
2. **Brooklyn, miles 2 to 13:** goal pace, 9:10, and not a second quicker.
   About 45 seconds behind at halfway is right (we've always said up to a
   minute).
3. **The quiet bridge, then the loud avenue:** on the Queensboro, let the
   pace drop to about 9:35 and hold the effort (5 words). On First Avenue,
   8:50 and no faster, however loud it gets.
4. **Fifth Avenue to the line:** the climb at about 9:15, shorter, quicker
   steps (as always). In the park, if you've still got 5 words, go: about 8:45.
   (Check: 2 miles at 9:30, 12 at 9:10, the bridge 2 at 9:35, First Avenue
