# Marathon week questions nobody has thought to ask

The filter: **would a marathoner three weeks out stop, and think "I
have genuinely never considered that, and now I need to know"?**
Anything they have already seen ten versions of is out, which rules out
pacing, carb loading and tapering as headline topics.

⚑ = verify before filming.

---

**1. Why your watch will say 43k and not 42.2** *(and what it costs you)* ⚑
Every single marathoner notices this afterwards and almost nobody knows
why beforehand. Running the tangents is a real, teachable skill worth
real minutes, and it is completely absent from race week content. The
strongest one here.

**2. What to do the night before when you cannot sleep** *(and why it matters less than you think)* ⚑
Universal, high anxiety, and the answer is genuinely reassuring: the
night two nights before is the one that counts ⚑. Nobody has told them
that, and it removes a fear rather than adding a task.

**3. Where to stand in your start wave** *(and the 2 minutes it costs to get it wrong)* ⚑
Nobody covers corral position and it shapes the entire first 5k.
Completely non-obvious, and physical enough to demonstrate.

**4. What to actually do at a water station** *(most people lose 20 seconds at every one)* ⚑
Twelve stations over a marathon. Nobody has ever been taught how to
drink on the move, and the cost adds up to minutes.

**5. What to do if you get a cold the week of your marathon** ⚑
The most frightening thing that can happen in race week, asked
constantly, answered by nobody. Needs a careful, honest, non-medical
framing.

**6. What to do if you need the toilet at 15k**
Taboo, universal, never covered, and the answer is genuinely useful.
Lands directly on ERN's gut territory.

**7. What to do when you are 20 seconds a kilometre down at halfway** ⚑
The decision every marathoner faces and nobody rehearses. A scenario
walkthrough rather than a timeline, so the device changes.

**8. What to wear when it is 8 degrees at the start and 14 by 10am** ⚑
Specific, seasonal, and the answer is not obvious. Pairs with the cold
twenty from the 30 minutes video.

**9. What to eat in the 20 minutes after you cross the line** *(and why nobody does)* ⚑
The recovery window nobody films because the video normally ends at the
finish. Extends ERN's territory past the race without leaving the
promise.

**10. How many gels to actually carry, and where to put them** ⚑
Pure practical, high search, and it is the exact question someone asks
the week before their first marathon.

---

## The four I would make, in order

**1, 2, 3, 4.** Each one is a question they have not been asked before,
each has a cost attached, each films as a talking head, and all four
sit in the same query family as the two winners so they compound (P75).

**#1 first.** "Why your watch will say 43k" is the most non-obvious
thing on the list, it is verifiable, and it has the rare quality of
being interesting even to someone not running a marathon.

## Note on account split

1, 3, 4, 7 and 8 are race execution and work on either account. **2, 5,
6, 9 and 10 are gut, fuelling and recovery, so they are ERN.** Keep the
two accounts out of the same query where possible.

## ⚑ Verification, before any of these are filmed

- Tangents and the real distance overrun. Widely documented, but get
  the typical figure right.
- The night-two-before sleep point. Real, and it must be sourced.
- Corral position costing time. Frame as his own experience unless a
  source exists.
- Water station technique and time lost. Do not invent the 20 seconds.
- **Illness in race week must not become medical advice.** Frame as
  what the decision looks like, not what to do about the illness.
