## The Zero to One secret (unexpected value, usable on race day)

Your first K should be the slowest of your whole race. Every runner's
instinct is to win back the time the crowd costs them; the right move
is to let it go, and pass those runners at thirty K. Second
secret: your watch is least accurate exactly when you look at it most.
Both are things a runner can do this Sunday with nothing to buy.

**[1. THE MAT: START YOUR WATCH]**
"First, the start mat. Your time only starts when you cross it, even if the gun went five minutes earlier, so start your watch as you step on the mat, with an alert set for your first gel at twenty minutes." ⚑1

**[2. FIRST K: IGNORE YOUR WATCH]**
"Second, the first K: ignore your watch. Tall buildings bounce the GPS around, so your pace can say five ten when you are really running five thirty. Run at an effort where you could still talk in full sentences." ⚑2

**[3. THE CROWD: DON'T CHASE]**
"Third, the crowd. Your first K might come in at five fifty, twenty seconds down, and that is fine. Weaving past people adds distance and burns energy you need at thirty K, so tuck in behind someone running your pace and let the crowd thin out." ⚑3

**[REHOOK]**
"And the last one is where the ten minute rule pays off."

**[4. 10 MINUTES: NOW CHECK]**
"Ten minutes in, the crowd has thinned and your watch has settled, so now you look. You want to be five to ten seconds a K slower than goal, so five thirty-five to five forty. If it says faster than five thirty, ease off now, while it still costs you nothing. Your first gel is ten minutes away." ⚑4

**[CTA, one sentence]**
"Save this for the start line, and the free race week guide and first go at the bar are in the bio."

## The worked example (caption)

The ten minute rule, for a 5:30 a K goal:
- The mat: start your watch on the mat, gel alert at 20 min
- First K: ignore the watch; it can say 5:10 when you are running 5:30. Run at talking effort
- The crowd: a 5:50 first K is fine. Don't weave, tuck in
- 10 minutes: now check. Aim for 5:35 to 5:40. Faster than 5:30? Ease off. First gel at 20 min
Any goal: run 5 to 10 seconds a K slower than goal pace once you check.
