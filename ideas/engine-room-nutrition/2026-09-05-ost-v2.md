# ERN on-screen text, v2

## Why v1 failed

Every line in v1 was about Engine Room Nutrition. "2 accountants,
⚑[N] failed batches." "What killed batch ⚑[N-1]." "Bar vs gel."

Run P54's three ingredients against them and all three are missing:

1. **An outcome the viewer already wants.** Our batch count is not one.
2. **A finite, enumerable set they can picture acquiring.** None.
3. **A signal they could not guess it themselves.** They can guess it:
   a small brand had some failures. Everyone knows that already.

So scrolling past costs nothing. The elite personal lines all pass all
three: *"10/10 uncomfortable habits of runners who never get injured"*
costs you the injury.

**The fix is not a better product story. It is that ERN's jeopardy has
to be a fear the viewer already owns, and we are the crash-test dummy
who goes and finds out.** We take the risk, they get the answer. That
keeps the jeopardy shape the account is rewarded for (1,271-2,158)
without turning the account into a founder diary.

⚑ = a real fact or number Luke supplies or a source he can stand behind.

## TOP OF FUNNEL

**1. 10/10 things elite runners eat before a race that sound completely wrong** ⚑
Heat: borrowed authority plus counterintuition. Collectible "10/10"
series device, his own reference shape, and the contents are
unguessable by definition. Needs ⚑ ten real, sourced examples.

**2. The gel that works every long run and turns on you at mile 20**
Heat: betrayal plus time bomb. Straight out of a real race report:
*"took 40g per hour and felt fine up until mile 20 or so, took a GU at
that point and had stomach issues from it which never happened to me
during training."* Keyword "gel" is surface-level, so New can hold it,
and mile 20 is a Casual benchmark.

**3. The 3 fuelling mistakes in almost every "I blew up at 30k" race report** ⚑
Heat: self-incrimination. The vehicle is real and it is ours: we have
read a few hundred of these. Finite, unguessable, and the outcome
(not blowing up) is already wanted.

**4. 90g of carbs an hour, or the amount that actually stays down** ⚑
Heat: betrayal of a trend the viewer is currently following. Real
confusion in the research, verbatim: *"some say 100g an hour, some 30,
and a lot in between."* Iceberg keyword for Core, plain enough for New.

**5. We took ⚑[N] gels in 90 minutes to find where the stomach gives up**
*(so nobody else has to)*
Heat: jeopardy, and the crash-test-dummy reframe made literal. Can
visibly fail on camera. This is the shape the account already gets
paid for, pointed at the viewer's fear instead of our product.

**6. 10/10 things runners force down before a race and secretly hate**
Heat: self-incrimination plus in-group recognition. Real scene:
*"I don't feel like the banana on offer, but I force it into my stomach
anyway."* Sets up the counter-position without arguing for it.

**7. Force the banana down at the aid station, or run past it** ⚑
Heat: a fork with a consequence, in a scene, per P53. Every marathoner
has stood at that table.
