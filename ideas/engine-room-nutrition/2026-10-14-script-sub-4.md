# How to pace a sub-4 marathon at every 10K

## OST

> **at every 10K**

**Markers, the stage, the pace, the time on your watch at the end of it:**

## The Zero to One secret

To break four hours, you should be behind four-hour pace at 10K. Every
sub-4 hopeful runs with the four-hour pacer or ahead of them to bank
time; the ones who go under pass that pacer after 35K.

## Caption

How to pace a sub-4 marathon at every 10K. Check the total time on your watch at each 10K sign, not the pace, because GPS reads long. Start your watch on the start mat.

0 to 10K at 5:45 per K, 57:30 at the 10K sign. Behind four-hour pace on purpose, and you can still say "I am running a marathon today and I feel good" in one breath.

10 to 20K at 5:38 per K, 1:53:50 at 20K. Level with four-hour pace. Gel every 20 minutes.

20 to 30K at 5:38 per K, 2:50:10 at 30K. Shorter, quicker steps. Now ahead.

30K to the line at 5:38 to 35K (3:18:20). Still saying all ten words? 5:33 to the finish for about 3:58. If not, hold 5:38 for about 3:59.
