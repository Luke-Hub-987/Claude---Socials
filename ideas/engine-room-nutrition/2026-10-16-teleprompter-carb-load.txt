If you are running a marathon in the next few weeks, you should carb load without eating a single extra plate of food, and that is your race going to plan.

Do it the usual way, a mountain of pasta the night before, and you can start bloated and still hit the wall at thirty K.

Do it right, and at thirty K your watch still shows goal pace.

There are two fuel tanks to fill before a marathon, and runners forget the second one, so save this.

For a Sunday race, the load starts at Friday breakfast.

Eat eight to ten grams of carbs for every kilo you weigh, each day: at seventy kilos, five hundred and sixty to seven hundred grams.

Every weight is in the caption.

Keep your plates the same size, and swap the cheese, butter and creamy sauces for plain carbs.

The first tank is filling.

Friday dinner is where clean eaters go wrong.

Brown rice, salad and broccoli fill you with fibre and leave less room for carbs.

So go white for two days: a big bowl of white rice with teriyaki sauce and a can of fizzy drink is about a hundred and fifteen grams.

By Saturday you will be fed up with eating, so drink some of it: two big glasses of apple juice add about seventy grams.

Eat your last proper meal early and plain, around five, and the first tank is full.

And the last one is the second tank, and it empties while you sleep.

The second tank is your liver.

It keeps your blood sugar steady while you run, and a lot of it drains overnight.

So three hours before the start, refill it with about two grams of carbs per kilo: at seventy kilos, two plain bagels with jam and a glass of juice.

Then you start with both tanks full.
