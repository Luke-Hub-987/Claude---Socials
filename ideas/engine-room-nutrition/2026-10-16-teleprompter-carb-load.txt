If you are running a marathon in the next few weeks, you should carb load without eating a single extra plate of food, and that is your race going to plan.

Do it the usual way, a mountain of pasta the night before, and you can start bloated and still hit the wall at thirty K.

Do it right, and at thirty K your watch still shows goal pace.

There are two fuel tanks to fill before a marathon, and runners forget the second one.

Here is the exact plan for a seventy kilo runner with a Sunday race, so save this.

Friday, the load starts: your normal meals, all white and low in fat.

Aim for eight to ten grams of carbs per kilo you weigh, so about six hundred today.

Eight, two white bagels with jam and a big glass of apple juice.

Eleven, a banana and a bottle of sports drink.

One, a big bowl of white rice with a little chicken, teriyaki sauce and a can of fizzy drink.

Four, two crumpets with honey and a handful of pretzels.

Seven, white pasta with tomato sauce, two slices of white bread and a glass of juice.

The first tank is filling.

Saturday, repeat Friday until four, plus an extra big glass of apple juice.

Five o'clock is your last proper meal, normal size: a bowl of white rice with a little chicken and soy sauce, and a glass of juice.

Then stop eating, just water until bed.

The first tank is full.

And the last one is the second tank, and it empties while you sleep.

The second tank is your liver.

It keeps your blood sugar steady, and a lot of it drains overnight.

So three hours out, six for a nine o'clock start, refill it: two white bagels with jam and a glass of juice, about a hundred and fifty grams.

Then sips of water, and your first gel at twenty minutes.
