# Brief: What your last marathon says about your next one

## OST

**ABOUT YOUR NEXT ONE**

**Markers (your race, by the K):**
1. NUMBER 1: YOUR FIRST 10K

4. YOUR NEXT ONE

## The aha (P140)
Runners think the next marathon gets better with more training. **The
answer is already on their watch:** 3 numbers from the last race show
exactly where the next one will go wrong, and the K where it fell apart is
never where it went wrong (the wall's own lesson, the cause sits earlier).
Before = last race's numbers. After = the 3 changes. The viewer is the
story (the brand advice, P158).

## The method (no sums: each number is a yes or no, P155)
1. **Your first 10K.** Open the splits on your watch app. Was the first 10K
   quicker than your goal pace? If yes, that's where your race went, not
   where you felt it. Next time: 5 to 10 seconds a K slower, all 10 words
   in one breath.
2. **Your first gel.** When was it? If it was after 20 minutes, or you
   took them when you felt you needed them, that's number 2. Next time:
   first gel at 20 minutes, then every 30, on the clock.
3. **The K it turned (the payoff).** Find the first K where you slowed by
   more than 15 seconds. That's where it fell apart, and it's never where
   it went wrong. Look back at numbers 1 and 2. Fix those, and you arrive at
   that K a different runner.
4. **Your next one.** Write the 3 numbers down. That's your before. The
   3 changes are your after.
