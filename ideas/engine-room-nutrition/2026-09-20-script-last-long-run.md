**[HOOK STACK]**

*Hook and gate, "you" first:* "You have two or three long runs left
before your marathon ⚑, and the fitness part of them is already done."

*Super hook, pain twisted:* "Every one you run as just another long run
is a rehearsal you gave away, and then the first time you test your
fuel, your breakfast and your kit for real is thirty kilometres into
the race you trained sixteen weeks for."

*Super hook, dream outcome and the cost:* "There are three things to
rehearse on Sunday, they take no extra effort and about forty seconds
to explain, and they decide whether race morning feels like something
you have already done."

**[ONE — THE FUEL]**
"Take exactly what you plan to take on the day, at the exact timings,
in the exact amounts. If the plan is a gel every twenty minutes from
minute twenty, do that on Sunday. Most people carry two on a long run
and then plan to take eight on race day, and the first time their
stomach sees eight is the day it counts. Your gut builds the machinery
to absorb that under running stress, and it takes something like ten to
fourteen days of practice ⚑. This weekend is inside that window. The
one after is the edge of it. And if part of your plan is real food
rather than another gel, that gets rehearsed too."

**[ON SCREEN ONLY, not spoken]**
"2 accountants. ⚑[N] failed batches. Still pre-launch."

**[TWO — THE MORNING]**
"Set race morning's alarm. Eat race morning's breakfast, the same
amount, and leave the same gap before you start running. You are
testing the clock here: the three hours, the coffee, the toilet, the
drive in. Plenty of people have a breakfast that works perfectly when
they start at nine, and find out something different about it when
they have to eat it at half five."

**[THREE — THE KIT]**
"Everything you will wear and carry goes on. Shoes, socks, shorts,
belt, the number pinned where you are going to pin it. Anything that
rubs at twenty K rubs worse at thirty five. The rule everyone repeats
is nothing new on race day, and the only way to keep it is to make
sure everything has already been old at least once."

**[CLOSE]**
"Do those three and race morning is the second time you have done it.
Leave them and race day is the first time for all three at once, in
the one place you cannot change anything."

**[THREAD]**
"The three hours before and the thirty minutes before the gun are both
their own video on the profile."

**[CTA]**
"Save this before Sunday. And if you want something for that last
thirty minutes that is not another gel, early access and the free race
week guide are in the bio."
