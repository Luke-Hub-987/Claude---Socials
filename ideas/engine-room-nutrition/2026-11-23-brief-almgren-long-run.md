# Brief: Almgren's long run, scaled for 3:00 to 4:00 (23 Nov, the pro's plan scaled; P138: Luke's yes before the script)

**The method in numbers:** his 40K in four equal blocks, each faster: easy (85% of race-pace speed), steady (95%), race pace (100%), faster (105%).
The Punto version keeps the shape and the time on feet, not his distance, and fuels from the start.
| Goal | Easy | Steady | Race pace | Faster |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3:00 | 5:01 | 4:29 | 4:16 | 4:04 |
| 3:30 | 5:51 | 5:14 | 4:59 | 4:44 |
| 4:00 | 6:41 | 5:59 | 5:41 | 5:25 |
Fuel: he drank 36 g every 15 minutes from the start, because it took him about an hour before it counted. Yours: the first gel at 20 minutes, then every 30.
