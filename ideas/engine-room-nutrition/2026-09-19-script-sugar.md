## ⚑ Before filming — one of these is an accuracy trap

1. **"Sugar" versus "carbohydrate".** The title says sugar because it
   is the universal word and it is what makes this CCN. But most gels
   are largely maltodextrin, which is not sugar in the everyday sense.
   **Say "carbohydrate, mostly in the form of sugars" once in the
   script**, or a pedant will correct it in the comments and be right.
2. **240 grams.** It is arithmetic on a target rather than a
   measurement, so say "about".
3. **Seven cans of Coke.** A 330ml can is roughly 35 grams ⚑, which
   gives just under seven. Check the can size before saying it, because
   it is the line people will repeat.
4. **The two-week gut adaptation window.** Load-bearing: it is what
   makes the whole video a deadline.
5. **250mg of sodium an hour** and the failed-batch count.
