# My co-founder has no idea I'm running this marathon

## OST
**MY CO-FOUNDER HAS NO IDEA**
**I'M RUNNING THIS MARATHON**

Nine words, "marathon" in it, the answer (his face) withheld. No "Dublin" in
the title: a narrow gate costs Instagram reach (sub-4 review), and the story
works for anyone.

**On-screen markers (clock times, filmed for real):**
1. THE NIGHT BEFORE
2. [6:30AM]
3. [8:15AM]: BAG DROP
4. [8:20AM]: HE'S RIGHT THERE

## Why it can work
- A real vehicle: Luke is the one doing it (the vehicle rule).
- Founder story is our best non-advice ERN content, and reactions are what
  people stay for.
- **The reaction is the named unknown, held to the end** (without the
  wall: payoff last = best watch time). Everything before it is the
  build-up.

## Script: what Luke says to camera, in edit order (about 45 to 60s)

**[THE NIGHT BEFORE]** (opening frame: the bib in your hand, close to the lens)
"There are 2 of us behind Engine Room. Tomorrow Jamie's running the Dublin Marathon, and he has no idea I'm running it too."

"He's been training for [X months]. I got this [how/when you got the bib]. If he sees me before the start, it's ruined."

**[6:30AM]**
"[He thinks I'm at home / coming to watch at X]. Bib's going under the jacket."

**[8:15AM: BAG DROP]**
"He's somewhere in here. [His goal / his pen colour]."

**[8:20AM: HE'S RIGHT THERE]** (whisper, phone low)
"He's right there."

THE REVEAL: no lines. Tap his shoulder, unzip the jacket, show the bib. Keep
filming for 20 seconds after, whatever happens.

Then ask him on camera: "What's going through your head?"

**END** (filmed after the race, 5 seconds)
"We both finished. How we ran it, every 10K, is in the next video. And if you want your next race planned, email us your race and goal time through the link in the bio."

## Practical (P146: does it add a worry?)
- **Do the reveal 30 to 40 minutes before the gun, at bag drop or the walk
  to the pens,** not in the pen in the last 10 minutes. He still needs his
  toilet, his gel and his head. And your bib may not get you into his pen.
- **Nothing goes up tonight anywhere Jamie can see:** no stories, no trial
  reels, no drafts on the ERN account if he has the login. Keep the clips on
  your phone.
- **Ask Jamie before posting.** He's the one on camera.
- If you've got bars, eat one on camera at bag drop (the 30 minutes before
  is what the bar is for, Pillar 2). Only if it's real.
- The no-Dublin rule on the personal account was there to protect the
  surprise. It lifts once he's seen you.
