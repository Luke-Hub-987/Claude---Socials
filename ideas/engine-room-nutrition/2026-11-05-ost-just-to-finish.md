# The OST for the recorded "just to finish" reel

## What the title has to do (from the recording)
Line one opens "If that's secretly you...", so the title must say who
"that" is: someone with a sub-4 goal they haven't told anyone. Line one
then brings the stake (sub-4 pace on the watch can still get you a 4:02);
the cover (the watch reading FINISH 4:02) is the pain picture. So the title
carries the identity and the keyword; it can't repeat 4:02 (rows 1, 29; G13).

## Why the current title is weak
SAID JUST TO FINISH. / WANT SUB-4 MARATHON? is clipped like a telegram
(no "you", no "a"), reads like an ad's question, and splits into two
thoughts. The keyword survives; the secret, the thing Luke says they'd
never tell anyone, doesn't.

## The pick
**THE SUB-4 MARATHON / YOU WON'T SAY OUT LOUD** (18 / 22)
- **Keyword:** "sub-4 marathon", the exact phrase runners type and our best
  TikTok title family (9.5K).
- **Briar's 5:** curiosity (what about it?); pain (the goal you're scared
  to say and miss); the dream is implied (saying it after); tangible (a
  sub-4 marathon is a clock time anyone pictures); no whisper needed.
- **The "never say it" moment:** it names the private thing exactly.
- **Layers:** title = the secret goal; line one = the 4:02 trap and the 4
  numbers; cover = the watch at 4:02. Nothing repeats.
- **Doesn't answer itself:** nothing about watches, signs or the hand plan.

## Runner-up

use, shorter to read, but two fragments and less of a picture.

Luke's right. THE SUB-4 MARATHON / YOU WON'T SAY OUT LOUD is a riddle for

The same shapes in his swipe file: "How to Become Noticeably More Athletic
in 90 Days (It only takes 3 Hours a Week)", "How To Become So Fast It Feels

by hour)" 18K; "...the last 30 minutes... (the part nobody plans)" 13.8K),
and "how to... sub-4 marathon" is our best TikTok family (9.5K). P80: the
whisper may raise the stakes, never settle them.

Line one is recorded: "If that's secretly you, sub-4 pace on your watch can
still get you a 4:02." The title has to give "that" a meaning, and
"secretly" has something to land on.
