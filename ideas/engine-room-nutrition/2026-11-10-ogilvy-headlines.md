# Ogilvy headlines, applied

## What Ogilvy adds to what we already do
Our titles already give a benefit when they're goal titles (HOW TO RUN A SUB-4).
Three of his rules we don't use yet:
1. **Call out the reader you want** in the headline ("flag down the prospect"):
   who they are, in their words. Most of our viewers work full time and fit
   training around it, and nothing we say tells them it's for them.
2. **Sell the benefit, not the format.** "RANKING..." leads with what we do;
   "...FOR A PB" is the benefit, at the end.
3. **No negatives, no one else, no blind headlines.** Readers take a negative
   at face value, and a line about someone else isn't about them.

## Luke's draft line one, checked
"What if you weren't a full-time runner, a running influencer, but you did work
a full-time job. You had a family... What if you wanted to run a personal best?
Which marathon is the best choice for you?"
- **Keep:** the prospect (full-time job, family) and the self-interest (a PB
  despite the busy life). That's the Ogilvy move, and it's the "that's so me"
  feeling the best titles gave us.
- **Change:**
  - About 45 words before the question (about 12 seconds); half our viewers are
    gone by 0:03, and P189 wants the question inside 10 words.
  - It opens on a negative and on someone else (influencers).
  - "Which marathon is the best choice for you?" promises help choosing. The
    audience is runners already booked (Luke's G9 call, 9 Nov), 5 of the 8 are
    closed, and the body ranks each course's catch, not which race suits a busy
    life. A headline the body doesn't pay is ledger row 17.

## The fix: the benefit the busy runner gets is already in the body
Every course's change in v3 happens on race day. **It adds no training.** For
someone with a job and a family, that's the benefit.

**OST:** 8 SPRING MARATHONS: / 1 CHANGE EACH FOR A PB (19 / 22)
- The benefit (a PB), specific (8 races, 1 change each), the gap (which change?),
  and their race is in the 8. The tiers stay as the markers, S last.

**Line one:** "Full-time job, a family, and a spring marathon booked? This won't add
a single run to your week. Each course has one change on the day, and the
flattest one can cost you before you've run a step."
- The prospect inside 9 words, the benefit by about 4 seconds, and v3's gap
  ("how can it cost me before I've started?") kept for S TIER to pay.
- True to the body: every change is on the day (v3).
