## Script v1 (331 words, about 1:38)
If you're running a marathon in the next few weeks, you should let the 4:30 pacer get away from you at every drink station, and that's your race going to plan.

Chase that flag through the tables and you'll throw half a cup over yourself, then be walking at 32K anyway. Walk them on purpose, and you'll still be running at 35K.

A 20-second walk gives you one thing the people chasing that flag never get. Save this.

First, your 4 times. 1:05 at 10K, 2:08 at 20K, 3:11 at 30K and 4:14 at 40K. They include every walk. Write them on your wrist or your lock screen.

If 4:30 isn't your goal, email us your race and goal time through the link in the bio and we'll send you your own times, free. Now, the walks.

For the first 10K, keep your watch between 6:20 and 6:25 a K. You should be able to say "I am running a marathon today and I feel good", all 10 words in 1 breath. At each drink station, walk from the first table to the last, about 20 seconds, then run again. It costs about 15 seconds, and that's in your times.

From 10K, drop to between 6:10 and 6:15, and keep walking every station. At 20K, look for 2:08, 10 words but a bit breathy. At 30K, 3:11, and about 5 words before you need a breath. The flag will pull away at every table, and you'll pull it back between them.

So what does that walk give you?

Run through a station and you'll get a mouthful from the cup and wear the rest. Walk it and you get the whole cup, and your gel goes down with proper water. That's what you're running on at 35K, when the people who chased the flag start walking. Hold 6:10 to 6:15 to 40K, about 4:14 at that sign, then run the last 2K between 6:00 and 6:05, and you'll finish around 4:28.

(Markers: FOUR TIMES on "First, your 4 times"; 0-10K on "For the first 10K"; 10-30K on "From 10K, drop"; 30K TO THE LINE: THE WHOLE CUP on "Run through a station".)

## Caption (final)
How to pace a 4:30 marathon at every 10K. Your 4 times: about 1:05 at the 10K sign, 2:08 at 20K, 3:11 at 30K and 4:14 at 40K. They already include walking every drink station.

Keep your watch between 6:20 and 6:25 a K (10:10 to 10:20 a mile) for the first 10K, then between 6:10 and 6:15 a K (9:55 to 10:05 a mile) to 40K, then 6:00 to 6:05 a K (9:40 to 9:50 a mile) for the last 2K. At every drink station, walk from the first table to the last, about 20 seconds, then run again. Step to the side before you slow down, take the whole cup, and if you're taking a gel, have it there with the water. The 4:30 pacer will pull away at every table, and you'll pull it back between them.

Effort check with the sentence "I am running a marathon today and I feel good": all 10 words in one breath to 10K, 10 slightly breathy to 20K, about 5 by 30K, 2 or 3 at the end.
