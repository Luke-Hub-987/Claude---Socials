## ⚑ Before scripting

1. **Absorption timing.** Ingested carbohydrate takes time to be
   absorbed and used, which is the direction everything rests on.
   **No minute figure out loud**, "later than you think" is the
   defensible form. Luke's science can set the range.
2. **"Breakfast refills what the night emptied."** Liver glycogen falls
   overnight. Direction is solid. No grams.
3. **Solid food early.** Consistent with the long run, gels
   alternatives and wall videos: blood leaves the stomach as the race
   goes on.
4. **No grams per hour** until one range is chosen for the whole
   account (60, 60 to 80, or 60 to 90 are all in circulation).
5. **The bar line.** "If any of your fuel is solid, it goes in here" is
   the honest place for it. The CTA stays early access and the guide,
   never a purchase (pre-launch).
