# Stories, every day: what to post and what we learn

## The rules for every story
1. **One frame, one question, one sticker.** Poll, quiz, slider or
   question box. Nothing to read but the question (the same one-glance
   rule as P159).
2. **Answer every reply within the hour,** by DM. Anyone who tells you
   their race and goal gets the plan link back (the link in bio, as a link
   sticker): their sign-up, no extra step for anyone else (P154).
3. **Show the result the next day.** "Here's what 240 of you said" brings
   people back, and it's a story in itself.
4. **Log the counts once a week** in `ideas/stories-log.md`: answers per
   option, replies, link taps. Counts only, never names or handles.
5. Stories reach followers, so they don't grow the account. They build the
   list, the topic list and the bar's research, and they warm up followers
   before a reel goes out.

## LLM Council (P150)
- **Contrarian:** Daily polls get tiring, and a story about eating before
  a race starts to look like a sales pitch for a bar that isn't out yet.
- **Assumption-ripper:** We assume followers answer polls about
  themselves; they answer fastest when it's about their race. Mon and Sun
  do that; the bar question is one day in seven, asked as a question, not
  a pitch.
- **Expansionist:** Mon's question box is a sign-up engine: every reply
  gets the plan link in a DM, and the race and goal arrive with it.
- **Outsider:** "I'd answer 'when's your next marathon' in a second. I'd
  ignore anything that needs me to type a lot."
- **Executor:** Start Saturday with the New York poll. Monday's race
  question is the one that matters most.
- **Blind cross-review:** All agree on one sticker per frame and showing
  results the next day. The bar question stays, as a question.
- **Chairman:** **Run this week as written, starting with the New York poll
  on Saturday and the race question on Monday.**
