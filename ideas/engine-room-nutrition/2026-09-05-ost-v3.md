## TOP OF FUNNEL

**1. Can you actually eat 700g of carbs the day before a marathon?**
Named: 700g, one day, one marathon. Real: *"8-12g of carbs per kg in
the 24 hours before, with 10g a good starting point"* — 10g/kg on a
70kg runner is 700g. Jeopardy we can film: a table with 700g of carbs
on it and two people trying. **CCN:** New sees an absurd pile of food;
Casual knows carb loading; Core knows the g/kg protocol. Want #1.

**2. Everyone says 90g an hour. We took 120g and ran hard for 90 minutes.**
Named: 90g, 120g, 90 minutes. Real: *"60-90g is a solid base
suggestion. The pros generally do more, like 120g."* Can visibly fail
on camera, and the failure is the video. Want #2.

**3. 10/10 uncomfortable fuelling habits of runners who never blow up at mile 20** ⚑
Named: mile 20. Direct transplant of the shape that did 2,156 on your
account, pointed at the fuelling identity. **Breaks P42** — it is a
list, and lists on ERN's grid are how-to shaped. See the note at the
bottom. Want #1.

**4. He took a gel at mile 20 that had never once caused a problem in training**
Named: mile 20, a gel, a real person's race. Verbatim: *"took 40g per
hour and felt fine up until mile 20 or so. I took a GU at that point
and had some stomach issues from it which never happened to me during
training."* Betrayal plus time bomb, and the whole video is why that
happens. Want #2.

**5. £⚑[X] of gels for one marathon, and ⚑[N] of them stay down**

**6. The 5 things people eat at 5am before a marathon, ranked by what stays down** ⚑

**7. Force the banana down at 30k, or run past the table**

**8. Nobody has built anything for the 30 minutes before the gun except a gel**
