# How pros actually race a marathon

| Line | Pass |

| Sibling of a top-8 video | The wall's race-clock stages (142K) plus the pacing family (goal pace 24.8K, first half 17.5K) |

## Why it is the creative one

- **A new title shape: the identity.** "How pros actually race a
  marathon" sells who they want to be, where every title so far sold a
  fear or a moment. "Actually" keeps the outlier's secret signal.
- **Named rules as the markers**, the papanca move, on our clock.
- **The head, not the gut.** Strategy, like goal pace and first half.
- **Luke's own idea inside it:** racing to the next block is the
  lamppost rule from his personal account, which links the two brands.

## OST

> **How pros actually**
> **race a marathon**

**Markers, distance plus rule:**
**0-10K: HOLD BACK / 10-20K: EAT EARLY / 20-30K: THE NEXT 5K ONLY / 30K-FINISH: NOW RACE**

## The secret (Thiel)

The pros look slow early. They start at a pace that feels almost
embarrassing, eat before they need it, never think about the whole
distance, and only start racing at 30K, which is exactly where everyone
else is hanging on.

## Hook stack (P106, five lines)

- Gate: "If you are running a marathon in the next few weeks,"
- Secret and loop, to "you": "you should start it at a pace that feels
  almost embarrassing, because that is how the pros race it, and it is
  why they are passing people at thirty K."
- Pain, the race outcome: "Go out with the crowd and you spend the last
  ten K hanging on and watching your time slip."
- Dream, a picture: "Race it like a pro and the last ten K is where you
  start passing people."

## Stages (each opens on the unexpected, ends on an in-race action)

| Stage | Unexpected | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0-10K HOLD BACK | Feeling almost embarrassed is the right pace; adrenaline makes race pace feel free | Let people go; check your watch, not the crowd |
| 10-20K EAT EARLY | Pros eat when they feel best | First fuel at twenty minutes, then every twenty |
| 20-30K THE NEXT 5K ONLY | Nobody races 42K in their head | Race to the next 5K marker or water station, one block at a time |
| 30K-FINISH NOW RACE | This is the first time a pro actually races | Same effort, it costs more now; shorter steps, then pick off one runner at a time |

## Truth checks before scripting

- "Pros run even or negative splits" is broadly true of modern
  marathon records; said as "pros look slow early", no numbers, no
  names (P105, P107).
- Fuel timing and stride match the wall videos.
- No study language, no statistics, no medication, clarity first.
