# Who actually hits the marathon wall

## Why this topic

**The wall is the account's biggest asset**: 68K and 19.4K, against
9.5K for cramp in the same package at the same age. Galloway: double
down on the outlier. The first wall video said **where** it starts, the
second **how to get out**. The obvious next question for the same
audience is **"will it be me?"**, and there is a data set of four
million marathon results that answers it, with a result most runners
would bet against.

## The secret (Thiel), from the data

**Smyth, 2021, PLOS ONE: 4 million race records, 270 marathons.**
Hitting the wall = slowing by at least 25% for at least 5K, after 20K.

| Finding | Number | Why it is unexpected |
|---|---|---|
| **Runners hit it more in the years just before a PB** | **36%** in the 3 years before a recent PB, **23%** in earlier years | You would think fitter means safer. The runners getting faster are the ones who hit it |
| Men hit it far more than women | **28%** of men, **17%** of women | Women pace more evenly (Smyth 2018, 1.7M runners) |
| Slower runners hit it more | The share rises steadily with PB times from 3 hours to about 5 to 5.5 hours | People assume it is a fast runner's problem |
| Where and how long | Starts at about **29.5K** on average, lasts about **10K** | Matches the first wall video's "thirty K" exactly |
| What it costs | About **half an hour** slower than their best (31.5 min men, 33.2 min women) | The share fact for the pain line |

## Four stages, one axis: the four runners most likely to hit it

Each stage: **who, the number, then what to do in the race** (P98).
Markers: **THE PB CHASERS / THE MEN / THE FAST STARTERS / 3 TO 5 HOURS**

| Stage | The unexpected | In-race action (nothing to carry) |
|---|---|---|
| **THE PB CHASERS** | 36% against 23% | Race the pace your long runs proved, not the one you want. At 10K, if it already feels like work, ease off now (thread: goal pace video, 19.8K) |
| **THE MEN** | 28% against 17%, and women pace more evenly | Run the first half at an even effort and race the second |
| **THE FAST STARTERS** | Fast starts make the wall more likely later | First 5K five to ten seconds a K slower than goal, as in the first half video |
| **3 TO 5 HOURS** | The slower the PB, up to about five hours, the more likely | Carbs from twenty minutes and every twenty after, as in the wall video ⚑ (our reasoning: longer on the course, more fuel needed) |
