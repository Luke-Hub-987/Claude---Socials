## How Instagram trial reels work
1. **Who sees it:** only people who don't follow the account. It doesn't
   go on the grid or to followers' feeds, so a flop costs the profile
   nothing.
2. **How to post one:** make the reel as normal. On the last screen (where
   the caption goes) there's a **Trial** toggle. Switch it on. It's there
   for professional accounts (ERN is one).
3. **Auto-share:** it asks whether to share it to followers automatically
   if it does well within 72 hours. **Turn that off for these first ones,**
   so you decide with the numbers (the wall's followers have already seen
   it, so it should never go to them).
4. **Collab:** as far as I know, a trial reel can't have a collaborator, so
   post trials from the ERN account alone. If one wins and you share it,
   reshare it to your personal story.
5. **Where to find it:** it shows in the ERN Reels tab with a "Trial"
   label that only you see. Insights work the same as a normal reel.
6. **Sharing a winner:** open it and tap share to followers. It moves to
   the grid and keeps the views it already has.
