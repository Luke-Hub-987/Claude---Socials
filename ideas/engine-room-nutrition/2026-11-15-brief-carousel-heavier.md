# Carousel: SAME RUNNER. 1.5KG HEAVIER. FASTER.

## Why the yogurt carousel works (what a pro sees)
508.1K likes, 165.1K saves (32% of likes), 56.7K shares, 2,541 comments, 25 slides.
1. **Slide 1 is a scene with a problem in it**: "Imagine you're selling yogurt...
   and nobody's buying." You're in the chair before you've decided to be.
2. **Slide 2 is a quiz you answer in your head**: two cups, "Which one sells?" Once
   you've picked, you have to swipe to find out if you were right.
3. **The answer, then "Why?", then the twist**: "It's the exact same yogurt." The
   thing you thought mattered didn't.
4. **A name to keep**: "That's the framing effect." The save.
5. **"What else?" opens a second example** that proves it's a pattern, not a fluke.
6. **One line per slide, 3 to 12 words.** The swipe is the retention: every slide
   is a small reward and a new question. The eyes do the acting (doubt, surprise,
   smug), so there's no voice to skip.
7. **The viewer is the student.** The money they'll make is the reason to care;
   ours is the finish time.

**Not ours:** their art. The suited silhouettes with white eyes are their look.
We take the mechanics (scene, quiz, twist, name, "what else?", second example)
and draw our own two characters.

## Facts update (16 Nov)
- **The loaded runner runs faster:** sub-elite marathon runners who ate more than
  7 g/kg of carbs the day before ran significantly faster (Atkinson and others,
  2011, *Int J Sports Med*, doi 10.1055/s-0031-1273739); loading postpones fatigue
  in events over about 90 minutes (Hawley and others, 1997, *Sports Med*,
  PubMed 9291549). Slide 4 holds.
- **About 1 to 2 kg gained:** only coaching sources (Topend Sports, Styrkr), built
  on 2.7 to 4 g of water per gram of glycogen. No study weighed runners. So the
  1.5 kg on the slides stays as an example, and the caption says "about 1 to 2 kg
  is typical", not a study.
- **Timing (16 Nov):** the fourth-hour reel (fuel) went to 15K in 12 hours; the
  carousel keeps the fuel lane with a different twist (the scales), not a sequel.
  TikTok photo mode first.
