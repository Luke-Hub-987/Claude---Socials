One-second reads, in short. The 4-hour runner stops on T5 ("I got 42.6") and T8. The runner who has never raced stops on T8 only. The non-runner stops on nothing except T8, and then reads it as a gadget gripe.

## Top 3
1. **T5.** 42.6K is the one thing every viewer has seen on their own watch, so it passes "they already know it's their problem" better than any sub-4-only title.
2. **T4.** The scare quotes route is true and needs nothing worked out, but it's sub-4 only and repeats 6 Nov word for word. That's why it sits under T5's wider recognition.
3. **T8.** It's the cleanest fight on the list, but the cards contradict it. It needs the rewrite before it can run, and the other two only need tightening.

## Rewrites
**YOUR WATCH SAYS 42.6K. / YOUR MARATHON PACE / IS LYING (3:30 TO 4:30)** (22 / 18 / 23; LYING in red)
This is T5 made true. The enemy is the pace on the watch, not the distance. The bracket gives each goal a card to swipe to. 42.6K is answered on screen because marathon is in line 2.

**'ON PACE' ALL MARATHON. / FINISHED 2 MINS LATE / (BLAME THE WATCH)** (23 / 20 / 17; LATE in red)
This is T4 opened up to every goal. 1% long costs 2:06 at 3:30 and 2:42 at 4:30, so "2 MINS" holds for all four cards. It no longer repeats the 6 Nov 4:02 line.

**NEVER TRUST YOUR / WATCH'S MARATHON PACE / (USE THESE 4 TIMES)** (16 / 21 / 19; NEVER in red)
This is T8 with the cause narrowed to the pace, so the cards can't contradict it. Each card then has to lead with the 10K sign times, not the pace bands. The bracket repeats the 20 Oct "4 times" line, so it's the weakest of the three.
