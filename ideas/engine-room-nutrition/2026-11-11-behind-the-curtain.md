**Our formula, from our own winners:** a moment every marathoner lives (the wall,
7 days out, carb loading, the first 10 minutes) + a word that says "you've had
this wrong" (ACTUALLY) or a paradox + an exact window or place (where, 7 days,
10 minutes, every 10K) + a structure under it to save. The flop proves the word
needs the famous moment and the exact place.

## What the outside examples add to our formula
1. **Maximum outcome, minimum cost** ("UNLIMITED", "3 hours a week").
2. **An order against a trusted rule** ("Stop doing zone 2").
3. **A flattering identity with a twist** ("The High Achiever Paradox").
4. **Permission for the forbidden** ('Copy', "Steal", carbs at S tier).
5. **Remove the obvious answer** ("It's NOT running").
6. **A famous benchmark to beat** ("Dethrone Alex Hormozi").
