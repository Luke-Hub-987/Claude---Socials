# What to do in the last 24 hours before a marathon

## Why this replaced "the last 3 weeks"

Luke asked whether I was certain the taper video would work. **I was
not, and on the topic data it was the weaker pick:**

| What drives reach in our data | Last 3 weeks | **Last 24 hours** |
|---|---|---|
| **Race day, or a decision about race-day pace.** Every video above 15K is one of those: the wall, 3 hours, 30 minutes, first half, and goal pace (a training run whose whole point is race pace) | A training-week topic. The race-outcome link is real (the taper decides the race), so this row is closer than it looks; the other training videos sat at 4.4K to 5.3K | **The day before the race** |
| **The exact title of the two clock winners**, "What to do in the last ___ before a marathon" (20.9K, 15.1K) | Yes | **Yes, and it completes the run: 24 hours, 3 hours, 30 minutes** |
| **Every viewer in every stage (P103)** | Someone racing in 10 days has missed half of it | **Everyone lives through the whole day, whenever their race is** |
| Advice the account has never given | Yes | **Yes**: the day before has never been posted |
| Contested claim for comments | Weak | **The pasta dinner** |
| Fuel inside, race in the title (P101) | Partly | **Yes** |

## How sure am I

**Not 100%, and nobody honestly could be.** Fifteen videos, with topic,
title and structure moving together, cannot guarantee the next one.
What I can say is that this is the **closest match to the account's
proven winners on every measure we have**, and it avoids each of the
three things that sank "who hits the wall". The nearest comparisons
(3 hours 20.9K, 30 minutes 15.1K, first half 16.5K) put a realistic
range at **10K to 25K**. A 135K outlier cannot be planned.

**[HOOK. Gate unchanged, secret, collision]**
"If you are running a marathon in the next few weeks, the big pasta dinner the night before can be what sends you to the portaloo on race morning, and the meal to fill up on is lunch." ⚑1 ⚑2

**[PAIN TWISTED, DREAM OUTCOME]**
"Get the last day wrong and you start heavy, tired and queuing. Get it right and you wake up light, a bit hungry and ready to race."

**[ONE. 24 HOURS OUT. Save prompt]**
"Every part of the last day, so save this. Twenty-four hours out, the most tiring thing you will do is walk. A day on your feet at the expo or around the city adds up, so get your bib and get off your legs. If you run at all, keep it to a short easy jog." ⚑3

**[TWO. LUNCH]**
"At lunch, have your biggest carb meal of the day, rice, pasta, bread or potatoes, because it has the whole afternoon and evening to go down. Then lay out your kit, bib, gels and breakfast, so nothing gets decided at five in the morning." ⚑2

**[THREE. DINNER]**
"At dinner, keep it light and early, around five or six. Fat, fibre and a big pile of protein all slow down how fast your stomach empties, so go for white rice or pasta with a plain sauce, and save the salad and anything fried for after." ⚑1

**[REHOOK]**
"And the last one is the one that panics everybody."

**[FOUR. BEDTIME]**
"At bedtime, expect to sleep badly. In a study of nearly three hundred elite athletes, almost two thirds had slept worse before a big competition, mostly from thinking about it. So bank sleep in the nights before, set two alarms, and if you lie awake, rest anyway." ⚑4

**[CTA, one sentence]**
"Save this for the day before, and the free race week guide and first go at the bar are in the bio."
