## Caption
Told everyone your marathon is just to finish, and secretly want under 4:30? Write these 4 numbers on your hand.

Your watch reads a bit long over a marathon, so run 4:30 pace on it and you'll finish around 4:33. The K signs are measured on the course, so the time on your watch as you pass each one is the real check. Within 30 seconds is on plan. More than 30 seconds behind, run the quicker end of the range until you're back.

Under 4:30: the first 10K between 6:25 and 6:30 a K on your watch (10:20 to 10:28 a mile), then between 6:10 and 6:15 (9:55 to 10:04 a mile). On your hand: about 1:05 at 10K, 2:08 at 20K, 3:10:30 at 30K and 4:13 at 40K. Anything up to 4:15 at 40K and you're under 4:30.

Under 4 (sub-4): 5:40 to 5:45 a K (9:07 to 9:15 a mile) to 10K, then 5:30 to 5:35 (8:51 to 8:59 a mile). On your hand: about 57:30, 1:54, 2:50 and 3:46.

Under 3:45: 5:20 to 5:25 a K (8:35 to 8:43 a mile) to 10K, then 5:10 to 5:15 (8:19 to 8:27 a mile). On your hand: about 54:15, 1:47, 2:39:30 and 3:32.

Under 3:30: 5:00 to 5:05 a K (8:03 to 8:11 a mile) to 10K, then 4:45 to 4:50 (7:39 to 7:47 a mile). On your hand: about 51:00, 1:39:30, 2:28 and 3:16.

Gels: a 30g gel at 20 minutes, then one every 30 minutes on the clock. If you're passing a water station, grab a bottle and take a few sips with it. That's about 60g of carbs an hour.

Want the plan for your own goal? Email us your race and goal time through the link in the bio and we'll send it to you for free.
