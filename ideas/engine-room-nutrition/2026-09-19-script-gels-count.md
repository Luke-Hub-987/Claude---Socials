## ⚑ Before filming

1. **"About ten gels."** It is arithmetic on a target and it depends on
   finish time and gel size. Say "around ten" and have the maths ready:
   roughly 60 to 90 grams an hour over three and a half to four hours,
   at about 25 grams a gel.
2. **Do not read out the Andrew Jones quote.** He is real and he worked
   on Breaking2, but the wording is unverified, and a named scientist
   quoted verbatim is the exact risk the cardiac-drift failure taught.
   Use the fact, not the quote.
3. **Do not call 2:01:09 the world record.** It has since been beaten
   ⚑. Say "his world record run in Berlin" and leave the time out.
4. **"Well over a hundred grams an hour" burned.** The 150 to 180 figure
   is at the elite end, so keep it vague rather than precise ⚑.
5. **400 to 500 grams of stored glycogen** ⚑, and the **10 to 14 day
   gut adaptation window** ⚑, which is what makes this a deadline
   rather than a tip.
6. **The failed-batch count.**
