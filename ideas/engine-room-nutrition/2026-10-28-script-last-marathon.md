## Script (293 words, 1452 characters, about 1:28)

If you're already thinking about your next marathon, the 3 biggest fixes for it are already on your watch. And none of them is more training.

Skip them, and you'll make the same mistake at the same K, and walk the same last 10K.

Or you know exactly where your next one would've gone wrong, before you've even entered it.

There are 3 numbers in there, and the third is the one nobody looks at. Send this to whoever you ran it with.

**[NUMBER 1: YOUR FIRST 10K]**
Open your splits from the last one. Was your first 10K quicker than your goal pace? If it was, that's where your race went, even if you felt great. Next time, go 5 to 10 seconds a K slower. You should get all 10 words of "I am running a marathon today and I feel good" out in one breath.

Want your next one planned from your last one? Email us your race and goal time through the link in the bio, it's free. Right, number 2.

**[NUMBER 2: YOUR FIRST GEL]**
When did you take your first gel? If it was later than 20 minutes in, or just whenever you felt you needed one, that's number 2. Next time, first gel at 20 minutes, then one every 30, by the clock.

And now the one nobody looks at.

**[NUMBER 3: THE K IT TURNED]**
Find the first K where you slowed down by more than 15 seconds. That's where your race fell apart. But it's never where it went wrong. Go back to numbers 1 and 2. That's where it went wrong.

**[YOUR NEXT ONE]**
So write those 3 numbers down. That's the runner you were last time. Fix the first 2, and you get to that K as a different runner. Save this, and do it before you pick your next race.
