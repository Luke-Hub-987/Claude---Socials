## Script v2 (324 words, 1698 characters, about 1:35)

If you're running New York, the loudest mile of the whole race is the one most likely to wreck it. And you get there straight off the quietest.

Run that mile wrong, and you end up walking up Fifth Avenue in front of the biggest crowd you'll ever see.

Hold your nerve there, and you're running into Central Park passing people.

And there's one bridge where your watch can stop making sense. What you do on it decides the rest of your race. Send this to whoever's running New York with you.

**[MILES 0-2: THE BRIDGE]**
I'll use a 4 hour marathon, and every other goal's in the caption. You start straight up a bridge, so run 9:30 a mile. You should get all 10 words of "I am running a marathon today and I feel good" out in one breath.

Want this worked out for your goal? Email us your race and goal time through the link in the bio, it's free. Right, Brooklyn.

**[MILES 2-13: BROOKLYN]**
Brooklyn is long and mostly flat. Settle at 9:10 a mile, and not a second quicker. Halfway should come up at about 2:01 on the clock. Save this for race morning.

Now the bridge, where a lot of New York runners lose their race without knowing it until mile 23.

**[MILES 15-20: THE QUIET BRIDGE, THE LOUD AVENUE]**
Around mile 15 you go onto the lower level of a bridge. It's quiet, and it climbs. Your watch can lose signal under there, so forget the pace and go by your breathing. About 5 words at a time is fine, even at 9:35. Then you come off it onto First Avenue, the loudest mile you'll ever run. Run 8:50, and no faster. Whatever you bank there, Fifth Avenue takes back.

**[MILE 20 TO THE LINE: FIFTH AVENUE]**
From mile 22, Fifth Avenue drags uphill for 2 miles. That's where everyone who rushed First Avenue starts walking. Shorter, quicker steps, around 9:15. Then into Central Park, and if you've still got 5 words, go. That's where you start passing people.

## Reaching New York runners (Luke, 29 Oct: "post on a New York VPN?")
No VPN. Instagram and TikTok show a reel to whoever they predict will watch
it, wherever they are, and a login from a new country can trigger a
security check or a locked account on the day it matters. What reaches New
York runners instead:
- **Search words:** "NYC Marathon" and "New York City Marathon" in the
  caption, and "New York" in the title on screen. TikTok also reads the
  words on screen and the audio, and "nyc marathon" was up 1000% in
  search.
- **A location tag:** New York, New York on the post (the video is about
  the city's course, so it's honest), which puts it on that place's page.
- **The gate and the send line:** "If you're running New York" filters in
  the right viewers, and they send it to whoever they're running with.
