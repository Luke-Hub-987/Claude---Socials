At elite speeds, they cut the energy you burn by about 3 to 4%, and that's real. But one study tested 16 runners near 4-hour pace, and a third used more energy in them, not less. A newer study at the same pace found runners saved about 2% on average. So for some of you they're worth minutes, but for others, almost nothing.

Save this before you open the Black Friday sales.

Borrow a pair, or buy from a shop that takes them back after a run. After that, run the same 5K loop at your easy-run heart rate on 2 different days. Do it once in your old shoes and once in the new. If your watch shows no quicker pace for the same effort, send them back, because the £250 isn't buying you anything.
