answer to a question people already had. Nothing in the raw material asks
for it now: the emails ask for 3:45 to 4:00 pacing, the comments ask for
3:30, a hydration plan, gels by carbs and what to do when gels make you
sick. "What the wall feels like" is also a description, not a method
(P127), which is the cold video's 0.9% save problem. The three wall videos
keep compounding on their own.

1. **What a top creator would do that we're not:** start from demand, not
   from our past winners. Count the questions people are actually asking,
   check what they type into search, and only then pick the format that
   wins (broad title, race clock, a method).
2. **The rawest material:** the plan emails and comments word for word;
   TikTok and YouTube search suggestions for "marathon ..."; Instagram's
   audience tab (non-follower share, top cities and so which races).
3. **Methods and tools:** tag every question since 14 Oct by theme and
   count them; TikTok search autocomplete and its "others searched for"
   list; Instagram trial reels to test two titles on non-followers.
4. **What shows it's right, other than us:** views against the channel's
   typical reel at 24 hours, emails per video, saves and follows per
   1,000 viewers, and TikTok views from search.

question from the plan emails, comments and DMs since 14 Oct and count them
