the point while you are running and it stops sloshing out. Three or

## ⚑ Before filming

1. **"More tables further along"** ⚑ is true of most big city
   marathons and worth checking against the Dublin course notes. If a
   station on the day has one table, the rule still holds as "go to
   the far end of it".
2. **"Most gels are made to go down with water"** ⚑. Standard on the
   packaging of most brands. Say "most", never "all".
3. **No numbers on water station spacing** unless he checks the
   course. "Every few K" is the safe form.
4. **"Most of it will end up on your shirt"** is observational, and
   that is how it should be delivered. No percentage.
5. **The failed-batch count** ⚑[N].
