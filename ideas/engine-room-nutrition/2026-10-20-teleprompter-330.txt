There's a right way to get that minute back, and it's slower than you'd think. Save this.

Start with 4 times. 50 minutes at 10K, 1:39 at 20K, 2:29 at 30K and 3:18 at 40K. Write them on your wrist, or put them on your lock screen.

If 3:30 isn't your goal, email us your race and goal time through the link in the bio and we'll send you your own 4 times, free. Back to 3:30.

For the first 10K, keep your watch between 4:55 and 5:00 a K. If it flicks between the two, you're spot on. You should be able to say "I am running a marathon today and I feel good", all 10 words in 1 breath, in your normal voice. 50 minutes at the 10K sign means you've nailed it.

From 10K to 30K, drop to between 4:50 and 4:55. At 20K, look for 1:39, 10 words but a bit breathy. At 30K, 2:29, and about 5 words before you need a breath.

So what do you do about that portaloo minute?

Leave it. From 10K you've been running a few seconds a K quicker than 3:30 needs, so those 4 times carry nearly 2 minutes spare. Lose 1 minute and you'll just be 1 minute behind each time on your wrist, which is fine. Go straight back to 4:50 to 4:55, hold it to 40K, then run the last 2K at 4:40 to 4:45, and you'll finish around 3:29. Lose more than 2 minutes, and take it back 5 seconds a K over the next 10K, never in 1 go.
