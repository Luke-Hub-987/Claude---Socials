## Proposal for the script chat (new principle + a gate line)
**The turn:** every script has a line, before the method, that shows the
moment something changed: what the viewer (or Luke) was doing wrong, what
they realised, said as a moment. The reviewer checks: is the turn something
the viewer didn't already believe? If they'd nod along, there's no turn,
and the method won't hold them (parkrun, 14s). And the 4 questions go in
every script file: Did I start with a moment they recognise? Did I say why it
matters? Did I show what changed? Did I give them something to do tonight?
