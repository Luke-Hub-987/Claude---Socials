# ERN on-screen text, v4 — proven shapes, no stunts

## What went wrong in v3

"Can you eat 700g of carbs the day before?" "We took 120g and ran hard
for 90 minutes." Every one was a dare. **If the OST asks a question
like that, the video is the answer, so he has to go and do it.** That
is a stunt account, and it is not sustainable at one video a week.

The library already solves this. The shapes that carry the highest
multipliers in our own research are mostly **talking heads, rankings,
and analysis** — no performance required.

---

## The format recommendation, before the lines

**Race Report Autopsy** — a numbered series where he reads a real race
report and diagnoses the fuelling.

Four proven mechanisms stack in one format:

- **Series numbering is the strongest single signal in the 82-title
  dataset.** Ross Mackay's "Building The Brand Ep. 55-64" holds 9 of
  the top 25 slots on a 1,492-view baseline, hitting 12,000-26,000.
- **Borrowed story with a real vehicle.** Named-person subjects are
  the most reliable multiplier we found (38.4x, 10.7x, 8.8x), and P40
  bans lifting them. Reading and diagnosing a report *is* the vehicle.
- **Diagnosis reversal**, the shape behind "You're Not Unfit. You're
  Underfuelled" (3.4x) — which is a fuelling title, on ERN's exact
  topic.
- **Zero production cost.** A phone, a face, a screenshot.

Supply is effectively unlimited: the research file alone has hundreds
of reports, and the fuelling detail in them is extraordinary. It makes
ERN the place that explains why races fall apart, which is the only
authority worth having before a product exists.
