# 10 story titles

## The candidates (14), before the council
Why people in finance love endurance; Why everyone's obsessed with endurance;
Why run clubs replaced the pub; Why runners pay to suffer; Why the average
marathon got slower; Why marathon ballots hit record highs; Why runners never
stop at one marathon; Why ultras are full of 40-year-olds; Why Hyrox exploded
in 2 years; Why your watch says 42.6K, not 42.2; Why runners post every run on
Strava; Why midlife men all start running; Why half of runners get injured;
Why your easy pace feels so hard.

## The council
- **Contrarian:** the finance and obsessed titles are the same boom, so in one
  poll they split the vote. Midlife men sorts viewers (row 10) and shuts out
  women. Injuries and easy pace are need topics, a how-to in disguise (P228).
  Strava every run reads as a joke (P230).
- **Assumption-ripper:** "40-year-olds" and "got slower" are claims we haven't
  checked; they die if the data doesn't hold. The average-got-slower data
  (RunRepeat) runs to 2019, so say what it measured. Don't assume finance is
  Luke's followers' world: it's his own example, so it stays, as his story.
- **Expansionist:** the identity titles are DEAD LEGS episodes and long-form
  chapters already; the poll comments hand us the audience's own words for
  the hook. Add a question sticker so people opine, not only tap.
- **Outsider:** "why the average marathon got slower" is the one I'd click
  first, because records keep getting faster. I don't know what Hyrox is,
  but anyone who does will vote. The watch one only lands if you've raced.
- **Executor:** a poll holds 4 options, so 3 slides (4, 3, 3), then a question
  sticker: "Which would you watch, and why?" Post tonight, read at 24h, the
  top vote share across the 3 polls goes to the title producer.

## Blind cross-review
- Survives: split the boom titles across polls (Contrarian + Executor); cut
  midlife men, injuries, easy pace and Strava (Contrarian, P228, P230); add
  the question sticker (Expansionist + Executor); check the two claims before
  any script (Assumption-ripper).
- Partly survives: the watch title stays as the one "seen and wondered" title,
  like 3:59 (Outsider's point noted: it's for anyone who has raced, which is
  most of the audience).
- Dropped: dropping Hyrox (it's the endurance boom the audience is in, P231).

## Chairman verdict
Post the 10 as three polls plus a question sticker, the identity poll first
(Luke's own examples point there).

Poll 1, WHO WE ARE: Why people in finance love endurance (36) / Why runners
pay to suffer (24) / Why run clubs replaced the pub (29) / Why runners never
stop at one marathon (38)

Poll 2, THE BOOM: Why everyone's obsessed with endurance (38) / Why marathon
ballots hit record highs (37) / Why Hyrox exploded in 2 years (31)

Poll 3, THE MYSTERIES: Why the average marathon got slower (34) / Why ultras
are full of 40-year-olds (35) / Why your watch says 42.6K, not 42.2 (35)

Then: "Which would you watch, and why?"

Facts to check before any script: Ironman athlete income and "running is the
new golf"; London ballot applicants; Strava Year in Sport run club growth;
the psychology of chosen suffering (Bloom, The Sweet Spot); RunRepeat's
average finish times (1986 to 2019); ultra finishers' average age; Hyrox
participant numbers; World Athletics course measurement (shortest route,
0.1% long). A title whose fact fails is dropped.
