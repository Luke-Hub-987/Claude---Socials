# Brief: What to do when you have to walk in a marathon

## OST
**WHAT TO DO WHEN YOU HAVE TO**
**WALK IN A MARATHON**

**Markers (the walk's own clock):**
1. 2 WORDS: WALK BEFORE YOU HAVE TO
2. THE FIRST 10 SECONDS
3. THE MINUTE
4. RUNNING AGAIN

## The aha (P140)
The complaint, in runners' words: "once I stop, I can't get going again."
What they don't know: **a 1-minute walk costs about 30 seconds. The race is
lost in the restart:** stopping dead, walking too long, and not getting
going again. And 10 one-minute walks over the last 10K cost about the same
as walking a single K.

## The method in numbers (answers only, no sums in the audio: P155)
1. **Walk before you have to.** From 30K, when the talk test drops to 2 or 3
   words, walk on purpose at the next station. A walk you choose is 1
   minute; a walk your legs choose is a K.
2. **The first 10 seconds.** Never stop dead: move to the side, check
   behind you, and walk fast straight away, arms bent.
3. **The minute.** 1 minute on your watch's race clock. Use it: your gel if
   it's due, 3 or 4 sips of water.
4. **Running again (the payoff).** Start running at 50 seconds, before the
   walk feels finished. The first 10 steps half your normal length, then
   run to the next lamppost. If you need another walk, run 1K, walk 1
   minute.

- **Line one:** "If you've got a marathon coming up, walking for 1 minute at
