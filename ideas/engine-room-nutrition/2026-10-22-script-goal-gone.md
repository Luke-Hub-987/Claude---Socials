## Script (341 words, about 1:42)
If you're running a marathon in the next few weeks and your watch says you're 3 minutes down at 25K, slow down. That's how a bad day still ends in a time you're proud of.

Chase it, and you're running every K faster than you trained for, and at 32K your legs just stop. You walk the last 8K while the runners you passed come back by you, and you finish 20 minutes off.

Slow down instead, and the last 5K is you picking people off, crossing the line 6 minutes off your goal with the strongest finish you've ever had.

There's a simple sum that gives you your new target, and it's the opposite of what your head tells you at 25K. Save this.

At the 25K sign, check the clock against the time you planned for 25K. For a 3:45 goal, that's about 2:13. Up to a minute down, hold your pace. 2 minutes or more, and today's goal is gone.

Want a plan with your own 25K time on it? Email us your race and goal time through the link in the bio and we'll send it, free. Back to 25K.

Ease off 10 seconds a K for the next 2K, until you can get about 5 words out in one breath again. It costs about 20 seconds and stops the slide.

Now the sum. Take the minutes you're down, double them, and add that to your goal. 3 minutes down on a 3:45 makes your new target 3:51.

So why double it, when you're only 3 minutes down?

Because if you're 3 down at 25K, you've been fading, and a fade doesn't stop on its own. Doubling gives you room to run the last 15K steady instead of hanging on. Your new pace is about 10 seconds a K slower than your goal pace. For 3:51, that's between 5:25 and 5:30 on your watch. Hold it, one K at a time, and if you've still got 5 words at 37K, let it go for the last 5K.
