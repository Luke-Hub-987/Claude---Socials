# The 16k video

## Why 16k is the idea, not a detail

16k is the most-run "long run" distance there is for club runners, and
at 5:30-6:00/km it lands at **88 to 96 minutes** — sitting exactly on
the 90-minute line where fuelling guidance kicks in. So it is the one
run where people genuinely do not know whether to bother, and almost
nobody does.

The reason they skip it is in the research, verbatim, from a real
runner: *"Almost never took any gels during less than 20km runs, just
because it was a bit too expensive."*

**That is the whole video.** Painfully specific distance, a reason
every single viewer has had, and a cost that shows up after the run
rather than during it, which is why nobody connects the two.

The bar is the vehicle at km 8. It is not the subject (P57).

## On-screen text options

**1. 16k is the exact distance where you stop getting away with not fuelling** ⚑
*(and Sunday afternoon is where you find out)*
Lead option. Rung four: the exact distance, the exact afternoon. The
identity is "someone whose long run is 16k", which is most of the
audience, so recognition is immediate.

**2. Nobody fuels a 16k, and £3 a gel is why**
Money painkiller, and the reason is verbatim from a real runner rather
than a guess. Highest "that is me" of the batch.

**3. 10/10 reasons runners skip fuel on a 16k, and the one that costs them** ⚑
The confession variant of the 10/10 format.

**4. 88 minutes. One bar. No gels.**
Ogilvy compression — three facts, the conclusion left to the reader.
Weakest on stakes, strongest as a thumbnail.

**5. The long run where "I'll just eat after" stops working** ⚑
Self-incrimination, and it quotes the thought they actually have.

- **B-roll that earns its place:** unwrapping mid-stride, chewing while
  breathing hard, the watch showing 8.0k. The difficulty of eating
  solid food at pace is the honest part of this product and should not
  be edited out.
