and still hit it, because there is a second wall: the leg damage from
long strides on tired legs and hammering the downhills. It is held back

## Caption (v2: the first line keeps the hidden wall hidden)

How to run a marathon without hitting the wall. You can take every gel on time and still hit it at 32K, because there are two walls, and most runners only plan for one.

0 to 10K: pay the speed tax. Run 5 to 10 seconds per K slower than your goal pace, so 5:35 to 5:40 for a 5:30 goal. Even a little too fast this early burns your fuel far quicker than it feels. You should be able to say "I am running a marathon today and I feel good" in one breath. First gel at 20 minutes.

10 to 25K: run the gel clock. One gel every 20 minutes by your watch, never by feel, with about half a cup of water. That is roughly 60 to 75g of carbs an hour, or about 11 gels for a four-hour marathon. That handles the first wall, the fuel wall.

At 25K, the wall check. Are your gels on schedule, and can you still get five words out in one breath? If either is off, ease off 10 seconds per K for 2K, while it still costs you nothing.

25K to the line: the hidden wall is in your legs. Long strides on tired legs, especially downhill, turn them to concrete however much fuel is in you. Shorten your step, quicken your feet, take hills shorter and slower, and let the downhills roll you forward from your ankles. Then take it one K at a time.

Want a plan built around your race and goal time? Email us your race and goal time through the link in the bio and we will send you a free personalised plan.

Practise your gels in training first. Dizzy, chest pain or confused at any point: stop and go to the medical team.

## Luke's science (15 Oct), checked

**Kept:** two causes of the wall, fuel and legs (the counter-position);
5-10 s/K slower for the first 10K ("the speed tax"); a little too fast
early burns fuel much faster; gels every 20 minutes by the clock, never
by feel, with water; 20-25g each (in the caption as 60-75g an hour);
shorter, quicker steps; hills shorter and slower; downhills rolling
from the ankles without overstriding; the ten-word talk test instead of
watch data.

**Changed or cut:**
1. **"Not a test of mental toughness ... it is a coordination failure":**
   the banned "not X, it is Y" shape. Said as "there are two walls".
2. **"In laboratory testing ...": cut** (study language, P105).
3. **"The brain forces a shutdown", "central nervous system", "emergency
   brake", "protect your vital organs", "green-light signals": cut**
   (governor claims, banned since the wall video).
4. **"5 s/K faster drains fuel twice as fast", "2-3% faster ...
   exponentially": cut** (statistics, and "twice" overstates it). Said
   as the wall video did: "burns your fuel far quicker than it feels".
5. **"Most runners do not hit a metabolic wall": softened** to "the one
   people forget is in your legs". No proportion claimed (P107).
6. **Cadence 175-180: cut.** Cadence depends on height and pace; a fixed
   number would be wrong for many. "Shorter, quicker steps" as every
   earlier video.
7. **150ml water: "about half a cup"** (the size of most race cups).
8. **Optical heart rate locking onto cadence: cut** (heart rate left out
   since 14 Oct as too individual).
9. **New, ours, consistent:** the 25K wall check (gels, five words,
   thighs; ease off 10 s/K for 2K), built from first 10 minutes ("ease
   off now while it costs nothing") and how hard (five words at 20-30K).
