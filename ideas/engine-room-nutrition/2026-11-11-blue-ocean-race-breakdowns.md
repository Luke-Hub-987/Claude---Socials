# Our blue ocean: the race, broken down

## The position
Others sell the lifestyle, the fast sessions, the elite paces. **We break a race
down so you can run it**: K by K, minute by minute, what to do and when. That's
what our winners were (the wall at every 10K, 7 days before, the first 10
minutes, the 3:45 at every 10K). The structure stays. What changes is the
packaging around it: a title that comes alive, and value inside 15 seconds.

## The rule: a structural body in a title with a pulse
Keep the breakdown dead simple; put the life in the title. A vivid image or an
against-the-grain frame on top of a practical plan.

## The hook, shorter: value by 0:15
**What the curves say:** about half gone by 0:03 and 70 to 80% by 0:08 to 0:10 on
the last two posts, then flat to the end. The body holds; the front leaks. Our
hook runs 80 to 95 words (about 28 seconds) before stage 1.

**Proposal (for the gate chat: it changes C4, P147 and the hook-stack rule):**
- **40 to 55 words before stage 1, about 15 seconds.**
- Line one (the viewer, the gap) → **one sentence of pain and dream together** →
  the named concept with "save this" → stage 1.
- The turn goes inside line one or stage 1, not as its own paragraph.
- The CTA stays after stage 1, one sentence.
- Example on #2: "If you've got a marathon coming up, the last 10K is where you get
  passed or do the passing. What you do at 32K decides which. We call
  it the chase plan, so save this." Then [32K TO 37K].
