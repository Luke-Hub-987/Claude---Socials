## What works across all of them
1. **Who you become, or lose.** Nerves of steel, a lethal gas tank, harder to kill, athletic freak, not a war hero, rare. The viewer gets an identity they want, or loses one they thought they had. Our winners did the second (DIE, EVEN THE FIT ONES).
2. **The bracket is the second hook.** (progressively more brutal), (Even while working a 9-5), (yet), (91.3% of People Are Wasting Their Time), (My 2027 Framework), (Full Masterclass). It adds an escalation, a constraint, a stat or a format, never the answer. Ours: (SECRETLY), (EVEN THE FIT ONES), (SUNDAY LEAGUE).
3. **Specific beats clever.** £500 three times; 2 mins 3 seconds; 91.3%; 10/10; New York City. An exact number makes it believable and makes it theirs.
4. **One idea in 3 to 7 words at the top**, with a named concept they can repeat (Sweet Spot, Brain Rot, the arc, Tendon Maxing).
5. **A picture strip above the title in 8 of the 12.** Three images that make the idea concrete in a quarter of a second, the title in a box under them, the creator below. They use famous faces; that's what makes it read so fast.
6. **Title case or sentence case in a box**, often serif, never shouting. Brandon is the exception (one word in capitals: FREAK, PLYOMETRICS, NOT).
7. **The spoken opener carries the hot take:** "I've got a hot take, and I don't think most of you..." (twice), "Okay, I know this sounds...". The title and the first line are two hooks, not one said twice.
8. **The comment-word CTA:** "Comment 'training'" (2,302 comments), "DM me 'Clean Fuel'". Comments and DMs feed reach and build a list.
9. **Saves come from lists and frameworks** (27.5K, 47.7K): 10 questions, 3 habits, a masterclass. Sends come from identity ("that's my mate": RARE 11.2K, the arc 29.6K).

## What we take, and what we can't
| Take | Not ours to copy |
|---|---|
| Identity words for runners (a lethal last 10K, harder to break, the 4-hour sweet spot) and the "who you lose" side we already win with | Famous faces in the strip: licensing, and G27 (no named athletes). **Test the strip with our own images instead:** Luke's race photos, the drawn scenes, a watch, a gel, a clock |
| The bracket as a constraint, stat or escalation: (EVEN WITH A 9-5), (EACH ONE MORE BRUTAL), (IT'S NOT WEAKNESS) | Made-up precision: every stat in a bracket is in FACTS, or it's out |
| One exact number, repeated if it helps (the £500 move) | Swearing in the title: Luke's call, not the default (brand voice) |
| A named concept they can repeat | Title case over our capitals: **a trial A/B first, not a switch** (our capitals white card won at 26.8% skip) |
