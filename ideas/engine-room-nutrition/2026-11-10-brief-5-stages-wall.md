## The 5 stages (draft names, Luke's call; each with what you feel and the action)
1. **The K you explain away:** same effort, the watch says 10 seconds a K
   slower. Action: check the clock at the next 10K sign.
2. **The maths:** you start working out what pace you need for the rest.
   Action (the payoff): sugar in now (the gel clock: 30g at 20 minutes, then
   every 30 on the clock, FACTS), a few sips at the next station, and 15 to
   20 seconds a K slower for 10 minutes.
3. **The bargain:** "walk to the next station." Action: one lamppost at a
   time (the lamppost rule).
4. **The legs go:** heavy, short steps. Action: shorter, quicker steps.
5. **The shuffle:** walk-run to the line. Action: run to a lamppost, walk 20
   steps, repeat; the clock, not the watch.
