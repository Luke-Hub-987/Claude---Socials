## Script (310 words, about 1:40, 1,522 characters)

If you're running a marathon in the next few weeks, 32K can be the best part of your race, legs gone and all, and it's all down to one text you send the night before.

You know the long run where you stared at your watch for 2 hours and remember nothing? Race like that and you miss your own marathon.

Or you finish with your head up, already planning the next one.

Save this, and send it to whoever's on the start line with you.

**[0-10K: LET THEM GO]**
If the first K feels exciting, you're too fast. Get all 10 words of "I am running a marathon today and I feel good" out in one breath, or ease off and let them go. You'll pass them at 32K.

Want your race planned K by K? Email us your race and goal time through the link in the bio, free. Back to 10K.

**[10-20K: HEAD UP]**
10 to 20K is the best you'll feel all day, and most runners spend it staring at their wrist. Check your watch once a K, at the marker, and nowhere else. In between, read a sign and high-five a kid. After 20K, it gets quiet.

**[20-30K: YOUR NAME]**
The night before, write your first name on the front of your top in black marker, letters as tall as your hand. When it goes quiet in your head, strangers shout it. Wave back.

And then there's the text, the reason 32K can be the best part.

**[32K: YOUR PEOPLE]**
Text whoever's coming to watch you. Skip the finish, stand at 32K, and tell them which side of the road and what colour top you're in. The finish is packed anyway. 32K is where it goes quiet and your legs go, so from 30K you're running to them, one K at a time. Send this to them now. And if nobody's coming, your name does the same job.

**If it runs past 1:40:** cut "Wave back." and "legs gone and all," (6 words).
