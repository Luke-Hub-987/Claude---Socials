## The script (313 words, 1,562 characters, about 1:30)

"If you've got a marathon this spring, it probably won't go wrong at 34K, where your legs go. It'll go wrong about 20K before that, at the moment you feel best."

"That's how people finish a few minutes off their goal and can't work out where the minutes went. Get this one moment right and you're the one passing people in the last 8K."

"It lasts about 4K, and it feels like the best part of your race."

**[0-10K: A MINUTE DOWN]**

"For sub-4, start between 5:45 and 5:50 a K. You'll pass the 10K sign in about 58 minutes, roughly a minute behind sub-4 pace. That's the plan, even though it'll feel like you owe the clock a minute."

"If you want these numbers for your own race and goal, email us through the link in the bio and we'll send you a free plan."

**[THE FIRST DOWNHILL]**

"Then the road drops away and your legs feel brand new, so you run 20 seconds a K quicker to win the minute back in one go. That's the moment. It feels free, and you pay for it later. On every downhill, take shorter, quicker steps and keep it between 5:35 and 5:40, the same as the flat."

**[10-30K: SLOWLY]**

"From 10K to 30K, that's your pace: a few seconds a K faster than sub-4 pace, never 20. You're at halfway in about 2:00, and by 30K, around 2:50, the minute's back without you feeling it go."

"So why do so many people's legs still go at 34K?"

**[34K: WHERE IT SHOWS]**

"Because 34K is where a surge from 20K earlier gets paid for. If you've saved it, you settle at goal pace, between 5:40 and 5:45, one K at a time, and finish around 3:59. Take your first gel at 20 minutes and about 60g of carbs an hour after that, all the way round."

"Send this to whoever's chasing a time with you this spring."

## Caption
Most marathons don't go wrong at 34K. They go wrong about 20K earlier, on the first downhill, when you feel great and run 20 seconds a K quicker to win back the minute you were told to give away. Here's how to take it back slowly instead.

Sub-4: the first 10K between 5:45 and 5:50 a K (9:15 to 9:23 a mile), about 58 minutes at 10K. From 10K to 35K between 5:35 and 5:40 (8:59 to 9:07), halfway in about 2:00 and 30K in about 2:50. Then between 5:40 and 5:45 (9:07 to 9:15) to the line, around 3:59.

3:45: the first 10K between 5:25 and 5:30 (8:43 to 8:51 a mile). From 10K to 35K between 5:15 and 5:20 (8:27 to 8:35), halfway in about 1:53 and 30K in about 2:40. Then goal pace to the line, around 3:45.

4:30: the first 10K between 6:30 and 6:35 (10:28 to 10:36 a mile). From 10K to 35K between 6:15 and 6:20 (10:04 to 10:12), halfway in about 2:15 and 30K in about 3:11. Then goal pace to the line, just under 4:30.

On every downhill, shorter, quicker steps and the same pace. First gel at 20 minutes, then about 60g of carbs an hour. Want it worked out for your race? Email us your race and goal time through the link in the bio and we'll send you a free plan.
