## TOP OF FUNNEL

**1. 10/10 uncomfortable fuelling rules of runners who finish the last 10k faster than the first**
His own 2,156 shape. The identity is a negative split, which is the
single most celebrated outcome in the whole research file, and it
replaces the cliché "never hit the wall".

**2. Every fuelling plan online was written for a 2:30 marathoner. You'll be out there for 4:30.**
*(the 4 things that change)*
The gate he picked, sharpened. Two clock times, and the cost of
scrolling is stated: you are following a plan built for someone who
finishes two hours before you.

**3. The 20-minute portaloo queue that decides your first 5k**
The most universal pre-race scene there is, and nobody films it. Real
in the research (*"the most important thing, to take a good ol' DUMP"*
and the porta-potty hobble). Lands directly on ERN's window.

**4. Why the third gel is still in your shorts at 30k** ⚑
Self-incrimination, and a real mechanism nobody explains. Needs ⚑ a
sourced reason before filming.

**5. He took a gel at mile 20 that had never once caused a problem in training**
*(Race Report Autopsy, Ep 1)*
Verbatim: *"took 40g per hour and felt fine up until mile 20 or so. I
took a GU at that point and had some stomach issues from it which never
happened to me during training."*

**6. £⚑[X] of gels for one marathon, and ⚑[N] of them come back up** ⚑
The money painkiller, the shape of the £200 carbon shoes line he
approved. Needs a real basket priced and a real number.

**7. 10/10 things runners choke down at 6am on race morning** ⚑
Named hour, and every item is a photographable object: porridge, three
plain bagels, a banana with peanut butter, black coffee.

**8. Ranking every gel in the shop D to S, by what's still fine at 30k**
Tier list, a proven format lever we have never used, with the ranking
criterion named so it is not a taste test.

**9. What a 2:30 marathoner eats at 6:30am on race morning** ⚑
"What a $1B CEO Actually Does in a Day" (16.6x), with the clock in it.
