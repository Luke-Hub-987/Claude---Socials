# Brief: the pro's easy run, scaled (series entry 2; P138, Luke's yes before the script)

**The method in numbers:** one 30-minute easy run in 3 parts, each a little quicker, never past your ceiling (Kipchoge's shape: start slow, finish faster, still well under race pace).
| Goal | First 10 min | Next 10 min | Last 10 min (ceiling) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3:00 | 5:20 | 4:57 | 4:38 |
| 3:30 | 6:11 | 5:45 | 5:22 |
| 3:45 | 6:36 | 6:08 | 5:44 |
| sub-4 | 7:01 | 6:32 | 6:06 |
| 4:30 | 7:51 | 7:18 | 6:51 |

**Line one (draft):** "Eliud Kipchoge starts his easy runs at about 5 to 6 minutes a K, which is your sub-4 marathon pace."
**Pain:** your easy runs drift quicker than his start, and the Sunday long run and the race pay for it.
**Dream:** easy runs that leave you fresh for the 2 that matter.
**Email:** comment EASY for your 3 paces and how many easy runs a week.

**Facts used:** FACTS "Kipchoge's easy runs, and your easy pace" (Canadian Running video, 2021; his 2017 log via Running Scientist; Daniels' formula for the band, worked out). His exact pace in the video wasn't measured from a watch: said as "about".

**Vetting note:** scaling his easy-to-race ratio would put a sub-4 runner's easy run near 10:00/km at the start, which is wrong (an elite's race pace sits nearer his maximum). So the band comes from Daniels' formula, not from his ratio.
