If you do a long run at the weekend, the first half should feel almost too easy, and if it doesn't, that's why the last few K fall apart.

Here's the test we use for race day, and it works just as well on a Sunday.

Say this sentence out loud: I am running a marathon today and I feel good. That's 10 words.

For the first half, you should get all 10 out in one breath, in your normal voice. If you can't, slow down until you can, even if the pace on your watch looks too slow.

In the second half, it's fine if the 10 words come out a bit breathy. That's the run doing its job.

If you're down to 5 words before the last 20 minutes, slow down until the 10 come back, and you'll finish the last K in control instead of hanging on.
