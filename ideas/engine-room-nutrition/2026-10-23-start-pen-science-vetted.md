## What's new enough to carry a video
Stand in the middle, calf raises and marching, GPS locked 10 minutes out,
double knots. The rest is already said (cold, 30 minutes before, first 10
minutes). On its own that's a checklist, not a fear: it fits as one stage
of a race-morning video, or the caption of the sleep video's Saturday
stage, more than a video of its own.
