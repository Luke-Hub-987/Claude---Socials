## Script

"Running the Valencia Marathon in December? Its gels start at 16.2K, over an hour after your first should go in. You'll feel fine until 30K, then pay for that hour. So we built the Valencia clock around your goal time instead."

**[THE FIRST HOUR]**

"The course hands out its first gel at 16.2K, but nothing before it. Your own gel goes in at 20 minutes, then one every 30, with a few sips if you're passing a station. On a sub-4, the first course gel reaches you at about 1:33. Save this and screenshot the clock, so it's on your phone in Valencia."

**[THE COURSE GELS]**

"The other course gels come at 25.8K and 34K, so treat them as spares. They're a brand you may never have tried, so race day is no day to test your stomach. If you've trained with them, swap one in for the gel that's due. The sports drink cups carry some sugar too, so have them between gels, never with one. The course gels are spares, your clock is the plan."

"Send this to whoever's running Valencia with you, so you're both on the same clock."

**[31K TO 34K]**

"From 31K, the course runs away from the finish for 3K, out to the Bioparc, just as your legs want home. On a sub-4 you'll hit 31K at about 2:56, just after a gel. So hold your pace one K at a time, because the turn is close."

"And the last course gel is waiting at the exact spot it turns."

**[THE TURN FOR HOME]**

"At 34K, the Bioparc, the course heads back towards the finish and hands out its last gel. That's at about 3:13, just before your own 3:20 one. Take one or the other, never both. From there it's about 8K to the blue carpet, so you'll be counting down the signs instead of hanging on."

"Follow, and run Valencia on your clock, not the course's."

## Caption
Valencia's course gels come at 16.2K, 25.8K and 34K. Your own first gel is due at 20 minutes, so the first course gel arrives over an hour late: about 67 minutes on a 3:45, 73 on a sub-4 and 84 on a 4:30.

The Valencia clock: your own 30 g gel at 20 minutes, then one every 30 minutes, none in the last 20, with a few sips of water if you're passing a station (33cl bottles from 5.5K). Have the sports drink cups between gels, never with one. With 20 to 25 g gels, one every 20 minutes; with 40 g gels, every 40. The course gels are spares: only take them if you've trained with that brand, and never two gels at once.

When the course gels reach you (course pace from our plans): 3:45, about 1:27, 2:18 and 3:02. Sub-4, about 1:33, 2:27 and 3:13. 4:30, about 1:44, 2:45 and 3:36. From 31K the course runs 3K away from the finish to the Bioparc, then turns back at 34K: about 2:46 and 3:02 on a 3:45, 2:56 and 3:13 on a sub-4, 3:18 and 3:36 on a 4:30.

Valencia is on Sunday 6 December, 8:15 start.
