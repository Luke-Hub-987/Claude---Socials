# What to do in the second half of a marathon

## The Zero to One secret

Everyone treats the second half as hanging on. For a runner who held
back early, it is where the race is won: hold goal pace to 35K, then
go, while the people around you slow down. The minute you let go in
the first half is the minute you take back after 35K.

**[1. HALFWAY: CHECK, DON'T CHASE]**
"First, the halfway mat. You want to be up to a minute behind your goal, still able to say a full sentence. If you are behind, do not try to win it back now. If you are ahead, ease off while it is still cheap." ⚑2

**[2. 21-30K: HOLD AND FUEL]**
"From halfway to thirty K, settle on five thirty exactly. Keep taking a gel every twenty minutes with water, even when you do not feel you need one, because what you take now is what you run on after thirty-five." ⚑3

**[3. 30-35K: SHORT, QUICK STEPS]**
"Thirty to thirty-five is where the same pace starts to feel harder. Shorten your stride and quicken your feet, so each landing is lighter on tired legs, and hold five thirty. Holding pace here is the win." ⚑4

**[REHOOK]**
"And the last one is where the negative split pays off."

**[4. 35K TO THE LINE: NOW GO]**
"At thirty-five K, check yourself. If you can still say a full sentence, drop to five twenty-five a K, and go a little quicker again for the last two. That is the minute from the first half coming back. If you cannot, hold five thirty and take it one K at a time." ⚑5

## The worked example (caption)

For a 5:30/K goal (3:52 marathon):
- Halfway: up to a minute behind goal, full-sentence test. Behind? Don't chase. Ahead? Ease off.
- 21-30K: 5:30 exactly. Gel every 20 min with water.
- 30-35K: shorter, quicker steps. Hold 5:30.
- 35K to the line: full sentence? 5:25/K, quicker for the last 2K. If not, hold 5:30, one K at a time.
Any goal: up to a minute behind at halfway, goal pace to 35K, then about 5 seconds a K faster.
