# Brief: drink your gel, don't eat it

**The aha:** the gel isn't the problem, the texture is. Squeeze it into the
water at the station and it stops being a gel. A 30g gel in a 330ml bottle
is a strong sports drink (about 9%); in a 500ml bottle, about the same as
the sports drink at the station (6%). In a cup, it's a sweet drink you're
done with in a few seconds. Same 30g, nothing thick to get down.

**Method (P127), on the schedule we already give (FACTS.md):** first 30g gel
at 20 minutes, then one every 30 minutes (60g an hour).
1. Time the gel for a station (the schedule is a guide, give or take 5
   minutes to land on water).
2. Bottle: cap off, squeeze the whole gel in, cap on, shake, then drink it
   over the next K or two. Finish it before the next gel.
3. Cup: squeeze the gel in, swirl, drink it walking the length of the tables.
4. No station near? Take it the old way, half now, half 2-3 minutes later,
   with water at the next station.
5. Practise once on an easy run this week (nothing new on race day).

**Line one:** "If you're running a marathon in the next few weeks, you
should never eat another gel again, you should drink it, and that's how
the late ones stop being a fight."
**Pain:** at 30K, the thick gel you have to hack down between breaths, then
the sloshing, then walking.
**Dream:** at 38K, you're sipping something that tastes like a sports
drink, and you're still passing people.
**Held reveal:** why it works: same carbs, no texture, and it's going in
with the water it needs anyway (gels are made to be taken with water).

**Facts used:** 30g gel, every 30 minutes, 60g an hour (FACTS.md); water
with every gel, never with sports drink (stomach problems, water stations:
mixed into water is the right way round); texture complaints and "hack it
down" (the emails); stations hand out water (cups or bottles depending on
the race).

**Risks:**
- Bottles or cups vary by race: say both.
- Opening a bottle mid-run is fiddly: it's a few seconds walking, which
  the 4:30 video already made normal.
- Not something they've done: one easy run this week to try it.
- Concentration in a small cup is still high; the cup version is for
  people who can't face the texture, not the main method.
