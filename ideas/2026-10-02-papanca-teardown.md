## What it does that we can use

1. **Identity plus pain in the title.** "Race like a pro" is who they
   want to be; "avoid blowing up" is what they fear. Our titles carry
   the fear or the moment, never the identity.
2. **Every rule is a named, two or three word command.** "Start too
   easy." "Chop distance." Screenshot-able, so saveable. Our markers are
   distances; they could be distance plus rule.
3. **No statistics, no studies. Feelings.** "Almost embarrassed."
   "Having fun instead of hanging on." Exactly Luke's P105 and P107.
4. **Race strategy and the head, not physiology.** The same family as
   goal pace (24.8K) and first half (17.5K), our best non-wall videos.
5. **Counter-intuitive commands.** "Start too easy" is a Thiel secret in
   three words.

## What we must not do

Copy it. Briar: a copied hook is already stale. Their rules are generic
across distances (it is a half marathon, "21k"); ours sit on the
marathon clock, with fuel inside and the wall at stake.
