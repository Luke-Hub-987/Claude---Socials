## What it says
1. **Once people got past the front, the body held better than our last four.**
   13.6s watched against 9.7 to 10.1s; 3.05% watched it all, double the last
   two; 3 follows, the most on any ERN TikTok this month. The new information
   (the trial, the watch data, the 30-day check) kept the people who stayed.
2. **The front lost them in the first second, so it was the picture, not the
   words.** At 0:01 nobody has heard line one. What they saw:
   - **The title was small, in the middle and low contrast:** a mid-blue box on
     Luke's chest, over a blue-grey T-shirt, with type about half the size of
     every example Luke sent. Every one of them puts the title in the top band,
     full width, in black on white or white on dark, so you read it in one glance.
   - **A cartoon runner filled the bottom half from 0:00.** The script said
     "Luke and the title only; no graphics until stage 1". This is the third post
     running where frame one wasn't what the brief asked for (ledger row 22;
     after the sub-4:30 crowd photo and the ranking tier board). The cartoon also
     says "kids' explainer" before the title can say "your injury".
3. **The title has no "you" and no feeling.** FAILED ITS ONLY TRIAL is a claim
   about a study. It's true, it's opinionated, and it doesn't contradict the
   payoff, but nothing in it is the viewer's body or the viewer's race. Every
   example Luke sent today is about the viewer becoming or losing something
   (NERVES OF STEEL, IS RARE, THEY DON'T TELL YOU). **My miss:** v3's pre-mortem
   fixed the contradiction and the split STOP, but swapped the viewer out for
   the study. Line one carried "Building for a spring marathon?", but at 0:01
   nobody has heard it yet.
4. **What I can't separate (one post, P174):** it went up at 8:47pm, not the
   planned 7am; it's only TikTok so far; the 0:01 drop-off is common to every
