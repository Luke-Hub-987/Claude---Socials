## Read

1. **Instagram split in two: the front failed, the body worked.** Skip
   40.8% against 31.9% for how hard. Saves 2.5% (Higher) and 41 shares:
   the people who stayed kept it and sent it.
2. **Why the front failed (two causes, both visible):**
   - **The gate narrowed the audience.** "If you are trying to break
     four hours" tells everyone else to swipe, as Dublin did. On
     Instagram, where reach comes from non-followers in the Reels tab,
     a narrow gate costs skip.
   - **The cover was a crowd with faces, under a "SUB 3:45" pacer flag.**
     Crowds ran 36% to 38% skip before; and the flag's number
     contradicts the title's.
3. **TikTok is the opposite: goal-time content is its best format.**
   9.5K views, 128 saves, 14 comments. TikTok works more like search,
   and "sub 4 marathon" is what people type.
4. **Length 1:30 held 30s (33%):** better than how hard's 27% at 1:48.

## Lessons
- **Instagram: keep the gate broad** ("If you are running a marathon in
  the next few weeks"). Narrow gates (Dublin, sub-4) cap reach there.
- **TikTok: goal-time videos are a format to repeat** (sub-3:30,
  sub-4:30), even when they would be too narrow for Instagram.
- **A number on the cover must match the number in the title.**
