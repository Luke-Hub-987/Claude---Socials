## Luke's diagnosis, checked against the data: he's right
1. **No specificity.** Our winners all had a famous thing or a number in
   the title: the wall, 7 days, the first 10 minutes, 3:45, carb-load.
   "How hard your long run should feel" has neither.
2. **No time pressure.** The winners went up 1 to 4 weeks before the
   viewer's race. A long run is every week for 5 months: nothing is lost
   by scrolling past it today.
3. **Nothing new.** Every runner has heard "run your long run easy". The
   10-word test is ours, but the title never said so.
4. **The pond.** In November most marathoners don't have a race in the
   next month. The match reel, in a different pond, stopped people
   (26.2%); the familiar marathon lure didn't (55.5%).

This repeats across posts, so it's a ledger row now (row 27, P178):
the long run 55.5%, where your marathon goes wrong 52.1%, what your last
marathon says 52.4%, taper 43.1%, heavy legs (0.5% saves). Every one of
them had a general title with no number, no deadline and nothing new.
