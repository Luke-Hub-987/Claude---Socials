## The spine of the script

The counter-position, stated plainly: **the last three long runs are
not fitness any more. The fitness is banked. They are a dress
rehearsal, and almost nobody uses them as one.**

Three named things to rehearse, each with its own consequence:

1. **The fuel.** Exactly what you will take on the day, at the exact
   timing, in the exact amounts. The last chance to find out your
   stomach hates it while it still costs you nothing.
2. **The morning.** Same wake-up time, same breakfast, same gap before
   you start. You are testing the timing, not the food.
3. **The kit.** Everything you will wear and carry, including the
   shoes, so nothing on race day is new.

Handover to the existing videos: "and the three hours and the thirty
minutes before the gun both have their own video on the profile."

CTA: save it, plus the two doors.
