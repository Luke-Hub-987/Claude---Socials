## Lessons
- **Keep the motion graphics: the opening frame shows the hook** (the
  number or the moment), then each stage's numbers on screen.
- **Say the send prompt in the audio** on race-weekend videos.
- **Last stage = the payoff number,** with the why in one line before it.
