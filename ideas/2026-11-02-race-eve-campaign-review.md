## The 4:05 race, the most useful data point
First 11K at 5:42 to 5:56 a K (on our plan: 5:45 to 5:50), then a surge
at 12 to 16K (5:33 down to 5:24, faster than 3:48 pace) to win back the
time, steady at about 5:43 to 33K, then 6:08 to 6:22 from 34K. The slow
start was the plan working; the surge to claw it back broke the race.
