## Read: enjoy
1. **The body was one of our best.** A quarter of viewers were still there
   at 1:20 on a 1:32 video, and the line is flat from the CTA to the end.
   The rehook and the payoff held. The P149 open loop ("one text you send
   the night before") did the job without a reframe.
2. **The front improved on sleep, but it isn't fixed.** Skip 38.2%
   against sleep's 41.4%. That's still well off goal gone's 31.9%, and
   enjoy's line one was a promise ("can be the best part") rather than a
   lived fear. That matches why-winners-win, which scored enjoy "3 as an
   idea, 5 built right".
3. **The CTA cost a third of who was left**, from 38% to 26%. This is the
   third clear CTA step (4:30 at 0:40, sleep at 0:49, enjoy at 0:42). It's
   now a pattern. Whether it pays depends on the email count, which only
   Luke has.
4. **The save and send prompt at the end of the hook cost about 7
   points.** It's the first time that step has been this visible. It could
   be because there was nothing yet to save at 0:23, when the viewer hadn't
   had any value.
5. **Saves 1.2%: nothing too long to remember.** The four actions (10
   words, watch once a K, name on your top, people at 32K) are easy to
   remember, so nobody needs to save them. Carb load (4.4%) and 7 days
   (3.0%) had schedules with times and amounts you can't hold in your head.
6. **It stopped after day one.** The posted gate was "this weekend or the
   weekends to come". Once Sunday passed, "this weekend" was over, and most
   of the audience's races had gone with it (the race-weekend plan, point
   5). That's dated audio (P82), and a race-weekend topic, so it doesn't
   follow the usual lifetime multiplier.
7. **Shares 0.4% from two send prompts.** Better than sleep and goal gone,
   below Dublin (1.8%). The 32K text is something to do, not something to
   send.

   hand from the plan. Nothing in the first frame shows the hook as a

   Outsider said this before posting: most of the reach is non-followers,
   and they don't know Luke or Jamie. A story needs a stake a stranger gets
   in 3 seconds. "My co-founder" isn't one.

   problem was the door.
5. **Posted on race day, not Monday.** P144: posting time isn't a reason.
   Not used as one.

## LLM Council on the lessons (P150)
- **Contrarian:** Don't read too much into two videos posted over a race
  weekend, when the audience was running, travelling and celebrating. The
  enjoy plateau may be the calendar, not the content.
- **Assumption-ripper:** We assume the CTA step is a cost. If the CTA
  brought emails, 12 points of retention at 0:42 may be a good trade. We
  don't have the email count. We also assume "saves need a schedule"; enjoy
  had numbers. The real test is whether the viewer could remember it all
  without saving.
- **Expansionist:** The surprise's flat body and 13.6% TikTok completion
  show that a story holds people. Use it as the *opening* of an advice
  video ("Jamie ran his first..." then the method), not as a standalone.
  And enjoy's body (25% at 1:20) shows the 4-stage race clock with a held
  payoff still works.
- **Outsider:** "I didn't know who these lads were, so I scrolled." "I
  watched the enjoy one, but I didn't need to save it, I'd remember it."
- **Executor:** Three changes for the next script: a lived fear in line
  one (not a promise); something too long to remember (a schedule or
  table) so it's saved; ask Luke for the emails per video so the CTA
  trade can be decided. And no dated words in the gate.
- **Blind cross-review:** All five agree on the opening frame (a repeat)
  and on "too long to remember" as the save driver. The Contrarian's
  calendar point is accepted as part of the enjoy plateau, not all of it
  (the skip and save rates aren't affected by the calendar). The CTA stays
  until the emails are counted (Luke's rule, P128).
- **Chairman:** Add three new principles (P151 to P153) and ledger rows 18
  to 20. Keep the CTA and get the emails counted. Next video: back to the
  proven ground (a lived race fear on the race clock), with a schedule to
  keep.

## Lessons
- **P151:** a story stands or falls on a stake a stranger gets in 3
  seconds, shown in the opening frame. On ERN, use a story as the opening
  of an advice video, not on its own.
- **P152:** a save needs something too long to remember: a schedule, a
  table, times and amounts.
- **P153:** no dated words in the gate ("this weekend"). The video goes
  stale the day the weekend ends.
- **The CTA step is now a pattern** (3 videos). It's kept, with emails
  counted per video so the trade can be judged (ledger row 9 updated).
- **The save prompt before any value** (0:23) cost about 7 points. Test the
  save prompt after stage 1 instead, once there's something to save.
