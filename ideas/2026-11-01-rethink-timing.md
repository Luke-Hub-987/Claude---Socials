## What that says
**It's mostly timing.** Up to 18 October almost everything we posted did 25K
to 150K. From 20 October nothing has, and that's 11 reels in a row across
every format we have: goal times, a fear, a feeling, a story, a course map,
motion graphics, a real email, the car, the house, a stock photo, one in
capitals and one not. Their skip rates ranged from 33% to 52%, and their
reach barely moved (about 2K to 6K at 5 hours). When completely different
videos land in the same place, the thing they share is the date.

The drop came a week before Dublin, after the big autumn marathons outside
Ireland had been run, and it kept falling after Dublin and New York. The
pool of people about to run a marathon is the smallest it'll be all year,
and it stays small until the spring blocks start in late December and
January.

**What performance does:** it moves a reel by a thousand or two, inside that
band. It doesn't turn 3K into 30K in November.

## Where I got it wrong
1. **I diagnosed every flop from one post** and called a cause each time (the
   motion frame, the gate, the photo, the title case), then built the next
   reel to fix it. With one post you can't tell a cause from noise, and the
   calendar was drowning everything.
2. **I blamed the photo I'd recommended.** The brief asked for one runner on
   a downhill road; Luke used one. Calling it the cause afterwards, from one
   post, wasn't fair or useful. Ledger rows 24 (the gate) and 25 (the photo)
   came from a single post each, so they're withdrawn as failures and kept
   only as ideas to test.
3. **My forecasts used the September grid.** They should have used the last
   few reels, and said so plainly: in November, 2K to 5K is normal for us.

## The rethink
1. **Nothing new goes straight to the profile.** Every new reel goes out as a
   trial reel first (non-followers only). Only reels that beat the base
   (above 4K and under 40% skip at 24 hours) are shared to the profile. The
   profile stops collecting flops, and every test is a real A/B instead of a
   story I tell afterwards.
2. **Go where the fish are.** In November the people playing every week are
   club footballers, rugby players and GAA players (soccer and rugby are in
   full season; most GAA club seasons are winding down, so check that before
   aiming there). Their match is this weekend, every weekend, which is exactly
   the "race in the next few weeks" that made our winners work. And the 30
   minutes before kick-off is the bar's slot. The match-day trial reel
   decides whether to build it out.
3. **Keep the marathon core warm, honestly sized.** Two profile posts a week
   from the formats that worked (goal-time pacing, the wall family), judged on
   saves, DMs and sign-ups, with 2K to 5K expected. They feed the list, which
   is the point.
4. **Use the calendar instead of fighting it.** Valencia (6 December): repost
   7 days, carb load and the 24 hours as trial reels from 26 November. The
   spring blocks (late December to January): the countdown from 16 weeks
   out. That's when the marathon pool refills and the race-week formats can
   do their numbers again.
5. **Reviews change shape.** Numbers against the base, then nothing about
   cause unless it repeats in 3 or more posts or a trial-reel A/B shows it.
