# The gap formula (23 Nov, from the research review)

**Two kinds of winners in the research:**
- **The creative spin:** a diagnosis of a belief they hold ("If zone 2 feels way too slow, you might be training off the wrong numbers", 1.53M).
- **The specific how-to:** the exact answer for one goal ("How to pace a perfect 5K", 578K; our own HOW TO RUN A SUB-4 (SECRETLY), skip 18.5%; the fourth hour, 18K, 1.7% saves).

**The gap across every platform:** the big topics (easy pace, eating more, the pros, match day) are covered in general terms. Coaches say "slow down",
dietitians say "eat more carbs", podcasts quote the pro's number. Nobody turns it into **the amateur's number for their goal time**. That's what our
winners did, and it's the save driver (P224: 4+ numbers per goal time).

**The formula:** proven topic + the belief in their words (line one, the turn) + the pro's or the study's number + your number by goal time
(the Keep card) + a personal version by email (comment word: their band, their day, their plan).

**Worked examples:**
1. Easy pace: YOUR EASY PACE FOR A / 4-HOUR MARATHON / (SLOWER THAN YOU THINK). Card: easy band by goal time. Email: EASY -> their band. Facts needed: the easy pace rule.
2. Eat more: YOU'LL GAIN 1.5KG / TRAINING FOR A MARATHON / (DON'T DIET IT OFF). Card: training-day carbs by weight. Email: FUEL -> their training-day plate. Facts: carbs and the scales (in FACTS), training-day intake (needed).
3. The pros: the pro's grams an hour against the 4-hour runner's (often 0). Card: grams an hour by goal time. Email: their gel clock. Facts: published pro intake (needed).
4. Match day: by kick-off time (FACTS has it). Card: what and when for 11am, 1pm and 3pm kick-offs. Email: their match day.

**Before each title run, a 5-minute gap check:** search the exact title on YouTube and TikTok. If the goal-time version exists and won, find another angle.

## Picking the next video (Luke, 23 Nov: "the best way to pick out a good one")
Score each candidate 1 to 3 on 8 tests; the highest goes next, and a tie goes to the one whose facts are ready first.
1. Proven demand: wins on 3+ accounts across 2+ platforms (research review).
2. Gap: no goal-time version won already (the 5-minute search).
3. Our data: close to our winners (goal-time how-to, fuel numbers, a myth killed).
4. Keep card: 4+ numbers per goal time.
5. Email reason: a personal version worth an address.
6. Seth want: status, fear or affiliation, clear in the title.
7. Not a repeat of the last 14 days.
8. Evergreen: works with no race in 4 weeks.
