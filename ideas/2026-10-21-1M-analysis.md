# Getting to 1M views in 30 days

## (a) The window: what drops out, and the real gap
**Method.** The export gives lifetime views only, so each reel's views are
spread over days with a curve fitted to our own early reads: normal reels
about 50% on day 0, 25% day 1, then a tail over 2 weeks (3:45 had 74% of its
lifetime by 17h; 4:30 the same by 10.5h); big reels (45K+) spread wider (7
days had 37% by 14h). Summed over 22 Sep to 21 Oct the model gives 842K
Instagram views against the dashboard's 860K, so it fits within 2% (and
the 860K is Instagram only: Facebook views aren't in it).

The wall (173.5K, posted 27 Sep), out of the wall, cramps, the day before,
7 days and quit all drop out between 27 Oct and 3 Nov. **1M is only
reachable by Sunday 25 / Monday 26 Oct.** After that the gap grows by
roughly 30 to 50K a day.

## (b) What predicts views
| Type | Reels | Median | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wall and race fears (wall, out of, without, cramps, quit) | 5 | **46.0K** | 31.6K to 173.5K | Excludes "4 types who hit the wall" (9.9K, sorting viewers, P103) |
| Before the race (7 days, day before, 3 hours, 30 min, taper, cold, heavy legs, sprints) | 8 | 19.6K | 6.8K to 84.0K | Race-day countdowns 22.5K to 84K; training topics 6.8K to 16.3K |
| Broad pacing (first 10 min, Dublin, goal pace, how hard, first half, second half, distance, first 5K) | 8 | 23.1K | 11.3K to 46.7K | |
| Goal time (sub-4, 3:45, 3:30, 4:30) | 4 | 13.6K | 8.2K to 26.8K | The best for emails (below) |
| Fuel (carb load, stomach, gels x3, eating x2) | 7 | 6.5K | 2.7K to 53.1K | Carb load is the one hit; it's a countdown schedule |

- **Saves and shares track views** (correlation with log views: saves 0.57,
  shares 0.49): the topics people keep spread. **Length doesn't matter**
  (-0.05 across 77 to 109 seconds).
- **Posting hour is weak** (0.19). Midday (11-3pm) median 26.8K (9 reels),
  evening 21.2K (20), morning 8.6K (3). Avoid mornings; midday vs evening
  isn't a real difference. (My "post at lunchtime" from the 3:30 review was
  over-read.)
- **Skip rate alone doesn't predict views** (carb load 46.7% skip, 53K;
  gel 35.8% skip, 4.1K). The topic decides reach.
- **Title shapes that won:** "How to get ahead of / out of / without [the
  wall]", "What to do [time] before a marathon", "Where [problem] actually
  starts".

## (c) What most of the audience has a problem with
Without the comments/DM text, Trends and competitor data, the best evidence
is what the whole audience already chose:
1. **The wall and late-race fears:** 5 reels, median 46K, our biggest by far.
2. **Race-week countdowns, timed to the race:** 7 days 84K, day before 34.6K.
3. **Pacing on the day:** median 23K; goal times bring the emails.
Fuel beyond the carb-load schedule is a minority problem (median 6.5K; the
gel video confirmed it).

**Realistic total by Monday: about 120K (range 70K to 200K) against a

**1M needs one of the 5 to do 100K+.** That has happened once in 32 reels
(the wall), so call it 1 in 10 to 1 in 5 across 5 posts, with the wall
remake as the best ticket.

**After Monday, the number falls whatever we post** (about 590K by 31 Oct
with no posts). Plan for it: judge next week by emails and by 7-day
views per reel, not the 30-day counter.

**Remakes:** the wall (173.5K) and 7 days (84K) are the formats to remake.
The wall now; 7 days before the next race cluster (spring marathons).

## (e) Testing the top 2 before posting

  same video with two different first lines and covers as two trial reels
  the evening before. After about 12 hours, keep the one with the lower
  skip and more views, and share it to followers at 12-1pm.
- **At 6 hours, working means:** above the "typical reel" line (about 8.5K
  Instagram views at 6h on the gel screenshot), skip under 36%, saves over
  2%. **On course for 40K+:** 15K+ at 6h with saves of 2%+ (without the
  wall was 41.5K at 18h, 7 days 31.4K at 14h).
