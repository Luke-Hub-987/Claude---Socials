## Why, in order of weight

1. **The topic is the gut, and gut topics have never travelled on
   this account.** Every fuel or gut-titled video sits at 1.6K to 4.4K
   (gels count, gels sick, gels alternatives, eating after a long run).
   Framing it as a race problem with the wall's title shape did not
   change that. P101 said the race is the title and fuel goes inside;
   "stomach problems" is still a gut title. **My call, and the data was
   already there.**
2. **The stages mixed timelines.** Long runs (weeks before), race
   morning, the first 10K, the course. Every viewer passes through them,
   but it is not one clock the way 0-10K/10-20K or the last 24 hours
   are, and the advice was split between training and race day.
3. **The cover was a two-photo collage of runners bent over**, which
   reads as tired (a wall video) and halves each image, the two things
   the cover notes flagged. It is a confound, not the main cause: skip
   still came in Lower.
4. **Length 1:29**, past the 1:22 to 1:27 cliff.
