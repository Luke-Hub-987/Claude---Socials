## Two things that did change in production

### 1. The OST became "How to run just 42K in a marathon"

The script said **42.2k**. The cover says **42K**.

That matters more than it looks. **The whole mechanism was the
precision.** "How to run 42.2k in a marathon" reads as a tautology and
makes a runner stop, because 42.2 is the exact number they know. "42K"
is a round approximation, so the sentence reads as ordinary and the
tension goes.

The 20.6% skip rate says the cover still worked, so this is a note for
next time rather than a diagnosis of anything. But **when a script
specifies a number to the decimal, the decimal is the device.**

Also: **"where the extra KM's comes from"** carries an apostrophe error
and a verb disagreement. On a video whose entire credibility rests on
being precise about a distance, that is worth thirty seconds with the
text tool.

### 2. The whisper is still under the play button

Same issue P89 named. The second line sits in the vertical centre of
the frame and the play triangle covers it in both thumbnails.

**Fix: the whisper moves up, directly under line one, in the top
third.** Nothing at all in the middle of the frame.

card is the thing that earns saves, it cannot live behind a minute of
video.
