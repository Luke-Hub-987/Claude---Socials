## Lessons
- **Hold one answer back for the last stage, and name it in the hook
  without explaining it** ("the hidden wall"). It carried a quarter of
  viewers to the last stage and gave our best watch time. This is P112
  proven, not a new rule.
- **The reframe in line one is now two for two.** Put it back next
  video. If a topic cannot take it truthfully, accept the skip cost
  knowingly, as here.
- **Test the AI cover on its own.** Next video: reframe back in, gate
  as written ("few weeks"), AI cover again. If skip returns to ~32%,
  AI covers are cleared; if it stays near 39%, the cover is the cause.
- **Up to ~1:40 is fine when the last stage holds the payoff.** 1:48
  without one lost the body.
- **The pinned comment as a two-option question** is worth repeating.
