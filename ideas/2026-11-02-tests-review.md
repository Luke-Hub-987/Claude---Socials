**What it tells us (the reason it was the control):**
1. **The ceiling is the calendar.** Our best reel ever, the one that did 147K
   in September, reached about 3.5K new people now. That's the same band as
   everything since 20 October. No edit gets past that in November.
2. **Under the ceiling, the reel still matters.** The same audience on the
   same days gave the wall 33.3% skip, 24 seconds watched, 32 saves and 5
   follows, against 52.1%, 14 seconds, 14 saves and 0 follows for Sunday's
   new reel. So the honest answer to "timing or performance" is both: timing
   sets how many people see it; the video decides how many stay and follow.
3. **Reposting winners works as a cheap, steady trial.** No filming, about
   3.5K new viewers, 5 follows and 32 saves. That's a better return than any
   new reel in two weeks.

One caveat: a trial goes only to non-followers and a profile post goes to
followers first, so the two columns aren't a perfect match. It's still the
fairest comparison we've had.
