## What it says

**Everyone who got past the first three seconds liked it more than any
video we have posted.** Record saves, record likes, record share of the
video watched. The steps worked.

**Fewer people got past the first three seconds.** Skip went from 25.5%
to 36.2%, and Instagram lists skip first "in order of importance to
reach". That is most of the reach gap, and two things changed at the
front:

1. **The OST lost its secret.** "Where the marathon wall actually
   starts" is a question with an unexpected answer. "How to get out of
   the marathon wall" is a useful promise with nothing surprising in
   it. It sells the save, and it does not stop the scroll.
2. **The first frame changed from the problem to a crowd.** The wall
   video opened on a runner in trouble. This one opens on a pack of
   runners at the "200" metres to go sign, which reads as the finish,
   someone else's good day. P93 has this exact result: triumph skips
   higher.

**The retention cliff at about 1:10 is the thread line and CTA.** Most
people who reach it have already saved. It is also why TikTok full
watch fell from 9.41% to 2.99%. Next time the thread line goes, and the
CTA is one sentence.

**TikTok nearly tripled with 5 follows**, the first video posted to the
ERN TikTok only. One video cannot prove the split caused it, and it is
consistent with it.
