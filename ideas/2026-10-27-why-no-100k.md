# Why no 100K video since the wall, and does it matter?

## The facts
- **Only one reel has ever passed 100K: the wall** (147K, about 173K
  lifetime). Two came close in October: carb load (84.4K) and without the
  wall (83.8K), both lifetime.
- **The front got worse.** Skip is the first rate Instagram lists for reach.
  - The wall: 25.5%.
  - 28 Sep to 14 Oct (12 reels): **median 34.0%** (out of the wall 36.2,
    cramp 33.0, 24 hours 38.1, who hits 32.1, 7 days 34.3, quit 32.5,
    taper 43.1, first 10 minutes 32.4, heavy legs 34.4, second half 38.1,
    how hard 31.9).
  - 15 Oct to 25 Oct (12 reels): **median 40.0%** (goal gone 31.9, cold
    35.1, gel 35.8, 4:30 37.0, enjoy 38.2, without the wall 39.3, sub-4
    40.8, sleep 41.4, 3:45 44.1, surprise 44.1, carb load 46.7, 3:30 48.9).
- **The mix changed, on purpose.** Since 15 Oct, 5 of 12 reels were narrow
  by design (4 goal times and the gel), for emails. Only one was the wall
  family (without the wall: the closest to 100K). Two were off the race
  clock (sleep, cold) and one was a story.

## Why
1. **A 100K reel needs a famous monster with a counter-position about its
   cause** (the wall starts in the first 10K). We've used the wall once in
   the last 12 reels.
2. **It needs skip under about 30%.** Our median went from 34% to 40%. The
   causes are in the ledger: goal-time titles (40.8% to 48.9%), AI covers,
   sentences in the opening frame, a crowd frame, off-clock topics.
3. **Narrow videos cap reach by design.** Goal times median 13.6K; broad
   race fears median 46K (1M analysis).

## Does it matter?
**For the 1M target, yes:** it needs one 100K reel in a 30-day window.
**For the business, less than it looks.** Goal-time videos bring 2 to 5
emails per 1,000 views; broad videos about 1. A 100K broad reel is about
100 emails; three 25K goal-time reels can bring more. What a 100K reel
does that nothing else does is put us in front of a new crowd that then
sees the rest of the grid, and the back catalogue already brings 11
sign-ups on a day with no new reel.

## The recommendation (council below)
**One reach reel a week built to the wall formula,** with a skip target
under 32%: a famous fear, a counter-position about its cause, a picture in
frame one, a schedule to save, everyone running a marathon. The rest of the
week earns emails and saves. This week's reach slot is the walk (a lived
fear), not yet a famous monster; next week's reach slot goes to the wall
family again.

## LLM Council (P150)
- **Contrarian:** Chasing 100K leads to clickbait and repeats of the wall
  that viewers have seen. One outlier in 35 reels is luck as much as
  formula.
- **Assumption-ripper:** We assume 100K is a goal. The real goals are
  emails and a launch list; views are a means. We also assume the wall
  formula still works: without the wall did 83.8K, so it does.
- **Expansionist:** A wall-family reel each week compounds: the family
  stacks (P75), and each one sends people to the others.
- **Outsider:** "I'd watch anything about hitting the wall; I've heard of
  that. I wouldn't stop for a 5:00 pacing video unless I was a 5:00 runner."
- **Executor:** One reach slot a week, skip under 32%, judged at 6 hours
  against the typical line. The rest for emails.
- **Chairman:** **It matters for the 1M, not for the business.** Build one
  reel a week to the wall formula and judge the others on emails.
