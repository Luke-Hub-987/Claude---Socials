*Super hook, pain twisted:* "Most people answer that by switching

*Super hook, dream outcome:* "Three changes, none of them cost
anything, and you go through the last ten kilometres still taking fuel
instead of gagging on it. First one you can do on Sunday."

**[FIX 1 — earlier and closer together]**
"Take them earlier and more often. Waiting until you feel low means
asking your stomach for a big load at the worst possible moment. Every
twenty minutes from twenty minutes in beats one an hour starting at
halfway, and it is the same total, just spread."

**[ON SCREEN ONLY, not spoken]**
"1 marathon. Ultras: Connemara 60k, Seven Sisters 55k."

**[FIX 2 — water with every one]**
"Take water with every single gel. On its own a gel is extremely
concentrated, and concentrated sugar sitting in a stomach that has
already slowed down is exactly the feeling people are describing ⚑. For
a lot of people that one change is the whole fix."

**[FIX 3 — rehearse it]**
"And practise it. Your gut adapts to what you regularly ask it to do
⚑, so taking gels only on race day is asking an untrained stomach to
do something new in the hardest hour of your year. Take race fuel on
your long runs from here on."

**[MECHANISM, late and short]**
"All three are the same idea. As the effort climbs, blood goes to your
legs and away from your stomach ⚑, so by thirty K the gel is not
disagreeing with you, it is queueing."

**[PAYOFF]**
"Almost nobody needs a different gel. They need it earlier, with water,
and rehearsed."

**[THREAD + CTA]**
"Next one is how much you should actually be taking an hour ⚑. Save
this before your next long run."
