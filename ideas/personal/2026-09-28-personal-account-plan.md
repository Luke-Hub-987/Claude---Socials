## The plan: two lanes, two videos a week

### Lane 1. The mountains, when you actually climb one

**The unexpected value (Thiel):** a running account showing the climb
from a runner's point of view, and the part of each mountain nobody
shows. Nobody in marathon content is on Irish mountains.

**Keep it plain (Ralston):** the title that did 148K was the name of
the mountain. No cleverness needed.

> **What's it actually like to climb [mountain]**

- Same title shape as Benbulben, word for word.
- **Show the climb, not the summit.** The pillars note is Luke's own:
  "people want to see themselves going up."
- **One unexpected thing per climb**, said plainly on the way: the
  false summit, the bit that is harder than the top, the descent that
  is worse than the climb.
- **Only mountains he actually climbs.** The two with the widest
  audience in Ireland are **Croagh Patrick** and **Carrauntoohil**,
  known well beyond running. Brandon and the Maumturks are ones he has
  already done.
- **Raw, not produced.** The pillars file warns against formatting this
  into something formulaic.

### Lane 2. Named ideas, one a week

**Luke's own concepts, with a name that explains itself in one
second.** Briar's format, Ralston's shared language, the HEEL LOCK
proof. Every one has to be something he actually believes (P94).

**Three candidates built only from things he has already posted:**

| Named idea | Where it comes from | The secret |
|---|---|---|
| **The lamppost rule** | His own run/walk to 3K, "1 lamppost at a time" | You finish the run you want to quit by shrinking the goal, and motivation has nothing to do with it |
| **The 1,200 hour rule** | "One missed session out of 1,200 hours of training" | One missed session is a rounding error, and the guilt costs more than the session |
| **The real problem with running everything easy** | His own post of the same name | Easy running has a ceiling most people never notice they have hit |

**Titles, plain first:**

> **The lamppost rule (for the run you want to quit)**
> **The 1,200 hour rule (for the session you missed)**

**Ralston's exercise, which only Luke can do:** write down ten things
runners say that he disagrees with. Those ten are the next ten named
ideas. I have not invented any, because they have to be his.
