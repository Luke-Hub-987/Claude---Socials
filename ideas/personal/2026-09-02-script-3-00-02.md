## Aha #2 (body block 2, the mechanism)

Think about what 3:00:02 actually proves, 3 weeks after a DNF. A clean
sub-3 might have felt like the ceiling, done, nothing left to chase. A
bigger miss might have felt hopeless. But landing 2 seconds away, right
after a low point, proves the fitness was already there the whole
time. That's what actually fuels someone forward. Not the result on the
clock, what the result proves is possible.

sit with, it's data. The real move is to look at what actually changed
between the low point and the near miss, and do more of that, not treat
the miss as a verdict on what you're capable of.
