## The new one, broken down

His line: *"Things everyone in endurance should be doing based on
what's working right now."*

**Why it works already:** "based on what's working right now" is the
news lever (Caples), and it carries an implied threat that the viewer's
knowledge is out of date. "Everyone in endurance" is a gate that is
wide but named, and it reaches past runners to triathletes, Hyrox and
cyclists. "Should be doing" implies they currently are not, which is
self-incrimination.

**What it was missing, against the current rules:** no finite number
(P54 wants an enumerable set), "things" is a category label rather than
a concrete noun (rule 6), and the cost of scrolling is implied rather
than stated.

**Upgraded:**

> **7 things working in endurance right now that you're probably still not doing** ⚑

Finite set, recency, and the self-incrimination moved into the line so
the cost of scrolling is explicit: you stay behind, and you will not
know it.

**⚑ before filming:** he needs to be able to stand behind "working
right now", meaning a real source for each of the seven, not a vibe
(P39).
