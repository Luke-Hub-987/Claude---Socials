stomach can still handle late on. As you go through 10k, 20k, 30k, more

**[FIX 2 — the single sip]**
"A sip of plain water with every single gel. On its own a gel is
concentrated sugar sitting in a dry gut, and it pulls water out of your

through before it can stall. For a lot of people that one change is the
whole fix."

race day is asking an untrained stomach to handle heavy fuel during the
hardest hours of your entire block."

## What changed from the draft, and why

1. **A dated gate was added at the very front.** Both winners opened
   this way and the one video that underperformed had no date attached
   to it. This is the strongest single pattern in our data (P74).
2. **The OST joined the winning query family.** "Q:" dropped, plain
   query kept, "marathon edition" added as the series marker.
3. **The three fixes were named.** The 20 minute rule, the single sip,
   the gut block.
4. **Fix 1's logic was reordered.** Blood flow explains why a late,
   large dose fails; it does not explain why twenty minute spacing
   works. The mechanism now sits on the claim it actually supports.
5. **"Acid" was cut.** A gel sitting in the stomach is not acid, and it
   is the one line someone would correct in the comments.
6. **The CTA was rebuilt.** "If you enjoyed this give us a follow" asks
   for the thing people do least. Saves are the account's defining
   signal at 3.7%, so the ask is a save tied to a moment, followed by
   the two doors the winning video used.
7. **A thread to the other videos was added**, which is the mechanism
   that made the sequel outperform the original.
