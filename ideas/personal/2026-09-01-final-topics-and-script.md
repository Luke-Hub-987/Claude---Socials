## Full script — Topic 1, the threshold plateau

Picked for the demo script because it has the cleanest natural
Double Aha structure (a numbered "3 reasons" shape gives 2-3 distinct
front-loaded insight slots) and comes from the newest, most specific
source. Bullet points per P19, not a word-for-word script.

**On-screen text hook:** "3 reasons your 'threshold' runs aren't
actually threshold anymore"

**Verbal hook (P30, viewer-facing dream outcome):** "By the end of
this, you'll know exactly why your hardest sessions might not even be
doing what you think they're doing, and how to fix it in one run."

**Super hook (on-screen credibility, real confirmed facts only):** "1
marathon, 3 ultras, Sligo-based"

**Body — Double Aha placement (HF11):**

- **Aha #1, right after the super hook:** Threshold isn't a fixed
  pace, it's a moving target. Most runners set a "threshold pace"
  once, usually off an old time trial, and just chase that number
  forever without ever checking whether it's still doing what it's
  supposed to do.
- **Aha #2, body block 2:** The exact same pace can stop being a
  threshold effort on a hot day, a tired day, or a hilly route,
  without you noticing, because you're still hitting the number on
  your watch. On a bad day it's now genuinely too hard for what the
  session is supposed to train. On a great day it's now genuinely too
  easy. Either way, the session's not doing its job, and the watch
  will never tell you that.
- **Supporting point, body block 3 (the fix, not a third aha, just
  the payoff):** check effort against the number, not just the
  number. If you can hold a full sentence at your "threshold" pace,
  it's stopped being threshold. Retest roughly once a training block,
  not once and forget it.

**Feeling:** the viewer should land on "I've been training slightly
blind without realizing it," not shame, more like relief that there's
a simple, concrete check for something that felt too technical to
question before.

**CTA (soft engagement):** ask viewers to think back to their last
"threshold" session and whether they could actually hold a
conversation at that pace, comment yes or no.
