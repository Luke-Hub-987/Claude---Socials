"Same session, same pace, higher heart rate than two months ago ⚑.
Clearest signal there is, and almost nobody looks for it, because the
pace looks fine and the pace is what gets written down. Open your
watch, pull up the same session from two months ago, and compare the
average heart rate. That takes sixty seconds. If it has climbed and you
are not ill and it was not twenty five degrees, that is your answer."

"The fix is not a harder session, it is a current number. Take it from
something you actually ran this month, reset the pace, and the same

## What changed from Luke's version, and why

1. **The hook is now a stack of three (P71).** His had the gate and the
   pain in one sentence and no dream outcome at all. The dream outcome
   is the beat that makes someone stay for a Core video they could
   otherwise scroll past.
2. **The pain is twisted further.** "Exerting yourself with no results"
   became "the hardest running of your week for nothing", and the cost
   now names what it keeps taking: the Tuesday and the long run.
3. **Sign 1 now pre-empts the obvious objection.** "If you are not ill
   and it was not twenty five degrees" kills the first comment anyone
   knowledgeable would leave, and it is the same move that makes his
   race day script feel lived rather than researched.
4. **The 60 seconds moved into the OST**, since it is the quick easy
   way and it was doing nothing buried in the body (P73).
5. **A save prompt was added.** Saves are one of the three metrics that
   matter (P66) and there was no ask at all.
6. Kept exactly as he wrote them: "drag the arse out of the last one",
   three by five minutes, "the pace is what gets written down", and the
   next-video thread.
