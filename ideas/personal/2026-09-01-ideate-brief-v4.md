# Ideation Brief (Personal) — 2026-09-01 (v4, sharpened hooks + on-screen text field)

## What changed from v3

Four fixes from direct feedback on v3, now permanent system-level rules
(P21, P22, P23 in `principles.md`; schema change in both `/ideate-*`
skills), not one-off patches:

1. **On-screen text hook is now its own mandatory field**, separate
   from any spoken/verbal hook (`on_screen_text_hook` vs `verbal_hook`
   in the schema). Every idea below has both, and the on-screen field
   is the one written to actually appear as text overlay.
2. **Killed the "it's not what you think" reversal pattern.** New rule,
   P21: a hook needs a specific number, a named taxonomy, or a concrete
   claim, not a withheld-answer trick. "The 3 types of hills near me"
   (idea 6, unchanged, the model) passes this. "The secret to finishing
   a race isn't what you think" (old idea 10) didn't, and has been
   rewritten to name the actual image instead.
3. **Dropped the fabricated daily-lunch claim.** Idea 3 no longer
   asserts anything about Luke's actual eating habits. New rule, P23:
   never assert an unconfirmed personal fact to fit a template. Idea 3
   now translates a different, non-autobiographical device (ironic
   understatement) from the same competitor research instead.
4. **Dropped the Johnny Harris source.** His outliers were all
   geopolitics videos (Iran, China, oligarchy) on a channel with a
   551k-view baseline, a textbook confound (reach + trending topic, not
   hook quality). New rule, P22: weigh whether an outlier's multiplier
   plausibly reflects genuine packaging quality before citing it.
   Replaced with a Nick Bare video on a much smaller (35k-baseline)
   running/hybrid-athlete channel, a more plausible case that the
   content itself, not raw reach, drove the result.

### Idea 1 — The 2-lift rule

- **Pillar:** Running technique & training
- **Format:** IG_reel
