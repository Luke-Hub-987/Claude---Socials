## What it's saying
1. **The topic pulled; the title cost the front.** Views beat the typical line and 24s is our second-best watch time this month on our longest reel, so the people who stayed found it interesting (P228 held). But skip was 37.9%, 8 points worse than the 30K reel: the cold reviewer gave the title 3/10 and the first 3 seconds agree.
2. **The send line at 0:44 lost almost half of who was left (32% to 18%).** It's the second time an ask in the body has cost a step: the 10% rule lost viewers at 0:38 to 0:45 at the CTA (ledger row 9). This time it also asked them to send it to "whoever you're running your next marathon with": most have no marathon (P225).
3. **The answer was done by 0:44.** Stage 2 (the speed-up near the finish) answered "why 3:59". Stages 3 and 4 were "how to use it", and an interest viewer leaves when the curiosity closes. The ask and the closed gap landed at the same second, so this one reel can't separate them.
4. **Saves were low (0.5%) because the card came too late.** The clock card was on screen from about 0:48, when only about 18% were still watching. G34's keepable numbers need to be on screen before the big drop, not after it.
5. **0 comments.** The pinned question ("what did you finish in?") got nothing; same lesson as the send line: no race, no answer.
