## Read
1. **The opening frame showed a sentence, and the sentence was the
   answer.** The motion graphic opened on "You'll sleep badly. And it won't
   cost you your race." in big serif type, beside a 2:00 AM clock, with the
   OST under it. That's two headlines to read in the first second, and the
   top one hands an anxious viewer the reassurance they came for. Goal gone
   opened on one number (+3:00) and ran 31.9% skip; this ran 41.4% with the
   same motion style. The difference is a picture against a sentence.
2. **Line one was a reassurance too.** "You'll probably sleep badly, and it
   won't cost you your race" closes the fear in the same breath it opens
   it. Most of the skip happened by 0:08, when line one finishes.
3. **The subject is off the race clock.** Sleep is a body worry, like kit
   (cold marathon: 0.9% saves) and the gut. People liked it (1.6%, Higher)
   but didn't save it (1.0%) or send it (0.4%): watch once, feel better,
   move on. The gate's line 2 (a race moment, felt in the legs or on the
   clock) would have caught it.
4. **The CTA lost about half of who was left,** from ~18% to ~10% at 0:49
   to 0:52. It's the second time (4:30 at 0:40). Here it landed at 0:49,
   not a third of the way in (0:33), because the hook stack ran 116 words
   (37 seconds) before the first stage.
5. **The body held.** From 0:52 to 1:30 the line is flat: the people who
   got past the CTA stayed for the word game.
6. **One content-timing point (not posting time, P144):** the main action
   was "the night that counts is 2 nights before, Friday". Anyone racing
   Sunday who saw it on Saturday had already missed it.

## Lessons
- **The opening frame is one picture or one number. Never a sentence, and
  never the answer** (P147).
- **Line one opens the fear and leaves it open.** The reassurance comes in
  the stages.
- **Keep the hook stack to about 80 words** so the CTA lands near 0:33
  (P128), straight after the first stage's payoff.
- **Off-clock body worries (sleep, kit, the gut) get likes, not saves.**
  The topic gate's line 2 stays.
- **Forecast from the multiplier:** first-day read times 1.6 to 2.1.
