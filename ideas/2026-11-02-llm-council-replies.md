## Chairman verdict
ERN does not have a reply problem. It has a reply-handling problem:
about 140 replies in three weeks, nearly 100 plans typed by hand by one
person with a day job, and at least two that contradict the brand's own
numbers. Asking for more replies before fixing that spends trust.

1. Make answering fast and consistent first: an answer bank built only
   from the brand numbers (km and miles splits by goal, gels, cramp, the
   surge, spring start, waitlist, thanks), checked with the humaniser
   skill. Jamie takes finish times, thanks and waitlist; Luke takes
   plans. Gmail label "Answered". Clear the unread backlog.
2. Keep the ask that works: a real deadline plus a specific reward
   ("reply by Friday for your plan", about 7 per 100). Keep the buttons
   and add "or just hit Reply" in words.
3. Next send: spring splits. Show the 3:30 to 4:30 rows inside so the
   value is instant; ask for race and goal time for their exact splits in
   km or miles; one line asking them to forward it to someone they train
   with. Target: 5 or more replies per 100 delivered.
4. Social: one reel tests a comment CTA ("comment your goal time and
   I'll reply with your first 10K pace"), answered from the bank. The
   4:05 surge story becomes the reel "What to do at 10K if you're
   behind", only with the runner's permission and no name.
5. Not now: the tester invite (recipe, allergens and shipping first),
   split tests (the list is too small), weekly sends. Two campaigns at
   most before Christmas; the flows do the rest.

## Measure
Replies per 100 delivered for every send (baselines: 7 with a deadline
and a plan, 3 for "reply with your time"); minutes per reply; replies
that contradict the brand numbers (target 0); comments on the test reel.
