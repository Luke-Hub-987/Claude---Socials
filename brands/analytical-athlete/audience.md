# Audience / ICP — The Analytical Athlete (LinkedIn)

Built from real LinkedIn Aggregate Analytics data (`AggregateAnalytics_Luke_Morrison_ACA_CTA_20260610_20260907.xlsx`, AUDIENCE DEMOGRAPHICS + CONTENT DEMOGRAPHICS sheets), not assumed. **This directly contradicts the working assumption that LinkedIn's ICP is the same as the personal IG/TikTok brand's** (`brands/personal/audience.md` — runners/endurance athletes, no employment framing at all). The two are different audiences and this brand should not borrow that file.

## Who they actually are (from the data)

- **Industry:** Accounting (20-24%), Financial Services (11-17%), Staffing & Recruiting (8%), Spectator Sports (7%), Business Consulting (5-6%), plus Law, Legal Services, Banking, Investment Management, IT Services.
- **Seniority:** Senior (30-37%) and Director (17-22%) dominate. Manager, VP, Partner, CXO, Owner make up most of the rest. This is an established-career audience, not entry-level (Entry is only 12-15%).
- **Company:** Deloitte, EY, PwC (Ireland and UK), Grant Thornton Ireland, KPMG (Ireland and UK) — this is overwhelmingly the Big 4 / large accounting-firm world, i.e. Luke's own professional network from his ACA/CTA career, plus company sizes skewed large (10,001+ employees is the single biggest bracket at 19-29%).
- **Job titles:** Tax Manager, Tax Director, Managing Director, Financial Accountant, Financial Director, CFO, Founder, Recruitment Consultant, Accountant.
- **Location:** Greater Dublin (16-34%) and London (8-20%) dominate, with smaller pockets in Sydney, Cork, Galway, Belfast, Melbourne — an Ireland/UK-centric, English-speaking professional-services audience with an Irish-diaspora tail.
- **Notable non-finance signal:** IRFU, Leinster Rugby, Saracens appear in the follower base at low percentages — a rugby-adjacent thread, consistent with Luke's own history, worth keeping in mind as flavor but not a pillar on its own.

## What this means for positioning

This is **not** a running/fitness audience that happens to have day jobs — it is a **finance/professional-services audience for whom endurance training is a proof point and a lens**, not the subject. They followed Luke because he's a peer/former colleague in accounting and tax, not because they searched for running content. The content that performs (see `swipe-file.md`) works because it translates training discipline into something this specific audience already values and measures: capacity, output, decision quality, career longevity — not running times or race results for their own sake.

## What they're struggling with

- Believing fitness and a demanding senior career are mutually exclusive ("I'll get fit once work calms down" — a belief the swipe file shows Luke directly naming and refuting).
- Afternoon cognitive fade, decision fatigue, the 4pm wall — framed as solvable through training, not caffeine or willpower.
- Watching peers who look identically positioned diverge in outcomes over years, without a clear mechanism for why.
- Skepticism that "wellness" content applies to someone in a genuinely demanding, billable-hours career — this audience will tune out anything that reads as generic productivity-guru fluff (see `voice.md` hard rules).

## Why they'd follow this account specifically over a generic fitness/productivity creator

Luke is a credible peer, not an outside influencer: ACA/CTA-qualified, in the same firms and career track as the audience, who also races ultras and marathons at a competitive amateur level while working full time. The credibility beat is "I am one of you and I also do this," not "let me teach you fitness."
