# Content Pillars — Luke (Personal Brand)

Clustered from real on-screen text + view count evidence, confirmed
and revised by Luke.

**Career/workplace is not a pillar, not even as texture.** An earlier
draft kept "busy full-time job" alive as a quiet counter-positioning
frame rather than a direct topic. Luke reviewed that and pushed back
further: the pillar list still read as too career/workplace-focused
for his taste, and he wants it moved away from more than that edit
did. Resolved: no workplace/career framing anywhere in personal-brand
content, texture included, not just direct topics like "leave work on
time." The busy-schedule fact stays available in `voice.md`'s
credibility background only insofar as running credentials go
(marathon + ultra count), not the job itself.

- **Pillar 1: Running technique & training** — strides, workout
  completion, base-building, recovery vs. rest, 10k/marathon pacing.
  The steady bread-and-butter pillar (roughly 500-2,600 views per
  post, consistent).
- **Pillar 2: Mental side of running** — spotlight effect, letting
  your head end runs early, guilt over one missed session, "3 reasons
  I run on holiday." Vulnerable/psychological rather than tactical.
- **Pillar 3: Adventure / outdoor — confirmed, lean in.** Benbulben
  climb, Mount Brandon, Maumturk Challenge hikes. Benbulben hit
  **148,000 views** against a normal baseline of ~500-2,500 (P8). Luke
  wants this made regularly, not as a one-off — he has real, repeatable
  access to mountains and no issue filming. His specific angle: show
  the actual climb/process, not just an end-of-summit payoff shot —
  "people want to see themselves going up," not just arriving. Explicit
  caution: don't let AI-generated scripts over-produce or over-format
  this into something formulaic; keep it raw/authentic per HF9 and the
  "just start talking" delivery style (research/briar-ai-podcast-transcript.txt).
- **Pillar 4: Relatable/self-deprecating running content** — "Feeling
  slow asf at Parkrun," hottest-place-on-earth stunt framing. Lower
  view counts individually but likely does relationship-building work
  (P3 Core/Casual fit) rather than reach work.
