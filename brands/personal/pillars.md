# Content Pillars — Luke (Personal Brand)

**Draft, clustered from the same on-screen text + view count evidence
as voice.md, not yet confirmed by Luke.** View counts are what's shown
in the grid Luke shared (looks like reach/views from IG insights).

- **Pillar 1: Running technique & training** — strides, workout
  completion, base-building, recovery vs. rest, 10k/marathon pacing.
  The steady bread-and-butter pillar (roughly 500-2,600 views per
  post, consistent).
- **Pillar 2: Working professional who runs** — fitting training
  around a demanding job: "leave on time without killing your
  career," "log off without guilt," "outperform people staying late."
  This is the pillar that's distinct from a typical running account —
  worth protecting as a differentiator, not diluting.
- **Pillar 3: Mental side of running** — spotlight effect, letting
  your head end runs early, guilt over one missed session, "3 reasons
  I run on holiday." Vulnerable/psychological rather than tactical.
- **Pillar 4: Adventure / outdoor** — Benbulben climb, Mount Brandon,
  Maumturk Challenge hikes. Only a few posts, but the Benbulben one hit
  **148,000 views** against a normal baseline of ~500-2,500 — roughly a
  60-100x outlier (P8, principles.md). Worth asking: is adventure
  content something to lean into more, or was this a one-off fluke
  worth investigating rather than assuming it repeats?
- **Pillar 5: Relatable/self-deprecating running content** — "Feeling
  slow asf at Parkrun," hottest-place-on-earth stunt framing. Lower
  view counts individually but likely does relationship-building work
  (P3 Core/Casual fit) rather than reach work.
