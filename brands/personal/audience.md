# Audience / ICP — Luke (Personal Brand)

## Who they are

Runners and endurance athletes, from beginners into the sport up
through experienced racers. Not defined by employment status or
career situation — per Luke's explicit pushback, that framing is
retired entirely (see `pillars.md`), so the audience isn't "working
professionals who run" either.

## What they're struggling with

Covered by the pillars in `pillars.md`: technical training questions
(pacing, base-building, recovery) and the mental/psychological side of
the sport (spotlight effect, guilt over missed sessions, burnout).

## What they follow this account for

Practical, specific running advice delivered by someone who's
genuinely done the distances (1 marathon, 3 ultras) — not just
technique tips but the process itself (adventure/outdoor content,
Pillar 3) and the honest, self-deprecating relatable side of the sport
(Pillar 4), not just the polished wins.
