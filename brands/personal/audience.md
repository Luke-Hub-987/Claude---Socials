# Audience / ICP — Luke (Personal Brand)

## Who they are

Runners and endurance athletes, from beginners into the sport up
through experienced racers. Relatable to people with full-time jobs
(Luke's own reality — busy full-time job, still trains seriously), but
that's not a requirement to be in the audience and it's not the whole
identity of the account either. Don't narrow the audience definition
down to "working professionals" as if that's a gate — it's a texture
that makes the advice feel earned, not a filter on who it's for.

## What they're struggling with

Covered by the pillars in `pillars.md`: technical training questions
(pacing, base-building, recovery), the mental/psychological side of
the sport (spotlight effect, guilt over missed sessions, burnout), and
fitting serious training into a life that also has real time
constraints (without that constraint becoming the entire pitch).

## What they follow this account for

Practical, specific running advice delivered by someone who's
genuinely done the distances (1 marathon, 3 ultras) — not just
technique tips but the process itself (adventure/outdoor content,
Pillar 4) and the honest, self-deprecating relatable side of the sport
(Pillar 5), not just the polished wins.
