# Content Pillars — Engine Room Nutrition

Clustered from real on-screen text + view count evidence, confirmed
and revised by Luke.

- **Pillar 1: Race-day fueling & performance advice** — general
  fueling advice, not always product-specific: "What to actually eat
  before a race," "When you actually need a gel." Same territory as
  the personal brand's Pillar 1, but framed toward the product's
  category rather than pure technique.
- **Pillar 2: Product proof-by-doing — confirmed flagship.** The
  founders using the bar themselves in real endurance conditions:
  "Can the ERN Carb Bar Get us a 5KM PB?", "POV: Climb Mount Brandon,
  only survived bcause of the ERN bar," "the bar that worked in a food
  lab was about to meet 1,200 metres of climbing." Strongest,
  most-consistent performer of the distinctly-ERN content
  (1,271-1,725 views vs. ~450-700 for most other posts). Luke
  reinforced this directly: ERN needs "proof, but proof so much that
  people care" (citing Briar's own "took personal brands from 0 to
  X income" as the model of proof people actually want to see) — this
  pillar is that proof mechanism for ERN.
- **Pillar 3: Founder/origin story** — "Why 2 accountants made a Carb
  Bar," "Nobody expected us 2 to be next" (2,158 views, the single
  best-performing non-intro post in this set). Underdog/unlikely
  founder framing is clearly resonating.
- **Pillar 4: Product R&D / behind the scenes** — "Rice Crisp Graveyard
  - Ep. 4" implies a recurring series about recipe iteration/testing.
  Confirmed live: ERN is taking product development more in-house
  (the external food scientist has been slow), so there should be more
  of this material coming, not less.
- **Pillar 5: Shared relatable running content** (crossover with
  personal brand) — "Feeling slow asf at Parkrun," "the morning that
  produced my fastest ever 5k, it shouldn't have." Not flagged as a
  problem — fine to keep cross-posting.
- **Pillar 6: Workplace tie-in humor — retiring the direct crossover.**
  Luke's decision: ERN should develop its own distinct angle here
  rather than just reposting the personal brand's workplace humor
  ("most interesting at work," "falling behind at work had nothing to
  do with my job"). What that distinct angle actually is still needs
  more thought — flagged by Luke himself as something to come back to,
  not resolved yet.
