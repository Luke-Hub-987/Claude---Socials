# Content Pillars — Engine Room Nutrition

**Draft, clustered from the same on-screen text + view count evidence
as voice.md, not yet confirmed by Luke.**

- **Pillar 1: Race-day fueling & performance advice** — general
  fueling advice, not always product-specific: "What to actually eat
  before a race," "When you actually need a gel." Same territory as
  the personal brand's Pillar 1, but framed toward the product's
  category rather than pure technique.
- **Pillar 2: Product proof-by-doing** — the founders using the bar
  themselves in real endurance conditions: "Can the ERN Carb Bar Get
  us a 5KM PB?", "POV: Climb Mount Brandon, only survived bcause of
  the ERN bar," "the bar that worked in a food lab was about to meet
  1,200 metres of climbing." This is the highest/most-consistent
  performer of the distinctly-ERN content (1,271-1,725 views vs.
  ~450-700 for most other posts) — worth treating as the core pillar
  to double down on.
- **Pillar 3: Founder/origin story** — "Why 2 accountants made a Carb
  Bar," "Nobody expected us 2 to be next" (2,158 views, the single
  best-performing non-intro post in this set). Underdog/unlikely
  founder framing is clearly resonating.
- **Pillar 4: Product R&D / behind the scenes** — "Rice Crisp Graveyard
  - Ep. 4" implies a recurring series about recipe iteration/testing.
  Worth confirming: how many episodes exist, is this a pillar to keep
  running?
- **Pillar 5: Shared relatable running content** (crossover with
  personal brand) — "Feeling slow asf at Parkrun," "the morning that
  produced my fastest ever 5k, it shouldn't have." Identical posts
  appear on both accounts.
- **Pillar 6: Workplace tie-in humor** (crossover with personal brand)
  — "most interesting at work" (H&B costume), "falling behind at work
  had nothing to do with my job." Also cross-posted territory.

## Open questions for Luke

- Confirm these 6, or which should merge/drop. Pillars 5 and 6 overlap
  heavily with the personal brand, worth asking whether ERN should
  keep cross-posting that content or develop its own distinct angle
  on it.
- Pillar 2 (proof-by-doing) looks like the strongest distinctly-ERN
  performer here, is that the intended flagship pillar going forward,
  especially pre-manufacturing when there's no live product to sell
  yet?
