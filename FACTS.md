# FACTS.md: the fixed facts every script must use

Read this BEFORE writing any script, after CLAUDE.md. If a script line
disagrees with this file, the script is wrong. When Luke gives a new fact
(an email, a correction, a number), it goes in here in the same turn,
with the date.

## Who's watching and what they carry (P126, P133, P134)
- Amateur marathon runners in Ireland, the UK and Europe. Goals mostly 3:30
  to 4:30 (the emails ask most for 3:45 to 4:00; comments and DMs for 3:30
  and 4:30). Many think in miles (P129).
- **In their pockets on race day:** gels of ONE flavour they tolerate (they
  don't buy mixed boxes); sometimes caffeine gels saved for the second
  half; jelly sweets as a fallback. Stations give water and sports drink;
  cola is not guaranteed.
- **What they will never do:** eat real food in the race (no bars,
  bananas, pretzels), carry soft flasks, wear arm sleeves, use bin bags or
  socks as gloves, set up watch alerts or apps. Kit = what's already on
  their wrist and in their pocket.
- **Their watch:** Garmin live pace moves in 5-second steps (P132). They
  read the time of day / race time easily.

## Fuel: the numbers we use (P118, P124, P137, P138)
- **Baseline gel: 30g of carbs** (Precision, and most gels this audience
  uses, are 30g or 40g; some older ones are 20 to 25g).
- **Target: about 60g of carbs an hour,** up to 75g. Say grams first.
- **Schedule:** first gel at 20 minutes, then a 30g gel every 30 minutes
  (on the clock: 20 and 50 past). 40g gels: every 40 minutes. 20-25g gels:
  every 20 minutes. Do the maths before writing: 30g every 20 minutes is
  90g an hour, too much.
- **What to carry** (30g, first at 20 min, every 30, none in the last 20
  minutes, plus 1 spare): 3:30 carry 7, 3:45 and 4:00 carry 8, 4:30 carry 9,
  5:00 carry 10. Totals in captions only, never in the audio.
- Maths check: 30g every 30 minutes = 2 an hour = 60g. Every 20 minutes = 3
  an hour = 90g.
- **Station bottles (Dublin): 250ml.** Drink-your-gel method (21 Oct): 3
  sips first to make room, squeeze the whole gel in, shake, sip it over
  the next 2K, finish before the next gel; no sports drink on top. Cup
  races: gel 100m before the station, then 3-4 sips.
- **Luke, 5 Nov (C22): the clock wins.** First gel at 20 minutes, then every 30
  minutes. Water is rough: if you're passing a station, grab a bottle and take
  a few sips with the gel. Don't move gels to the stations or carry bottles.
  The "100m before the station" lines above and below are superseded.
- Every gel with 3 or 4 sips (100-150ml) of plain water, about 100m before
  a station. Never washed down with sports drink. Sports drink between gels.
- Never 2 or more gels together (out of the wall). Missed one: let it go.
- Never a total gel count in the audio (the "eleven gels" argument, P124).
- Gels have no smell (P135). Real complaints: the texture, thick and
  sickly sweet, "hacking it down", not hungry, sloshing, sick of them late,
  switching to coke late.

## Pacing: the numbers we use
- First 10K 5-10 s/K slower than goal; up to a minute behind at halfway.
- Watch reads about 1% long; watch paces a few seconds quicker than real
  goal pace; the times at the 10K signs are the real check. **Luke, 5 Nov (C24):
  give the clock time at every 10K sign (10K, 20K, 30K, 40K) as the check.**
- Paces as 5-second bands; numerals in scripts (P131, P132).
- Talk test: "I am running a marathon today and I feel good": 10 words in
  one breath, 10 breathy, about 5, then 2-3.
- Shorter, quicker steps when tired; one K or one lamppost at a time.

## What we've already told them (don't contradict, don't repeat as new)

  (sugar in, liquid, slow 15-20 s/K for 10 minutes).
- Tangents: the course is measured on the shortest line; weaving adds
  distance.
- Water stations: skip the first table, never stop dead, gel with water.
- Gel alternatives (24 Sep): jelly sweets ~5g each, drink mix, chews, flat
  cola, a bar. (Bars are now ruled out in-race, P134.)

- Carb load schedule; 7 days; 24 hours; the night before.
- 3:45 (mat gap: the pacer crossed the mat before you); 3:30 (portaloo

- Sunday 6 Dec 2026, 8:15. Starts at Plaça de la Marató; finishes on "the
  spectacular blue carpet, floating over the water at the City of Arts and
  Sciences" (valenciaciudaddelrunning.com, official route page).
- Flat: altitude 1.8m to 18m, maximum difference 16.2m (map profile). The
  official site calls it "the world's fastest and flattest circuit".
- Route unchanged from previous editions (runnea.com).
- 27K to 31K: a loop of tight, twisting streets round the old town (Torres de
  Serranos, Plaça de la Reina on the map).
- 31K to 34K: the course runs away from the finish, out west to the Bioparc /
  Parc de Capçalera at 34K. From 34K it heads back towards the finish (35K and
  36K south, then 37K, 38K east, 39K Estació del Nord, 40K Porta de la Mar).
- Aid stations: 5.5, 10, 14.5, 18.7, 23, 28.3, 33.1, 37.1, 40.4K and the
  finish: water in 33cl bottles, Aquarius in cups; fruit from 23K.
- Gels handed out (Enervit, with and without caffeine): 16.2K, 25.8K, 34K.
- Not confirmed: spectator numbers on any stretch; any climb at 30-34K (the
  2025 blog's "gentle rise" isn't visible on a 16m profile). Don't use either.
