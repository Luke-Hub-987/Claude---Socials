# The OST Structure

## The shape

```
LINE 1   [KEYWORD]  +  [PROMISE or TENSION]
LINE 2   ([WHISPER: objection killed, or the concrete payoff])
```

Two lines maximum. Roughly 6 to 10 words on line 1, 4 to 7 in the
whisper. One font. No function words (and, so, the, is). Break the
line by structure, not by where the box runs out. Hold it on screen
about 10 seconds, not the native 2-second cut.

That is the whole shape. Everything below is how you fill it.

---

## The fill order (this is the part that was wrong)

Never start at the keyword. Start at the end of the video and work
backwards.

**1. Dream outcome.** Write what the viewer walks away with, in words
they would use about their own life. "I want to stop blowing up at
30k." If you cannot write this line, stop. There is no video yet.

**2. Consequence of the pain.** Not the pain, what the pain is costing
them in real behaviour. Not "shin splints" but "the three weeks you
lose every time you ramp up." Not "bald" but "wearing a hat all the
time."

**3. Keyword.** One nameable thing, pulled from the ICP's real
vocabulary, never invented. Match the depth to the TAM you are aiming
at:

| TAM | Vocabulary depth | Running examples |
|---|---|---|
| Broad | Surface words everyone knows | long run, race day, the wall |
| Core | Iceberg words the ICP argues about | junk miles, threshold, negative split, easy-pace creep |

If it is a named thing you did, name the **event**, not a concept.
"The Paperback Potato Experiment," not "Cadence Lock."

**4. Gap check.** The viewer must already half-know the word, so the
hole is small. Small gap, high curiosity. Large gap, no curiosity, no
click.

**5. Whisper.** Find the single biggest reason they scroll past, and
bury it in the parentheses. Or put the concrete payoff there. It must
be a real number, outcome or scenario. Never a hype word, never a
mechanism name, never a feeling, never you.

**6. Shape.** Pick from the list below by account. Do not default to
whichever device worked last.

**7. Test.** Run the seven gates.

## The shapes that work, by account

Both lists are from real ranked view counts, not theory
(`ost-anatomy.md` Parts 5 and 6).

### Personal (`@luke_morrison_er`)

1. **How-to + viewer gain.** "How to actually use strides (To get you
   a PB)" 2,266.
2. **Scenario-question.** A real fork the viewer will face. "Q: If
   it's hot on race day, should I pour water over myself?" 2,505.
3. **Self-recognition.** A thought they have had and never said. "Are
   they trail running... or just walking really fast?" 2,578.
4. **Vicarious experience.** Promises a feeling or a place, not a
   lesson. "What's it actually like to Climb Benbulben" **148,000**.

**Shape 4 is the biggest thing on the account by a factor of about 60
and there was not one of it in the last ten OSTs.** Pillar 3 in
`pillars.md` is confirmed, Luke wants it regularly, and he gave the
angle himself: show the climb, not the summit. "People want to see
themselves going up," not arriving.

### ERN (`@engineroomnutrition`)

Every how-to on that grid sits 439-645 views. Every narrative with
stakes sits 1,271-2,158. So:

1. **Jeopardy test.** Something that can fail, on camera. "the bar
   that worked in a food lab was about to meet 1,200 metres of
   climbing" 1,725.
2. **Underdog narrative.** "Nobody expected us 2 to be next" 2,158.
3. **Boring process revealed.** The unglamorous thing nobody explains.
   Rice Crisp Graveyard.

**No how-tos on ERN.** And the sharpest unused asset in the whole
brand file: ERN's real problem is **the 30 minutes before a race**,
where the only option is a gel people tolerate rather than enjoy. That
is a counter-position, not a product spec, and no OST has used it yet.

## The seven gates

An OST ships only if all seven pass.

1. **Small gap.** They already half-know the keyword.
2. **Nameable thing.** A noun they can hold: a test, a rule, an
   experiment, a number, a place.
3. **Points, never delivers.** The answer is not in the text. "What
   fixed watering schedules are doing to your garden" ships; "fixed
   watering schedules are killing your garden" is a B+.
4. **The payoff is theirs.** The whisper names their gain, stakes,
   objection or path. Not a mechanism, method, feeling or you.
5. **Glance and picture.** Processable in well under a second, and it
   leaves an image. "Ketchup," not "my front yard."
6. **TAM chosen.** You know whether this is a broad or a core video,
   and the vocabulary matches.
7. **Sayable.** Someone could repeat it to a friend after hearing it
   once.

**And the gate above the gates:** if you cannot write the OST and
sketch the frame, you do not have an idea. Kill it there, not after
filming.

## Worked examples

Built live with the structure, to show it running. Sources are real
(`research/reddit-running-research-manual.txt`, `voice.md`,
`pillars.md`).

### A. Core, personal, how-to + gain

- Dream outcome: "I want to know my easy runs are actually doing
  something."
- Consequence of pain: doing 40 miles a week and improving at nothing.
- Keyword: **junk miles** (iceberg, real, from the research).
- Gap: small. They know the term and suspect they are guilty.
- Whisper: kills "I don't want to change my whole plan."

> **Junk miles or base building**
> *(one question tells you, no plan changes)*

### B. Broad, personal, vicarious experience — Pillar 3, the missing one

- Dream outcome: "I want to feel what that's like."
- Keyword: **Benbulben** (a real named place, surface vocabulary,
  broad TAM, and it already did 148,000).
- Shape: vicarious. No lesson promised, the feeling is the payoff.
- Whisper: Luke's own angle, the going up rather than the arriving.

> **The last 200m of Benbulben**
> *(the bit nobody films)*

### C. Personal, self-recognition

- Consequence of the pain, not the pain: waiting for the car park to
  empty before starting your watch.
- Keyword: none needed. Self-recognition shapes carry themselves.

> **Waiting for the car park to empty**
> *(before you start your watch)*

### D. ERN, jeopardy

- Counter-position: the 30 minutes before a race, where a gel is the
  only option and nobody likes it.
- Shape: a test that can fail.

> **30 minutes before the gun**
> *(bar vs gel, no second chances)*

## What to do when it will not come

Per Paddy: cap it at an hour or two, never a week. Returns on the OST
are non-linear, so the hour is worth it, but the other variable is
time passing and unposted videos teach you nothing.

If the OST still will not come after an hour, that is the gate doing
its job. The idea was not there. Take the next one.
