# The device library

## What the 2,156 video actually did

Any replacement has to do all five, or it is a different video with
worse numbers.

1. **A complete, scored set.** An open loop that only closes at the end.
2. **A stated price.** "Uncomfortable" signals the contents are not
   guessable, which is the ingredient P54 says creates a cost to
   scrolling.
3. **A possessable unit.** Habits, not reasons.
4. **An identity they already want.** Never get injured.
5. **Save-worthiness.** 25 saves against 34 likes and 0 comments. It
   worked as reference material, and that is the signature to design
   for.

## The twelve devices

### 1. The tier list — S to F
*Fires: set, comparison, argument.* Categorisation is a cognitive
shortcut and a score invites disagreement in a way a list never does.
> **Every runner snack, S to F, by what survives race effort**
> **Every excuse for skipping a long run, S to F**

### 2. The ladder — levels, not items
*Fires: set, identity, self-scoring, progression.* The viewer places
themselves and immediately wants the next level.
> **The 5 levels of fuelling a long run. Most runners never leave level 2.** ⚑
> **The 4 levels of easy running. Level 1 is where injuries live.** ⚑

### 3. The rulebook — commandments nobody wrote down
*Fires: set, price, possessable unit, saveable.* Rules are the most
saved unit there is.
> **The 6 rules of eating on the run that nobody writes down**
> **The 5 rules of race week, written by people who got it wrong**

### 4. The glossary — words that decide an outcome
*Fires: information gap at its sharpest. They have seen these words a
hundred times and never looked them up.*
> **The 4 words on a gel packet that decide whether it stays down** ⚑
> **3 words on a shoe box that tell you it is not for you** ⚑

### 5. The archetype cast — people at a scene
*Fires: self-reference hard, shares rather than saves.*
> **The 5 people in every start line toilet queue**
> **The 4 runners at every parkrun, and which one you are**

### 6. The stages — a sequence everyone recognises
*Fires: open loop, self-reference, comedy.* The b-roll is the video.
> **The 5 stages of trying to eat while running**
> **The 6 stages of a long run going wrong**

### 7. The bracket — a knockout
*Fires: set, jeopardy, an ending worth waiting for.*
> **16 runner snacks, one winner, decided at race effort**

### 8. The autopsy — a named failure taken apart
*Fires: gap, jeopardy, and series numbering, which is the strongest
signal in the 82-title dataset.*
> **He fuelled perfectly for 20 miles and lost the last 6** *(Race Report Autopsy, Ep 1)*

### 9. The inventory — everything in the bag, itemised
*Fires: possessable units, no arithmetic, and it is inherently visual.*
> **Everything in a race vest, and what each thing is really for**
> **Everything banned from my race vest** ⚑

### 10. The graveyard — the failures, kept
*Fires: price, honesty, series. Already ours.*
> **The graveyard of every bar that failed before this one** ⚑

### 11. The survey — real answers from real people
*Fires: authority, gap, and it is a genuine vehicle we can build
cheaply with a story poll.*
> **I asked 200 runners what they eat before a race. The answers are grim.** ⚑

### 12. The one-question filter — a single fork
*Fires: fluency, self-scoring, extremely low friction.*
> **One question that tells you if you will blow up at 30k** ⚑

## Which device to use for which outcome

The last video's numbers were **25 saves, 34 likes, 0 comments**. That
is a reference-material signature, and the device chooses which
signature you get.

| Want | Use |
|---|---|
| **Saves** (reference material) | rulebook, glossary, inventory, tier list, one-question filter |
| **Shares** (recognition) | archetype cast, stages, graveyard |
| **Comments** (argument) | tier list, bracket, ladder, survey |
| **Watch time** (a loop that only closes at the end) | bracket, autopsy, countdown, stages |

The zero-comments result on 2,156 views is still unexplained, and the
comment-driving devices are the cheapest way to test whether the format
or the CTA was the cause.

---

## The rotation

Never run the same device twice in a row. A working order for the next
six: **tier list → rulebook → stages → autopsy → ladder → glossary.**
That alternates saveable and shareable, and keeps the open-loop
mechanism live without the wrapper becoming familiar.
