# The account split

## The short answer

**Do not split the advice videos. Add a second kind of video that only
goes on the personal account.**

| | ERN account | Luke's account |
|---|---|---|
| **The job** | **What**: the useful thing | **Who**: the person and the build |
| **Content** | Marathon training, fuelling and race day advice. Everything that has worked so far | Luke's own Dublin build, race week, race day, building the bar, opinions |
| **How it is posted on Instagram** | Collab, posted from ERN, Luke as collaborator. As now | **Solo, personal only** |
| **TikTok** | The advice videos | **Personal-only videos. Never the same file** |

## Why the advice videos stay as collabs on Instagram

- **A collab is one post, not two.** It shows on both grids, the views
  are counted once, and the feed shows it once. **There is no
  duplicate-content problem on Instagram.**
- **The collab put every video in front of both follower bases** (P76),
  and it is the setup the 68K video was posted under. Changing it now
  would change the package that just worked (P95).

## Why the personal account still needs its own videos

1. **Right now the personal grid is an ERN grid.** Anyone who taps
   Luke's name sees a brand account with his face on it, and nothing
   that says who he is.
2. **People follow people.** A useful video gets saved. A person gets
   followed. The 68K video got 1.3K saves, and the follows are still
   unconfirmed. The personal account is where the reason to follow
   *him* lives.
3. **Fixed niche, never repeating (P72, Dr Becky).** Each account
   should have one clear promise. ERN's is the advice. Luke's is the
   story nobody else can tell: a man training for Dublin while building
   a carb bar with his co-founder after ten years of tax returns.
4. **It is the founder-led brand model.** The brand account teaches,
   the founder's account builds the trust that makes people buy from
   the brand.

## What goes on the personal account

All of it real, dated where the date is the point, and filmed on a
phone with no production.

| Series | What it is | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **The Dublin build** | His own training, week by week, to 25 October | Real context (P70), a deadline everyone can watch arrive |
| **Race week and race day** | His own race, including the result, whatever it is | The biggest personal content moment of the year |
| **Building the bar** | Failed batches, the factory day, the first real bar | The founder story. The pinned post already exists |
| **Opinions** | One contested claim he believes (P94, the Thiel test) | Comments, and the reason people follow a person |

**The Thiel rule still applies.** Personal does not mean generic. Every
personal video needs something only Luke knows or believes.

**Cadence:** two a week, on top of the advice videos. More than that
dilutes the thing that is working.

## TikTok, and this one matters

**Never upload the same file to both TikTok accounts.** TikTok has no
real collab post, and the same video on two accounts risks being
treated as duplicate or unoriginal content, which can hold back both.

- **ERN TikTok:** the advice videos.
- **Luke's TikTok:** personal-only videos, or nothing until there is a
  personal series to post.

## One thing to check first

**Where the follows actually land.** Open the 68K video's insights from
the ERN account as well as the personal one and compare follower counts
against last week. If the follows are landing on Luke, that is the
strongest possible argument for building the personal series now.
