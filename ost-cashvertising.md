# Cashvertising, applied

## 1. Caples' four qualities, and the one we keep missing

A good headline may possess: **self-interest, news, curiosity, and the
quick easy way.**

Audit of the current seven OSTs against all four:

| OST | Self-interest | News | Curiosity | Quick/easy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| €200 carbon shoes | yes | **no** | yes | yes |
| 3 levels of an easy run | yes | **no** | yes | partial |
| 10k time hasn't moved | yes | **no** | yes | **no** |
| What people eat at 5am | partial | partial | yes | **no** |
| Finishing versus racing | yes | **no** | yes | **no** |
| Why gels make you sick | yes | **no** | yes | **no** |
| Last 30 minutes | yes | **no** | yes | **no** |

**News is missing from every single one, and the quick easy way from
five of seven.** That is the finding.

**News, for us, honestly:** a real test we just ran, a real thing we
found in the research, a seasonal peg (race season), or a genuinely new
batch. Never invented novelty (P39).

**Quick easy way, for us:** "costs €25", "one number", "three fixes,
all free", "fix it on your next run", "takes a minute to check".

## 2. The 22 starters, filtered to the ones that fit the voice

Usable: **HOW · WHY · THESE · HERE'S · IF YOU · DO YOU · CAN YOU ·
WHICH OF · WARNING · AT LAST · FINALLY · NEW · ANNOUNCING · STARTING
TODAY**

Do not use: AMAZING, LOOK, FREE as a shout, PRESENTING, INTRODUCING.
They read as advertising, and both accounts trade on not sounding like
advertising.

Luke's own "Q:" prefix (2,505 views) is the DO YOU / CAN YOU family
already working in his voice.

## 3. Specificity: the hardware store and the pizza

Store one: "Hammers, screwdrivers, power tools, at neighborhood-friendly
prices." Store two: "343 kinds of fasteners, 28 types of nails, 86
gauges of wire, 43 grit sizes of sandpaper, a daily inventory of
354,000 bolts and screws."

Whitman's point, and Luke's margin note: **nobody needs to know how
many bolts you stock. The length of the specifics implies strength.**
Because hardly anyone else says these things, the one who does is
judged to be better.

The pizza version is the same move applied to ingredients. Not "better
ingredients" but buffalo milk mozzarella rather than cow, placed in
chunks rather than shredded because shredding releases moisture, San
Marzano tomatoes crushed in-house, hand-stretched dough, coal-fired
oven.

### What this means for ERN, concretely

**Never describe the bar as a category. Describe it as a spec.** This
is exactly Luke's "clean label brands like RxBar" note: RxBar built a
brand by printing the ingredient list on the front of the packet.

- Weak: "a high carb bar for endurance."
- Strong: "30 grams of carbs. Rice crisp, honey, sea salt. ⚑[N] failed
  batches before this one. Built to be eaten standing still, thirty
  minutes before a race, not halfway round one."

Every number we can honestly state is worth stating: grams, ingredient
count, batch number, months in development, metres of climbing tested
on. The count is the credibility.

## 4. The 12 ways into the body copy

Whitman's list of ways to move from headline to first line without
jarring the reader. The ones that map onto a verbal hook: continue the
thought in the headline, ask a question, quote a respected authority,
give a free taste. Our hook stack (P71) already does "continue the
thought" by default. **"Give a free taste"** is the one we do not use
and it is the closest thing to a lead magnet in the book, which is
exactly what the free race week guide is.

# The upgraded OSTs

Each adds the missing quality without losing the search terms.

**1. Carbon shoes**
> **If you are about to buy carbon shoes, buy these 4 things first**
> *(all under €100)*
IF YOU starter, quick easy way in the whisper.

**2. Easy runs**
> **How slow your easy runs actually need to be**
> *(3 levels, and you can fix it on your next run)*
HOW starter, quick easy way added.

**3. Threshold**
> **Why your 10k time has not moved since spring**
> *(one number is wrong)*
Quick easy way added, and "one number" is the smallest possible fix.

**4. Race morning**
> **These 5 breakfasts come up in every marathon race report**
> *(and the ones that come back up)*
THESE starter, plus the hardware-store move: a counted, specific set,
with the study implied as news.

**5. Finishing versus racing**
> **60 grams an hour is a finishing plan, not a racing one**
> *(the 2 levels above it)*
Self-interest sharpened. ⚑ Check with Luke: the "X is A, not B" shape
sits close to the banned negative parallelism (P60), so the safer
version is **"60 grams an hour will get you round, and no further"**.

**6. Gels**
> **Q: Why do gels make you feel sick at 30k?**
> *(3 fixes, all free)*
Quick easy way added, query untouched for search.

**7. The last 30 minutes**
> **What to do in the last 30 minutes before a marathon**
> *(3 things, and the first one is doing nothing)*
Quick easy way plus a real curiosity spike. The strongest upgrade in
the batch.
